Glossary名詞表

Every term defined in the chapters, with its one-line definition and a link to the paragraph where it is introduced. 104 terms.各章定義的每個名詞，附一行定義與連到引入它的段落的連結。共 104 個名詞。

+Grid+Grid Ch. 2
The topology that links each satellite to its two intra-plane neighbors and the same-index satellite in each adjacent plane.
把每顆衛星連到同面的兩個鄰居，以及相鄰兩面中同一編號衛星的拓樸。
Acquisition擷取 Ch. 2
The search phase in which two terminals find each other's beams before data transfer. Current acquisition takes seconds to tens of seconds.
兩個終端在資料傳輸前搜尋並找到彼此光束的階段，目前需要數秒到數十秒。
Activation compression激活值壓縮 Ch. 12
Sparsifying, quantizing and entropy-coding the tensor that crosses a layer boundary between two devices.
對跨越兩個裝置之間層邊界的張量做稀疏化、量化與熵編碼。
all-reduce全歸約（all-reduce） Ch. 10
The collective in which every worker ends with the element-wise sum of all workers' vectors.
讓每個工作者最後都拿到所有工作者向量逐元素總和的集體通訊。
Aperture口徑 Ch. 2
The diameter of the telescope that transmits or collects a beam. Gain scales with the square of this diameter.
發射或收集光束的望遠鏡直徑，增益與口徑的平方成正比。
BBRBBR Ch. 6
A model-based controller that sets the sending rate from estimated bottleneck bandwidth and minimum RTT.
以模型為基礎的控制器，依估計的瓶頸頻寬與最小 RTT 決定傳送速率。
Beam波束 Ch. 3
A steerable radio spot formed by a satellite's array. The array forms many beams at once.
衛星陣列形成的可轉向電波光斑，一顆衛星可同時形成多個波束。
Bent pipe彎管轉送 Ch. 3
A path through one satellite: terminal → satellite → gateway within the same footprint. The path uses ground links for both hops.
終端 → 衛星 → 同一覆蓋區內閘道的路徑，兩跳皆為地面與單一衛星之間的鏈路。
Bisection capacity對分容量 Ch. 5
The summed link capacity across the cut that splits a network into two halves.
把網路切成兩半的割集上所有鏈路容量之和。
bisection intensity對切強度 Ch. 10
Floating-point operations per step divided by bytes that must cross the bisection per step.
每一步的浮點運算量，除以每一步必須穿越對切面的位元組數。
Bufferbloat緩衝區膨脹 Ch. 3
Latency inflation caused by a large standing queue, 2–4× the idle RTT on a loaded Starlink link.
由大型常駐佇列造成的延遲膨脹，在負載下的 Starlink 鏈路上達到閒置 RTT 的 2–4 倍。
Cell服務格 Ch. 3
The ground area whose users are served together by a beam, roughly 24 km across in Starlink.
由同一波束一起服務的地面區域，在 Starlink 中直徑約 24 km。
Clos networkClos 網路 Ch. 10
A layered switch fabric in which compute nodes attach to leaf switches and every leaf reaches every other through spine switches.
一種分層交換架構：運算節點接到葉交換器，每個葉交換器都透過脊交換器到達其他所有葉交換器。
Cluster fabric叢集網路架構 Ch. 11
The network inside a formation cluster: terminals per satellite, switching and the topology that links them.
編隊叢集內部的網路：每顆衛星的終端數、交換方式，以及連接它們的拓樸。
Congestion control壅塞控制 Ch. 6
The sender-side algorithm that sets how much data may be in flight from the signals the network returns.
傳送端根據網路回傳的訊號決定可以有多少資料在途中的演算法。
Congestion window (cwnd)壅塞視窗（cwnd） Ch. 6
The sender's limit on unacknowledged bytes, so that throughput is at most cwnd divided by the RTT.
傳送端允許在途並等待確認的位元組數上限，吞吐量最多為 cwnd 除以 RTT。
Consistent hashing一致性雜湊 Ch. 9
A hash maps each object to one of N buckets, and each bucket maps to one cache. Requests reach the cache holding the object's single copy.
雜湊函數將每個物件分配到 N 個桶之一，每個桶再對應一台快取。這組快取只儲存每個物件的一份副本，並將請求送往持有該物件的快取。
Content delivery network (CDN)內容遞送網路（CDN） Ch. 9
Caches near users that serve popular objects locally, reducing requests to the origin server.
部署在使用者附近的一組快取，從近處提供熱門內容，減少向來源伺服器擷取資料。
CUBICCUBIC Ch. 6
The loss-driven Linux default that cuts the window on loss and regrows it along a cubic curve.
以遺失為訊號的 Linux 預設演算法，遺失時縮小視窗，再沿三次曲線回升。
Decode解碼（decode） Ch. 12
The second phase, generating one output token per step, each step depending on the last.
第二階段，每一步產生一個輸出 token，每一步依賴前一步。
Depth of discharge放電深度（DoD） Ch. 8
The fraction of a battery's capacity drawn each cycle, traded against cycle life.
每個循環取用的電池容量比例，與循環壽命互為取捨。
Digital twin數位孿生 Ch. 7
An emulation whose constellation, ground stations and link states are built from real data such as filings and TLEs.
由申報文件與 TLE 等真實資料建構星座、地面站與鏈路狀態的模擬平台。
Direct-to-cell (D2C)直連手機（D2C） Ch. 3
Service from a satellite to an unmodified mobile phone in a terrestrial cellular band.
衛星使用地面行動通訊頻段，直接服務維持原廠配置的手機。
Divergence發散角 Ch. 2
The full angle over which a beam spreads, about 1.22λ/D for an aperture D.
光束擴散的全角，對口徑 D 約為 1.22λ/D。
Doppler shift多普勒頻移 Ch. 2
The optical frequency offset vr/λ caused by range rate. At 1,550 nm, each km/s produces a 0.645 GHz shift.
距離變化率造成的光頻偏移 vr/λ，在波長 1,550 nm 時，每 km/s 對應 0.645 GHz。
Downlink bottleneck下行瓶頸 Ch. 8
The limit set by pass minutes times station rate on how much a satellite can send to the ground per orbit.
由過頂分鐘數乘以地面站速率決定的每圈可下傳資料量上限。
Drop-front queue前端丟棄佇列 Ch. 3
A queue that discards its oldest packet when full and admits the arriving packet.
滿載時丟棄最舊封包，並接納新到封包的佇列。
Duty cycle工作週期 Ch. 8
The fraction of time a payload runs when heat or energy forces it to pause.
當散熱或能量迫使酬載暫停時，酬載實際運行的時間比例。
DWDMDWDM Ch. 2
Dense wavelength-division multiplexing: many independent channels on one beam, spaced 100 GHz or less apart.
高密度分波多工：在同一道光束上放多個獨立通道，間距 100 GHz 或更小。
Eclipse地影 Ch. 1
The part of each orbit spent in Earth's shadow.
每一圈軌道中位於地球陰影內的部分。
Elevation angle仰角 Ch. 1
The satellite's angle above the user's local horizon. The elevation mask sets the minimum permitted angle.
衛星高於使用者當地地平線的角度，仰角遮罩設定營運商允許的最低值。
Emulator模擬平台（emulator） Ch. 7
A testbed that runs standard operating systems and applications in real time. A model sets each link's delay and rate.
以實際時鐘執行原版作業系統與應用程式，並由模型設定鏈路延遲與速率的測試平台。
evidence tag證據標籤 Ch. 4
A label attached to a number stating what kind of document it comes from and therefore how a reader can check it.
附在數字上的標記，說明它來自哪一類文件，因此讀者能用什麼方式查證。
Expert placement專家配置 Ch. 12
The assignment of experts to satellites so that the hidden state travels the shortest expected path.
把專家指派到衛星上，讓隱藏狀態走的期望路徑最短。
exposed communication暴露的通訊 Ch. 10
Communication that extends a step because it occurs outside the time spent on computation.
發生在運算時段之外、因此直接延長步驟耗時的通訊。
Federated learning聯邦學習 Ch. 9
Training in which participants update a shared model using local data. They exchange only model updates with an aggregator, keeping training data local.
每個參與者使用本地資料訓練共享模型，只向彙總者傳送模型更新，訓練資料留在本地。
filing snapshot申報快照 Ch. 4
The constellation geometry stated in one dated regulatory document, which later filings replace and which the papers of its year freeze.
某一份附日期監管文件中陳述的星座幾何，之後的申報會取代它，而當年的論文則把它凍結下來。
Formation flying編隊飛行 Ch. 11
Several satellites controlled so that their relative positions follow a prescribed, repeating geometry.
多顆衛星受控制，使其相對位置遵循預先設定、週期重複的幾何。
Gateway閘道站 Ch. 3
A ground station of several parabolic dishes that carries the satellite's traffic to and from the terrestrial network.
由數個拋物面天線組成的地面站，把衛星的流量送往地面網路並接回。
Geographic routing地理路由 Ch. 5
Routing that uses identifiers for Earth-fixed cells as its address reference.
以地球固定格元的代碼作為位址依據的路由方式。
Ground relay地面中繼 Ch. 5
A ground station that receives from one satellite and retransmits to another, replacing an ISL hop.
從一顆衛星接收再轉發給另一顆衛星的地面站，用來取代一跳星間鏈路。
Handover換手 Ch. 3
The moment a terminal switches from one serving satellite to another.
終端從一顆服務衛星切換到另一顆的時刻。
HCW equationsHCW 方程 Ch. 11
The Hill–Clohessy–Wiltshire linearised equations of relative motion about a circular reference orbit.
Hill–Clohessy–Wiltshire 方程，描述繞圓形參考軌道的相對運動的線性化方程。
Hop count跳數 Ch. 5
The number of inter-satellite links a packet crosses, used as a proxy for capacity consumption.
封包穿越的星間鏈路數，是所消耗容量的代理指標。
ICI晶片間互連（ICI） Ch. 10
The inter-chip interconnect that directly links accelerators inside one host or pod, bypassing network interface cards.
繞過網路卡，直接連接同一主機或同一 pod 內加速器的晶片間互連。
Inclination傾角 Ch. 1
The angle between the orbital plane and the equator, which bounds the satellite's latitude range.
軌道面相對赤道的傾斜角，決定衛星能到達的最高緯度。
Inter-satellite link (ISL)星間鏈路（ISL） Ch. 2
A direct radio or laser link between satellites that carries traffic across the constellation entirely in space.
兩顆衛星之間的直接電波或雷射鏈路，讓流量全程在太空中穿越星座。
J₂J₂ 項 Ch. 11
The oblateness term of Earth's gravity field, the dominant perturbation that drifts relative orbits apart.
地球重力場的扁率項，是使相對軌道漂移分離的主要攝動。
KV cacheKV 快取 Ch. 12
The keys and values that each attention layer stores per token. Decode reuses them to avoid repeated prompt computation.
每個注意力層按 token 儲存的 key 與 value，解碼時可重用提示的計算結果。
Link budget鏈路預算 Ch. 2
The accounting, in decibels, of transmit power, antenna gains and losses that yields received power.
以分貝記帳發射功率、天線增益與各項損耗，最後得到接收功率的計算。
Low Earth orbit (LEO)低地球軌道（LEO） Ch. 1
Orbits below about 2,000 km altitude, where broadband constellations fly.
高度約 2,000 km 以下的軌道，寬頻星座都在這裡飛行。
Mixture of experts (MoE)混合專家（MoE） Ch. 12
A transformer whose feed-forward block is split into experts of which a router activates only a few per token.
前饋區塊被切成多個專家、每個 token 只由路由器啟用其中少數幾個的 transformer。
MotifMotif Ch. 2
A local link pattern repeated identically by every satellite, searched over as an alternative to +Grid.
每顆衛星都以相同方式重複的局部連線樣式，作為 +Grid 之外的搜尋對象。
Near field近場 Ch. 2
Distances below an aperture's Fresnel distance, where the beam remains concentrated and calculations require a near-field model.
距離小於口徑的 Fresnel 距離、光束仍集中的範圍，計算時需採近場模型。
Nodal precession升交點進動 Ch. 1
Slow drift of the ascending node caused by Earth's oblateness (J₂).
地球扁率（J₂）造成的升交點緩慢漂移。
Node delay節點延遲 Ch. 2
The time between a packet's arrival and departure on separate satellite links. The Starlink value awaits measurement.
封包從一條鏈路進入衛星，至另一條鏈路離開的耗時，Starlink 的數值仍待量測。
OFDM frameOFDM 幀 Ch. 3
The repeating 1.333 ms unit of the Starlink downlink: sync symbols, header, then data symbols over 1,024 subcarriers.
Starlink 下行每 1.333 ms 重複一次的單位：同步符號、標頭，然後是分佈在 1,024 個子載波上的資料符號。
On-board inference機上推論 Ch. 9
Inference on a satellite's processor, with only the results sent over the downlink and raw data kept on board.
在衛星處理器上執行訓練好的模型，只將結果下傳，原始資料留在衛星上。
Orbital data center軌道資料中心 Ch. 13
A formation of compute satellites joined by short-range optical links into one cluster, with its own ground segment and user path.
一群以短距光鏈路連成單一叢集的運算衛星，連同自己的地面段與使用者路徑。
Orbital edge computing軌道邊緣運算（OEC） Ch. 8
Inference on a satellite, with only the results sent to the ground and raw sensor data kept on board.
在衛星上執行推論，只將結果下傳，原始感測資料留在衛星上。
Orbital period軌道週期 Ch. 1
Time for one revolution, fixed by altitude alone for a circular orbit.
繞行一圈所需的時間，圓軌道時只由高度決定。
Orbital plane軌道面 Ch. 1
The plane containing one orbit, located by its inclination and ascending node.
包含一條軌道的平面，由傾角與升交點定位。
oversubscription超額訂閱 Ch. 10
The ratio of capacity entering a network tier to capacity leaving it upward.
進入某一層網路的容量，與該層向上送出容量之間的比值。
Packet simulator封包模擬器 Ch. 7
Software that advances a model network event by event in simulated time, with every protocol and link written as a model.
在模擬時間中逐一事件推進模型網路的軟體，每一個協定與鏈路都是寫出來的模型。
parameter sheet參數表 Ch. 4
The single dated table of every constant an evaluation rests on, each with its value, provenance and date.
一份附日期的表，列出評估所依賴的每個常數及其數值、出處與日期。
Pass過頂 Ch. 1
The interval during which one satellite stays above the elevation mask.
一顆衛星停留在仰角遮罩之上的時間區間。
Path emulator路徑模擬平台 Ch. 6
A testbed that replays one pre-computed satellite path, with its delay, capacity and handover schedule, under a real operating-system network stack.
在真實作業系統網路堆疊下，重現一條預先算好的衛星路徑及其延遲、容量與換手排程的測試平台。
Phase offset相位偏移 Ch. 1
The along-track shift between satellites in neighboring planes, 360·f/t degrees.
相鄰軌道面衛星之間沿軌方向的錯位，等於 360·f/t 度。
Phased array相位陣列 Ch. 3
A flat antenna whose elements steer a beam electronically through their relative phases. The dish stays mechanically fixed.
由許多元件組成的平板天線，藉由相對相位以電子方式轉向波束，碟盤維持固定。
Point of presence (PoP)網路接入點（PoP） Ch. 3
The point where an operator's network passes traffic to the public Internet. Every Starlink flow reaches a PoP through a tunnel.
業者網路將流量交給公共網際網路的地點，每條 Starlink 流量都經隧道抵達某個 PoP。
Point-ahead angle預指向角 Ch. 2
The offset 2vt/c between where a partner is seen and where the beam must be sent, because light takes time to arrive.
因光需要時間抵達，光束必須送往的方向與看到對象的方向之間相差的角度 2vt/c。
Pointing, acquisition and tracking (PAT)指向、擷取與追蹤（PAT） Ch. 2
The control problem of aiming a microradian beam at a partner thousands of kilometres away and holding it there.
把微弧度級的光束對準數千公里外的對象並持續鎖住的控制問題。
Prefill預填（prefill） Ch. 12
The first phase of serving a request, in which every prompt token passes through the model at once.
服務一個請求的第一階段，提示的所有 token 一次通過模型。
Propagation delay傳播延遲 Ch. 2
Distance divided by light speed in the medium. In vacuum, propagation takes 3.336 µs per kilometer.
距離除以介質中的光速，在真空中每公里需要 3.336 µs。
Radiator輻射散熱板 Ch. 8
The surface that sheds a satellite's waste heat as infrared, at a rate set by its temperature to the fourth power.
把衛星廢熱以紅外線輻射出去的表面，速率由溫度的四次方決定。
Rain fade雨衰 Ch. 3
Extra attenuation of a Ku- or Ka-band link while rain lies on the path, seen in Starlink as a 17–27% throughput drop.
當路徑上有雨時 Ku 或 Ka 頻段鏈路的額外衰減，在 Starlink 上表現為 17–27% 的吞吐量下降。
Reconfiguration interval重新配置週期 Ch. 3
The 15-second period on which Starlink's global scheduler reassigns satellites and resources, at seconds :12, :27, :42 and :57.
Starlink 全域排程器重新指派衛星與資源的 15 秒週期，落在每分鐘的第 12、27、42、57 秒。
Reconfiguration-aware congestion control感知重新配置的壅塞控制 Ch. 6
A controller that learns, predicts or infers the satellite network's scheduled changes and discounts the loss and delay signals they produce.
能得知、預測或推斷衛星網路排程變動，並對其產生的遺失與延遲訊號打折扣的控制器。
Relative orbit相對軌道 Ch. 11
The closed path a satellite traces in the rotating frame attached to a reference satellite.
衛星在附著於參考衛星的旋轉座標系中所描出的封閉路徑。
Research question研究問題 Ch. 13
A question stated with the measurement or simulation that could show it false and the baseline it must beat.
同時列出檢驗假設所需量測或模擬，以及必須超越之基準的問題。
Seam接縫 Ch. 1
Where the first and last planes of a Star pattern meet moving in opposite directions.
Star 型星座中第一與最後一個軌道面相遇、彼此反向運動的地方。
SGP4SGP4 Ch. 7
The standard propagator that turns a TLE into a position and velocity at any later time.
把 TLE 轉換成之後任意時刻位置與速度的標準軌道推算器。
Shell殼層 Ch. 1
A group of planes sharing one altitude and inclination, filed and operated as a unit.
共享同一高度與傾角的一組軌道面，在申請與營運上視為一個單位。
Silent data corruption (SDC)靜默資料損毀（SDC） Ch. 8
An incorrect computational result that the system accepts silently.
系統靜默接受的錯誤計算結果。
Single-event effect (SEE)單粒子效應（SEE） Ch. 8
A fault caused by one particle, from a flipped bit to a destructive latch-up.
單一粒子造成的故障，從位元翻轉到破壞性閂鎖都算。
Single-event functional interrupt (SEFI)單粒子功能中斷（SEFI） Ch. 8
A single-event fault that halts a device until it is reset or power-cycled.
使裝置停擺、直到重置或斷電重啟才恢復的單粒子故障。
Slant range斜距 Ch. 1
Straight-line distance from user to satellite, which sets the propagation delay.
使用者到衛星的直線距離，決定傳播延遲。
Spatial multiplexing空間多工 Ch. 11
Splitting one aperture into an array of smaller sub-apertures, each carrying an independent beam, at the price of range.
將一個口徑分割為多個較小子口徑的陣列，每個子口徑承載獨立光束，代價是縮短距離。
Specific power比功率 Ch. 8
Watts of array output per kilogram of array mass.
每公斤陣列質量所產生的瓦數。
Staleness陳舊度 Ch. 9
The number of global rounds that have passed between a participant downloading a model and its update being aggregated.
參與者下載模型到它的更新被彙總之間，已經過去的全局回合數。
step time步時間 Ch. 10
The elapsed time for one training step: computation plus communication that extends beyond it.
一個訓練步驟的實際耗時，等於運算時間加上延伸至運算時段之外的通訊時間。
Stretch伸長比 Ch. 5
Path latency or length divided by the great-circle value between the same endpoints.
路徑延遲或長度除以同一對端點之間的大圓值。
Sun-synchronous orbit (SSO)太陽同步軌道（SSO） Ch. 1
A retrograde orbit whose nodal precession matches the Sun's apparent motion.
升交點進動速率與太陽視運動相同的逆行軌道。
Switch satellite交換衛星 Ch. 11
A satellite assigned to switching in a Clos network, which occupies a position otherwise available to a compute satellite.
在 Clos 網路中專責交換的衛星，會占用原本可配置運算節點的位置。
Temporary ISL暫時性星間鏈路 Ch. 2
A link to a satellite that is only briefly within range, such as one in a crossing plane.
與只短暫進入範圍的衛星（例如交叉軌道面上的衛星）之間的鏈路。
Time per token每 token 時間 Ch. 12
The wall-clock time one decode step takes for one request stream, the inference analogue of step time.
單一請求串流中一個解碼步驟所需的時鐘時間，相當於訓練中的步驟時間。
