How satellite networks work, and whether AI can run on them衛星網路的運作方式與在軌 AI 的可行性

This primer introduces orbital mechanics, optics and networking. Part I explains the physics using numbers from peer-reviewed papers and public filings. Part II covers the research by topic. Part III examines training and inference across satellites, using Google's Suncatcher formation as an example. It ends with a network research plan for orbital data centers.這份教材從軌道力學、光學與網路的基礎講起。第一部介紹物理原理，數字取自經同儕審查的論文與公開申報文件。第二部依主題介紹研究文獻。第三部以 Google 的 Suncatcher 編隊為例，討論跨衛星訓練與推論的需求，最後提出軌道資料中心的網路研究計畫。
