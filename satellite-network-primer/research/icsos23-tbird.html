Recent On-Orbit Results and ARQ Performance Analysis for the TBIRD 200-Gbps MissionTBIRD 200 Gbps 任務的近期在軌結果與 ARQ 效能分析

ICSOS 2023 / NASA NTRSSchieler et al. (MIT Lincoln Laboratory, NASA JPL) · paper論文 peer-reviewed

TBIRD demonstrates a high-rate optical downlink using commercial telecom transceivers. Selective-repeat ARQ recovers data lost during atmospheric fades, while oscillator pre-compensation keeps Doppler within the receiver window. Its ground telescope and downlink geometry define the scope of this flight result.TBIRD 使用商用電信收發器示範高速光學下行，選擇性重傳 ARQ 復原大氣衰落造成的資料遺失，本地振盪器預補償則讓多普勒偏移維持在接收窗口內。地面望遠鏡與下行幾何共同界定這項飛行結果的適用範圍。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

Every other digest in this group reasons about laser links that are assumed to exist. This one reports a laser link that flew. It studies optical link performance through elevation angle, received power and oscillator offset. Networking papers instead tend to study hop count and topology.

For the primer it does three jobs. The NTRS copy carries an "In Review" marking. The primer records that marking and treats the ICSOS conference paper as peer-reviewed. The study covers one satellite, one terminal and one ground station.

Constellation snapshots, node-delay models and setup-delay models lie outside its scope. Each pass does begin with an acquisition, but the fact base leaves TBIRD acquisition time unspecified.

這篇報告實際在軌運作的雷射鏈路，為本組以假設鏈路為主的導讀提供實證。它屬於光學文獻，分析仰角、接收功率與振盪器偏移，網路文獻則多分析跳數與拓樸。對本教材而言它做了三件事。這是一篇會議論文，其 NTRS 版本帶有「審查中」標記，所以本教材基於 ICSOS 的會議身分把它視為同儕審查，同時註明該標記。

星座版本位於這項單衛星研究的範圍之外，因為只有一顆衛星、一個終端、一個地面站，因此節點延遲假設與建立延遲模型亦位於範圍之外。每次過頂確實從一次擷取開始，但事實庫將 TBIRD 擷取時間保留為待補。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

TBIRD addresses the Earth-observation downlink bottleneck described in Chapter 8. A small satellite can generate more data than a radio pass returns. The conventional larger antenna exceeds the available volume of a CubeSat. The mission's measurement is therefore the volume returned per pass and the conditions under which the link holds. The third is the receiver's tolerance to Doppler, measured the hard way.TBIRD 處理的問題是第 8 章描述的地球觀測下行之牆：一顆小衛星產生的資料遠多於一次無線電過頂能送回的量，而加大無線電天線的傳統答案體積超出立方衛星的可用空間。所以這個任務的量測是每次過頂送回的資料量，以及鏈路能維持的條件。第三個是接收器對多普勒的容忍度，從實際鏈路失敗中量得。

3. The design3. 設計

The design idea is to buy the modem and build only what space requires. Turbulence causes millisecond fades in the optical downlink. A fiber telecom modem treats each fade as a burst of lost frames. TBIRD therefore adds selective-repeat automatic repeat request.

On the ground, a 1 m telescope with adaptive optics collects the beam and couples it into single-mode fiber. This permits a commercial receiver. Space-to-space links require a separate receive design. A coherent receiver must lock its local oscillator to the incoming carrier.

The telecom DSP acquires signals over only tens of ppm, requiring Doppler compensation. Thus, the oscillator is pre-shifted from the predicted range rate before each pass.

設計理念是買現成的調變解調器，只打造太空所必需的部分。穿越湍流的光學下行會一次衰落數毫秒，而為光纖設計的電信調變解調器把每次衰落當成一串遺失的幀，所以 TBIRD 把資料流包進選擇性重傳自動重複請求裡。地面端是設計的另一半，也是星對星鏈路須另行設計的那一半：一座帶自適應光學的 1 m 望遠鏡收集光束並耦合進單模光纖，讓同樣的商用接收器可以在地面使用。多普勒修正補齊全貌：相干接收器必須把本地振盪器鎖到入射載波，而電信 DSP 只能在數十 ppm 內拉入，所以每次過頂前依預測的距離變化率預先偏移振盪器。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The evaluation is an on-orbit measurement campaign rather than a simulation. This makes it the most trustworthy source in this group and also the narrowest. The link is a downlink. Thus, the receive aperture is a metre across and sits under an atmosphere that the ARQ is designed to defeat. The study has one satellite, with filing snapshots, node delay and routing outside its scope.

There is one optical terminal rather than three or four. Each pass is a scheduled contact. The reviewed material leaves acquisition time unspecified, so setup delay lies outside the model. The paper leaves the space terminal's transmit power and aperture diameter unspecified.

An earlier SPIE paper gives these parameters and remains outside this source review. Thus, the link budget requires additional source inputs.

評估採用在軌量測活動，這使它成為本組最可信的來源，同時也是最窄的。鏈路是下行，所以接收孔徑有一公尺寬，而且位於 ARQ 所要克服的大氣之下。研究只有一顆衛星，申報版本、節點延遲及路由皆位於範圍之外。只有一個光學終端，其他星座的三或四終端配置則屬另一情境。

建立延遲位於模型範圍之外，因為每次過頂是排定的接觸，而在事實庫驗證的資料中論文將擷取時間保留為待補。論文也將太空終端發射功率與孔徑直徑保留為待補，這些存在於一篇本次來源範圍之外的較早 SPIE 論文中，所以單靠這份文件須加入額外輸入才能閉合鏈路預算。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The result describes an optical downlink. The 1 m receive telescope has a hundred times the collecting area of a 10 cm space terminal. This is 20 dB of the budget. Its adaptive optics do work that a space receiver treats through a separate optical design. The 200 Gbps therefore demonstrates the transceivers and the pointing, with a 2,000–5,000 km satellite-to-satellite link requiring a separate budget. The primer uses TBIRD as a receiver-sensitivity reference. SpaceX claims 100 Gbps sustained and 200 Gbps peak per laser. Journalists relay these company statements (fact base Part C, item 14 company).此結果描述光學下行鏈路。1 m 的接收望遠鏡有 10 cm 太空終端一百倍的集光面積，相當於預算中的 20 dB，而它的自適應光學做的工作太空接收器須以另一套光學設計處理。因此 200 Gbps 示範的是收發器與指向，2,000–5,000 km 的星對星鏈路則須另行計算，本教材把它當作靈敏度錨點，SpaceX 每個雷射 100 Gbps 持續、200 Gbps 峰值宣稱的證據則仍限於透過記者轉述的公司說法（事實庫 Part C 第 14 項 company）。
  • The link budget requires additional source inputs. Transmit power and space-aperture diameter are unspecified in the ICSOS text the fact base verified. Thus, the −41 dBm threshold is the only end of the budget available here. Chapter 2 closes the loop with a worked 1 W, 10 cm example that is the primer's computation, with TBIRD's transmitter parameters treated separately.閉合鏈路預算需要其他來源的參數。發射功率與太空孔徑直徑仍待從事實庫驗證的 ICSOS 文字以外查證，所以 −41 dBm 的門檻是此處唯一可用的預算端。第 2 章用 1 W、10 cm 的範例閉合迴路，那是本教材的計算，TBIRD 的發射參數則須另行核對。
  • Headline versus average. The 4.8 TB record was one pass after Doppler compensation. The campaign average was about 1.5 TB and the pass-average rate about 128 Gbps [computed]. A budget of 4.8 TB per pass uses the best case. A continuous 200 Gbps budget uses a rate achieved only above 30° elevation.標題數字對平均數字。4.8 TB 的紀錄是多普勒補償後的一次過頂。活動平均約 1.5 TB、過頂平均速率約 128 Gbps［計算值］，所以以每次過頂 4.8 TB 預算的前端設計用的是最佳情況，以持續 200 Gbps 預算的設計用的是只在 30° 仰角以上才成立的速率。
  • The Doppler window is a warning for crossing-plane ISLs. The −17 to +21 ppm tolerance equals about 3–4 GHz at 1,550 nm. Counter-moving LEO satellites approaching at 15 km/s produce about 9.7 GHz or 33 ppm [computed]. Coherent COTS-based ISLs in that geometry therefore require ephemeris-based oscillator pre-compensation, as TBIRD used. This is one more reason the temporary links of the companion digests are hard. Same-direction adjacent-plane links in a 53° shell see only about 0.17 GHz [computed in Chapter 2].多普勒窗口是對交叉軌道面 ISL 的警告。−17 到 +21 ppm 的容忍度在 1,550 nm 約為 3–4 GHz，而兩顆以 15 km/s 接近的 LEO 衛星產生約 9.7 GHz 或 33 ppm［計算值］，所以任何反向運行衛星之間以 COTS 為基礎的相干 ISL 都必須像 TBIRD 一樣依星曆預補償振盪器，這是姊妹導讀中暫時鏈路困難的又一個理由。53° 軌道殼中同向相鄰軌道面鏈路只看到約 0.17 GHz［第 2 章計算］。
  • ARQ needs a return path and a buffer whose size requires another source. Selective repeat requires ground feedback within the pass. Feedback latency and buffer depth require additional source records. For a store-and-forward data return this is immaterial. For the interactive traffic of Chapters 5–6 it would set the retransmission RTT.ARQ 需要一條回程與一個容量須另查來源的緩衝區。選擇性重傳預設地面能在過頂內告訴衛星哪些幀失敗，而回饋通道延遲與緩衝深度都須另查來源。對儲存轉送式的資料回傳這些參數對總資料量影響較小。對第 5–6 章的互動式流量它會決定重傳 RTT。
  • One site, one satellite, a reviewed copy. All passes used the OCTL telescope, so weather and site statistics are absent. The NTRS copy carries an "In Review" marking. The venue is a refereed conference, but readers citing exact figures should check the final ICSOS proceedings version.一個站、一顆衛星、一份審查中的版本。所有過頂都用 OCTL 望遠鏡，天氣與站點統計須由多站量測補充，而 NTRS 版本帶有「審查中」標記。會議本身有審查，但引用精確數字的讀者應核對 ICSOS 會議論文集的最終版本。
