LLMSpace: Carbon Footprint Modeling for Large Language Model Inference on LEO SatellitesLLMSpace：LEO 衛星上大型語言模型推論的碳足跡建模

arXiv 2605.05615Jiang, Ildefonso, Loveless, Chen (Indiana University) · paper論文 preprint

LLMSpace calculates inference-satellite carbon from component masses, launch intensity and hardware lifetime. Solar power makes operational carbon small within the model, while manufacture and launch dominate. Mass, link assumptions and radiation lifetime determine how readers should interpret the comparison.LLMSpace 從元件質量、發射碳強度及硬體壽命計算推論衛星的碳排放。模型中的太陽能使營運碳相對較小，製造與發射則占主要部分。比較結果取決於質量、鏈路及輻射壽命的假設。

LLMSpace is the first carbon model for language-model inference on a LEO satellite, built bottom-up from components rather than measured [97, §4–5] preprint. The main caveat for this primer is that the network appears only as mass and carbon. Thus, the paper leaves request deadlines outside its evaluated scope.LLMSpace 是第一個針對 LEO 衛星上語言模型推論的碳模型，由元件由下而上建構，量測則仍待完成[97, §4–5]預印本。對本教材而言主要的限制是：網路只以質量與碳的形式出現，所以論文將從軌道服務的請求期限列於評估範圍之外。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

Chapter 12 treats SpaceMoE, CollabLLM-LEO and LLMSpace as the published orbital-inference core. LLMSpace focuses on lifecycle carbon. SpaceMoE and CollabLLM-LEO study distributed inference latency. LLMSpace compares lifecycle carbon for an inference satellite with the same compute on a terrestrial grid. This is the environmental half of the argument for orbital data centers that Chapter 11 introduces through Suncatcher's power-cost comparison.第 12 章稱 SpaceMoE、CollabLLM-LEO 與 LLMSpace 為在軌推論文獻已發表的核心，而 LLMSpace 問的問題與另外兩篇各異。SpaceMoE 與 CollabLLM-LEO 問的是推論分散到多顆衛星時要花多久，LLMSpace 問的則是一顆推論衛星在生命週期中要付出多少碳，並比較其碳排放與地面電網執行相同運算的差距。這是軌道資料中心論證中環境面的那一半，而第 11 章透過 Suncatcher 的電力成本比較引入了經濟面。它也是這組論文中假設酬載最大的一篇，H100 等級的系統只飛過一次，就是 Starcloud-1，屬於公司宣布的展示、公開遙測資料仍待補充。它假設的 ISL 也最強，是最高 200 Gbps 的終端。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Environmental-benefit claims require explicit carbon accounting. Solar electricity addresses operational emissions, while satellite manufacture and launch add embodied emissions. The embodied carbon covers manufacturing and launch, while operational carbon covers electricity use. The authors model both from component inputs. [97, §4] preprint問題在於，關於在軌運算環境效益的說法需要明確的碳盤查，而最直覺的一句話論證，「陽光使用階段的排放為零」，漏掉了建造與發射衛星的碳。因此作者把隱含碳（embodied carbon），也就是製造與發射硬體所產生的排放，與營運碳，也就是硬體所用電力的排放，區分開來，兩者都由元件建模[97, §4]預印本。

Model configurationEach subsystem adds manufacturing carbon and mass-dependent launch carbon. Falcon 9 supplies 3.3 × 10⁵ kgCO2e for 22,800 kg, giving 14.5 kgCO2e per kilogram. [97, Tab. 6]
Orbital periodThe subsystems are the solar array, the battery, the cooling panel, the computing hardware, and the network and satellite bus. The orbit has a 90–100 min period, 55–65 min sunlight and 30–35 min eclipse. It repeats 15–16 times daily. Batteries cover eclipse at 80% depth of discharge. [97, §4.1]
Model configurationThat orbit model is where the physics of Chapter 8 enters. A 10 kW payload needs 5.0–5.8 kWh through eclipse. At 80% depth of discharge, battery capacity is about 6.3–7.3 kWh. The rad-hard variant doubles power and both energy requirements. Thus, the rad-hard satellite carries almost twice the embodied carbon (calc table).

星座配置子系統包括太陽能陣列、電池、散熱板、運算硬體，以及網路與衛星平台，它們的大小由軌道決定：週期 90–100 分鐘，日照 55–65 分鐘、日蝕 30–35 分鐘，每天 15–16 圈，電池容量要能以 80% 放電深度撐過日蝕[97, §4.1]。

The network enters in the same currency. Latency, topology and traffic models lie outside the link description. This is the gap a networking reader notices first.網路也以同樣的單位進入模型。CubeSat 的 RF 地面鏈路則為 kbit/s 到數百 Mbit/s[97, §4.1]。這些鏈路將延遲、拓樸與流量列於模型範圍之外，這是網路讀者最先注意到的缺口。採用多接面陣列與抗輻射電池的「rad-opt」設計，則比抗輻射版本減少 7% 的隱含碳[97, §6.1]。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The bottom-up analytical model is checked against a reference assembled from prior work. Measured lifecycle validation, traffic simulation, testbeds and flight hardware lie outside its scope. The "up to 200 Gbps" rate affects only terminal mass, power and manufacturing carbon. [97, §5] Latency, topology and failures lie outside the model. Thus, the number of terminals per satellite and their pointing remain outside the result calculations.

Period and eclipse fraction represent the orbit. They size batteries and arrays, with propagated constellation geometry and time-varying load outside the model. preprint

評估是一個由下而上的解析模型，驗證的對象是作者根據先前文獻建立的參考值，實測生命週期資料、流量模擬、測試平台與飛行硬體則位於評估範圍之外[97, §5]預印本。ISL 速率寫為「最高 200 Gbps」，但它只透過終端的質量、功率與製造碳進入模型，延遲位於模型範圍之外、拓樸及故障模型皆位於範圍之外，所以每顆衛星的終端數與其指向皆位於結果的計算範圍之外。軌道以週期與日蝕比例表示，星座傳播動態則位於模型範圍之外，這固定了電池與陣列的大小，卻遺漏了任何隨時間變化的負載。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • Carbon with request timing outside its scope. The links appear as mass and carbon only, with latency, topology and failure outside the model, so request deadlines lie outside its evaluation. The ground comparison uses grid carbon per kilowatt-hour. An equivalent serving design with matched tokens, users and latency requires separate evaluation.聚焦於碳，請求時序則位於範圍之外。鏈路只以質量與碳出現，延遲、拓樸及故障模型皆位於範圍之外，所以請求期限位於論文評估範圍之外。它與地面電網的比較是每千瓦時碳的比較，若要與地面端服務方案比較，須另行確保使用者、token 交付量及延遲條件相同。
  • Energy modeled as an average orbit, with orbital dynamics outside its scope. The period and eclipse fraction size the battery, but load is constant and thermal behavior lies outside the model. The model excludes measured duty cycling. Xing et al. use a 5–10 W board, while the Suncatcher prototype reportedly operates for 15–20 minutes, a company statement. Chapter 13 asks how joint energy and orbital dynamics affect operation.能源以平均軌道建模，軌道動態則位於模型範圍之外。週期與日蝕比例決定了電池大小，但負載是常數、熱行為位於模型範圍之外，而 Xing 等人在 5–10 W 板子上量到的工作週期，以及據報 Suncatcher 原型以 15–20 分鐘運作的工作週期 公司 皆位於範圍之外，所以能源與軌道動態的聯合建模仍待完成，而這正是第 13 章提出的聯合問題。
  • Unreconciled mass and overheads. A 130 kg payload on a 191 kg bus leaves 61 kg. That remainder must cover 10–20 kW arrays and batteries plus 28 kg terminals. Chip-area overhead is 15× in §5, 2× in the §4.1 RAD750 example, and 10×/20× in §6. Power overhead is 2× versus 1.5×/3× (fact base Part C, item 40). Only the embodied totals are safe to quote.仍待釐清的質量與額外負擔。130 kg 的酬載放在 191 kg 的平台上，只剩 61 kg 給其他所有東西，包括 10–20 kW 所需的陣列與電池，以及每具 28 kg 的終端。抗輻射晶片面積額外負擔在 §5 為 15 倍、在 §4.1 的 RAD750 例子為 2 倍、在 §6 為 10/20 倍，功率額外負擔則為 2 倍對 1.5/3 倍（事實庫 Part C，第 40 項）。只有隱含碳總量可以安全引用。
  • Lifetime assumptions drive the answer. The assumed two-year COTS life raises annual carbon above the rad-hard case. Suncatcher tests find HBM irregularities only from about 2 krad. One chip shows zero hard TID failures up to 10 krad(Si), or 15 krad(Si) in arXiv v2. The five-year requirement is 750 rad. COTS life beyond two years could halve annual carbon or improve it further. [108, §2.3] [36]壽命假設主導答案。COTS 衛星受輻射限制的兩年壽命讓它每年的成本比抗輻射版本高，然而 Suncatcher 的質子測試發現 HBM 要到約 2 krad 才出現異常、單顆晶片直到 10 krad(Si) 觀測到的硬性 TID 失效為零（arXiv v2 預印本寫 15 krad(Si)[108, §2.3]），而五年的需求是 750 rad[36]，這意味著 COTS 加速器可能活超過兩年，屆時每年的碳會減半甚至更少。
  • Inputs awaiting primary-source evidence. The "up to 200 Gbps at ~1 W" terminal is attributed to Starlink V2, while SpaceX keeps laser rate proprietary. The H100 has one company-announced flight demonstration, with public telemetry pending. The 191 kg bus mass comes from a secondary citation. Thus, the network and payload inputs are assumptions rather than measurements.仍待一手來源的輸入。「在約 1 W 下最高 200 Gbps」的終端被歸於 Starlink V2，但 SpaceX 從仍待公布其雷射速率。酬載所代表的 H100 只飛過一次，屬於公司宣布的展示、公開遙測仍待補充。191 kg 的平台質量則來自二手引用，所以網路與酬載的輸入都是模型假設，量測則須另行確認。
