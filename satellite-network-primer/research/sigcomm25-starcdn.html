StarCDN: Moving Content Delivery Networks to SpaceStarCDN：把內容遞送網路搬上太空

ACM SIGCOMM 2025William X. Zheng, Aryan Taneja, Maleeha Masood, Deepak Vasisht (UIUC), Anirudh Sabnis (Akamai), Ramesh K. Sitaraman (UMass Amherst & Akamai) · paper論文 peer-reviewed

StarCDN coordinates caches as satellites move between user regions. Examine its bucket placement and relayed fetch, with results scoped to the proxy topology and replay model.StarCDN 協調持續移動的衛星快取。導讀說明桶配置與接力擷取，並依代理拓樸及重播模型界定結果的適用範圍。

StarCDN uses satellite-specific consistent hashing and relayed fetch to keep popular content near changing user groups. Buckets divide objects across neighboring satellites, and a cache miss first checks a nearby satellite holding the same bucket [62, §3.2–3.3]. Akamai trace replay in CosmicBeats improves hit rate and reduces ground uplink use relative to per-satellite LRU [62, §5.1–5.2, Tab. 1, Figs. 7–8]. The model adds ISLs to a Gen-1 proxy topology and derives latency from earlier idle measurements. Separate source counts and hit-rate figures require location-specific citation.

StarCDN 以衛星專屬的一致性雜湊與接力擷取，讓熱門內容接近持續變換的使用者群。物件分桶配置於相鄰衛星，快取失誤時先詢問持有同桶的鄰居[62, §3.2–3.3]。CosmicBeats 上的 Akamai 軌跡重播，相對逐衛星 LRU 提高命中率並減少地面上行使用[62, §5.1–5.2, Tab. 1, Figs. 7–8]。模型在 Gen-1 代理拓樸上加入 ISL，延遲則取自較早的閒置量測。各處衛星數與命中率有差異，引用時需附具體位置。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

StarCDN follows Bose et al.'s SpaceCDN proposal [89] with a placement policy, production CDN traces and an uplink-use evaluation. The independent team includes UIUC, UMass Amherst and Akamai, whose participation provides production logs. Chapter 9 treats it as a caching system with measurable hit rates. Its bucket placement depends on the regular four-neighbor +Grid used as a modeling baseline in Chapter 5.

StarCDN 接續 Bose 等人的 SpaceCDN 提案[89]，補上放置策略、實際營運 CDN 軌跡與上行使用評估。獨立團隊包含 UIUC、UMass Amherst 及 Akamai，後者的參與提供了營運紀錄。第 9 章將它視為具有可量測命中率的快取系統，其桶配置依賴第 5 章作為建模基準的規則四鄰居 +Grid。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Requests through a terrestrial CDN repeatedly cross the ground-to-satellite uplink, adding access delay [62, §1]. A local LRU cache follows its satellite into new regions, so its learned popularity soon becomes stale [62, §1, §3.1]. Satellites covering the same ground also duplicate popular objects. Coordinating these caches can turn their separate storage into a larger shared service [62, §3.1.2].

透過地面 CDN 取得內容時，請求反覆跨越地面至衛星上行，並增加存取延遲[62, §1]。本地 LRU 快取隨衛星進入新區域，原先學到的熱門度很快過時[62, §1, §3.1]。共同覆蓋地面的衛星也會重複儲存熱門物件，協調這些快取可將分散儲存整合為較大的共享服務[62, §3.1.2]。

3. The design3. 設計

Consistent hashing maps objects to N buckets and tiles them across the satellite grid, so adjacent caches hold complementary objects. Requests reach the owning satellite through bounded ISL forwarding [62, §3.2]. On a miss, relayed fetch asks the closest inter-orbit neighbor holding the same bucket before falling back to the ground [62, §3.3]. That neighbor recently served nearby ground, so content can pass between satellites and remain near its users as the constellation moves. Inter-orbit links provide the shorter relay path in this geometry, and proactive prefetching proved less efficient [62, §3.3]. Transient failures trigger ground fetches, while persistent failures remap buckets to another satellite [62, §3.4].

一致性雜湊將物件映射至 N 個桶，再鋪排於衛星網格，使相鄰快取儲存互補的物件，請求以有限跳數轉送至持有者[62, §3.2]。快取失誤時，接力擷取先詢問持有同桶的最近跨軌道鄰居，再視結果回到地面[62, §3.3]。該鄰居剛服務過附近區域，因此內容能在衛星間傳遞，隨星座移動仍留在使用者附近。跨軌道鏈路在此幾何中較短，主動預取的評估效率則較低[62, §3.3]。暫時故障觸發地面擷取，持續故障則將桶重新配置至其他衛星[62, §3.4]。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The evaluation replays traces with CosmicBeats orbital motion and link scheduling, using a step matched to observed reassignment [62, §5.1]. Gen-1 TLEs provide a topology proxy because Gen-2 data was unavailable, and the model adds laser links to that proxy [62, §5.1, Tab. 1]. Link rates and average propagation delays are configured inputs [62, Tab. 1]. Instantaneous setup and discrete steps keep transfers active across changes that could interrupt a real connection.

Sampled production video traces cover several Akamai clusters, with proportional cache scaling [62, §5.1]. SpaceGEN extends duration and workload types, with measured agreement to production LRU hit rates [62, §4.3, §5.5, Fig. 6]. Baselines include local LRU, hashing-disabled and relay-disabled ablations, and a fixed-position Static Cache bound.

評估使用 CosmicBeats 的軌道運動與鏈路排程重播軌跡，步長配合觀察到的重新指派週期[62, §5.1]。Gen-2 資料取得受限，因此採 Gen-1 TLE 作為拓樸代理，並在模型中加入雷射鏈路[62, §5.1, Tab. 1]。鏈路速率與平均傳播延遲皆為設定輸入[62, Tab. 1]。即時建立及離散步長，讓傳輸在實際可能中斷的連線變化期間繼續。主要工作負載是多個 Akamai 叢集的取樣營運影音軌跡，快取也按比例縮放[62, §5.1]。SpaceGEN 延長時間並加入其他工作負載，並以營運 LRU 命中率檢驗一致程度[62, §4.3, §5.5, Fig. 6]。基準包含本地 LRU、停用雜湊及停用接力的消融版本，以及固定位置的 Static Cache 上界。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待研究的問題

  • Bucket placement depends on a proxy laser graph. The model equips a Gen-1 satellite set with four links per node. Starlink states three laser terminals and keeps its actual topology confidential. The hop bound and bucket tiling therefore require derivation on the deployed graph (fact base Part C, items 13 and 55).桶配置依賴代理雷射圖。模型為 Gen-1 衛星集合配置每節點四條鏈路，Starlink 則聲明三個雷射終端，實際拓樸仍屬保密資訊。跳數界限與桶鋪排因此需在部署圖上重新推導（事實庫 Part C 第 13、55 項）。
  • Source values need precise operating points. The setup and failure sections give separate satellite counts, and the abstract and result figure give separate hit rates. The tables preserve each value and source location. This digest uses the main evaluation count and Figure 7a's stated operating point (Part C, item 42).來源數值需附具體操作點。設定與故障章節的衛星數各異，摘要與結果圖的命中率也各有數值。表格保留各值及位置，本導讀採主要評估的衛星數，以及 Fig. 7a 標明的操作點（Part C 第 42 項）。
  • The simulation step sets time resolution. It matches the observed reassignment interval, while transfers remain active across mid-step changes and links appear instantly. Evaluating handover failures and real acquisition time requires an explicit extension (Part C, items 18, 56 and 57).模擬步長設定時間解析度。步長符合觀察到的重新指派間隔，傳輸在步長中途的變化仍持續，鏈路也即時可用。評估換手失敗與真實擷取時間，需要明確擴充模型（Part C 第 18、56、57 項）。
  • Latency combines prior idle measurements and propagation. The comparison assumes zero per-hop node delay. Queuing and loaded bufferbloat require a separate evaluation, such as the conditions measured by SpaceCDN (Part C, item 19).延遲結合先前閒置量測與傳播。比較採每跳零節點延遲，排隊及負載下的緩衝膨脹則需另外評估，例如 SpaceCDN 量測所涵蓋的條件（Part C 第 19 項）。
  • Broader traces and deployment models remain useful. The production trace has limited duration and sampling, while SpaceGEN lacks cross-location temporal correlation. DNS assignment, TLS keys and constellation–CDN integration remain future work, alongside flash-memory power and mass.後續需擴大軌跡與部署模型。營運軌跡的時間與取樣範圍有限，SpaceGEN 也缺少跨地點時間相關性。DNS 指派、TLS 金鑰、星座與 CDN 整合，以及快閃記憶體的電力和質量，均留待後續研究。
