Network Topology Design at 27,000 km/hour時速 27,000 公里下的網路拓樸設計

ACM CoNEXT 2019Debopam Bhattacherjee, Ankit Singla (ETH Zürich) · paper論文 · project page專案頁面 peer-reviewed

A motif repeats a local link pattern between co-moving satellites, preserving its links through an orbit. The study scores patterns by stretch and hops. Longer shortcuts improve this objective, while shorter range limits the gain. The results provide a dated design baseline.motif 在同向運動的衛星間重複局部連線規則，使鏈路維持整圈軌道。研究以拉伸比與跳數評分，較長的捷徑改善目標值，縮短射程則限制增益。結果提供一套附日期的設計基準。

Bhattacherjee and Singla optimize four laser links per satellite in a constellation moving at 27,000 km/h. They seek short distances and few hops. A motif assigns two same-direction neighbors to every satellite through an identical local rule. Repeating it preserves link feasibility throughout the orbit. Each laser retains its acquired partner. [1, §4.1–4.2]Bhattacherjee 與 Singla 問的是，一個以時速 27,000 公里運動的星座應如何為每顆衛星的四條雷射鏈路接線，使路徑在公里數與跳數上都短，而他們的答案是 motif，也就是每顆衛星向兩顆同向運動的衛星各接一條鏈路的局部模式，在整個星座中以相同方式重複，使每條鏈路在整個軌道週期內都保持可行，各具雷射皆能維持已擷取的對象[1, §4.1–4.2]。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

This is the first full-length paper on inter-satellite topology design and the quantitative baseline that every later wiring proposal measures itself against. Thus, Chapter 5 gives it the center of Section 5.2. The same group's HotNets 2018 agenda used +Grid held static for one minute. This paper develops that work before Hypatia adopts +Grid as its default.

The enduring result is the stable-topology argument. Links changing every few minutes incur setup times of tens of seconds. The paper therefore favors patterns stable by construction, alongside the 54% gain. Ali and Zhang (2026) find motifs depend on complete symmetric deployment.

TinyLEO instead uses a topology-recompiling controller that favors long-lived links. Both retain the emphasis on link stability.

這是第一篇關於星間拓樸設計的完整論文，也是後來每個接線提案都以之為對照的量化基準，所以第 5 章把它放在第 5.2 節的中心。它直接承接同一群人 HotNets 2018 的議程論文，該論文假設 +Grid 並每次保持一分鐘靜態，而它又先於把 +Grid 作為預設寫進模擬器的 Hypatia。這篇論文持久的貢獻在於穩定拓樸的設計框架，54% 則是特定設定下的結果：它是「每隔幾分鐘就改變的鏈路建鏈成本偏高，因為雷射建鏈要花數十秒，所以拓樸應該在結構上本就穩定的模式中選擇」這一論證的標準參考。後來的研究繼承這個框架但對細節提出異議，Ali 與 Zhang 在 2026 年發現 motif 需要完整對稱部署，真實殼層則為部分部署，因為它假設完整且對稱的部署，而 TinyLEO 以一個在重新編譯拓樸時偏好長壽鏈路的控制器取代固定模式。

3. The design3. 設計

Every satellite applies the same two-link rule. Candidate partners stay within range and travel in the same direction. Thus, a motif is a three-satellite, two-link pattern whose repetition fully specifies the topology [1, §4.1, Fig. 6] . Each satellite initiates two links and receives two more, using the four ISLs in Starlink's revised filings. Partners move together, retaining links for the orbit. Relative velocities rise only at high latitudes, as with +Grid. [1, §3.1, fn. 3] [1, §4.2]motif 是每顆衛星都以相同方式套用的規則，從射程內且同向運動的衛星中選兩顆相連，所以一個 motif 就是一個「三顆衛星、兩條鏈路」的模式，把它重複到整個星座便完整決定了拓樸[1, §4.1, Fig. 6]。因為每顆衛星主動建立兩條鏈路，又從規則指向它的衛星接收兩條，所以每顆衛星用上 Starlink 修訂後申請文件所示的四條 ISL[1, §3.1, fn. 3]，而由於夥伴與它一起運動，鏈路在整個軌道週期內都保持，相對速度只在高緯度增大，與 +Grid 鏈路完全相同[1, §4.2]。+Grid 本身就是一個 motif，如圖 A 左側所繪。由於高緯度的衛星彼此較近，論文接著讓 motif 隨緯度變化，以寬度 W 的帶狀區從赤道向外逐區決定 motif。

Measure or comparison參數或比較項目Values, conditions and evidence數值、條件與證據
Motif searchThe +Grid is one motif, shown at left in Figure A. Search enumerates candidate neighbor pairs at the Equator, where separation is largest. This ensures orbital feasibility, and symmetry removes equivalent patterns. This leaves 1,029 for the 40×40 constellation, and scores each by shortest-path stretch and hop count under the population-product traffic matrix [1, §4.1, §4.3] .
Plane configurationThe center of Figure A shows links skipping planes and indices. Longer individual links reduce total path hops. The paper's Pareto plot shows that a motif giving up 2% of stretch cuts hops by 32% [1, §4.3, Fig. 7] .
Constellation configurationCloser high-latitude spacing permits latitude-dependent motifs. Zones extend outward from the Equator in bands of width W, with W = 18°. Three zones cover latitude up to 53°. Each satellite changes ISLs about every 12 minutes. The authors judge setup of a few seconds to small tens of seconds tolerable. Gains beyond a single motif saturate near 7%. [1, §5.3–5.4, Fig. 13]
ThroughputThe abstract's "nearly 2× the throughput" rests on hop count as the throughput proxy. This occurs because a connection crossing fewer ISLs consumes capacity on fewer links. A shortest-path congestion check supports the proxy. The +Grid 75th- and 90th-percentile link loads are 4× and 5× the Φ1-optimized multi-motif loads. [1, §2.4, §5.6, Fig. 15]
星座配置搜尋時列舉赤道上一顆衛星射程內的所有衛星對，因為赤道處衛星彼此最遠，所以每條候選鏈路在任何位置都可行，再刪去因對稱而等價的 motif，40×40 星座剩下 1,029 個，並以人口乘積流量矩陣下的最短路徑拉伸與跳數為每個 motif 計分[1, §4.1, §4.3]。
路徑長度與跳數圖 A 中間顯示搜尋會找到的那類替代方案，鏈路跳過軌道面與編號，使橫越星座的路徑需要較少跳數，代價是單條鏈路較長，而論文的 Pareto 圖顯示，犧牲 2% 拉伸的 motif 可以減少 32% 的跳數[1, §4.3, Fig. 7]。

吞吐量摘要中「近 2 倍的吞吐量」是以跳數作為吞吐量的代理，因為經過較少 ISL 的連線只佔用較少鏈路的容量，作者並以最短路徑路由下的壅塞檢驗支持這個代理，其中 +Grid 第 75 與第 90 百分位的鏈路負載各自是 Φ1 最佳化多 motif 的 4 倍與 5 倍[1, §2.4, §5.6, Fig. 15]。

ILP solutions one minute apart share 9% of links. Random graphs lose over 8% of feasible links within 2 minutes and 19% within 5 minutes. [1, §3.1–3.2]鏈路動態：相隔一分鐘算出的 ILP 拓樸只共用 9% 的鏈路，隨機圖的鏈路有超過 8%（19%）在 2（5）分鐘內變得超出可行條件[1, §3.1–3.2]。For the 20-city ILP and for random graphs, used as motivation. Single-motif topologies have zero churn by construction [1, §4.2].針對 20 個城市的 ILP 與隨機圖，作為動機。單一 motif 拓樸在結構上零變動[1, §4.2]。
A later preprint reuses motifs on a synthetic full four-ISL Starlink shell. Balanced motifs give 48.9 ms and 21.5 hops. +Grid gives 60.6 ms and 23.5 hops. [34, Tab. 2]被重用為基準：在四條 ISL 的合成完整 Starlink 殼層上，平衡 motif 給出 48.9 ms 與 21.5 跳，對比 +Grid 的 60.6 ms 與 23.5 跳[34, Tab. 2]preprint。That reimplementation uses 72×22, compared with the original 24×66. Its roughly 9% hop saving is below CoNEXT's 32–53% reductions on 40×40. [1, §4.3, §5.5] The same authors report that motifs require full deployment for direct application.在 72×22 佈局上，對照原研究的 24×66，且 motif 由預印本作者重新實作，其約 9% 的跳數節省遠低於 CoNEXT 在 40×40 上報告的 32–53% 跳數減少[1, §4.3, §5.5]。同一作者也報告 motif 直接套用時須滿足完整部署條件。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The ground segment and traffic are idealized. Each city has one unlimited-bandwidth station reaching any in-range satellite. Joint ground-station and ISL design remains future work. The traffic matrix is static, with time-varying traffic also deferred. Thus, the gains are those of the ISL graph alone [1, §3.1, §4.7, §7] .地面段與流量都被理想化。每個城市只有一座頻寬視為充足、可連到射程內任何衛星的地面站，地面站與 ISL 的聯合設計留待後續，流量矩陣也是靜態的，隨時間變化的流量同樣延後處理，所以這些增益只屬於 ISL 圖本身[1, §3.1, §4.7, §7]。
  • The constellation and terminal count are superseded. The August 2019 change from 24×66 to 72×22 increases intra-plane separation from 659 km to 1,969 km. This changes range-feasible motifs. The four terminals have become three with an undisclosed topology (fact base Part C, items 12, 13 and 55). Ali and Zhang found that a three-ISL grid is disconnected on real ephemerides. Thus, the motif search would have to be redone under the revised degree constraint.星座與終端數已被取代。24×66 殼層在 2019 年 8 月變為 72×22，面內間距從 659 公里變為 1,969 公里，也改變了哪些 motif 符合範圍限制，而四個終端已變成三個且拓樸仍待公開（事實庫 C 部第 12、13 與 55 項）。Ali 與 Zhang 發現三 ISL 網格在真實星曆上是分成多個連通分量的，所以 motif 搜尋必須在相異的度數限制下重做。
  • Full symmetric deployment is assumed. A motif rule presumes every slot in every plane is occupied. Ali and Zhang find motifs unsuitable for partially filled real Shell 1. Their stable-link designs on 90 days of TLEs achieve 52.9 ms and 8.2 hops. Adapted +Grid gives 89.0 ms and 23.4 hops. The motif result therefore bounds regular-wiring gains. preprint假設完整對稱的部署。motif 規則預設每個軌道面的每個槽位都有衛星。Ali 與 Zhang 報告這使 motif 需要完整部署，與部分填滿的真實 Shell 1 各有條件，而他們在 90 天 TLE 上的穩定鏈路設計達到 52.9 ms 與 8.2 跳，調整後的 +Grid 則為 89.0 ms 與 23.4 跳preprint，所以 motif 結果最好被解讀為規則接線所能達到的上界。
  • Setup delay motivates but is outside the model. The three-zone design behind the 54% headline changes satellite ISLs about every 12 minutes. The authors accept setup from a few seconds to small tens of seconds. Setup cost itself lies outside the metrics. [1, §5.4] Modern terminals require 2–30 s (Part C, item 18). Later work finds a noticeable latency increase from 100 ms setup per path change. Routing churn and the 15-second scheduler lie outside this model (Part C, items 56–57). [32, §IV-B]建鏈延遲是動機則位於成本模型之外。整個設計的存在就是為了避免重新對準，但 54% 這個主要數字背後的三區多 motif 會讓每顆衛星約每 12 分鐘更換一次 ISL，作者認為對數秒到十幾秒的建鏈時間而言可以容忍，其成本則位於指標範圍之外[1, §5.4]。現代終端需要 2–30 秒（C 部第 18 項），後續研究顯示每次路徑改變 100 ms 的建鏈成本會明顯提高平均延遲[32, §IV-B]，而路由層的變動或 15 秒排程器皆位於模型範圍之外（C 部第 56–57 項）。
  • Capacity is a hop-count proxy. The "nearly 2×" throughput is inferred from fewer ISL hops per connection rather than computed as a flow. The paper uses link rate outside the model, only noting that 5.6 Gbps laser ISLs over 4,900 km had already been demonstrated [1, §2.3–2.4]. Applying it to the measured network requires a separate evaluation, whose ISL paths are dominated by routing policy rather than topology (Part C, item 58).容量是以跳數代理。「近 2 倍」的吞吐量是從每條連線經過較少 ISL 跳數推得，其依據為拓樸代理指標，論文將鏈路速率保留為待定，只提到 4,900 公里、5.6 Gbps 的雷射 ISL 已經實現過[1, §2.3–2.4]。它套用到實測網路時須另行評估，後者的 ISL 路徑由路由政策主導，拓樸則須配合路由條件解讀（C 部第 58 項）。
  • Numbers to read with care. Percentages combine median M1 and traffic-weighted Φ1. They also mix a single motif, gaining about 44% on 40×40, with the Table 1 three-zone design. The 40×40 baseline is one snapshot. Minimum ranges come from +Grid connectivity, providing a worst case rather than terminal specifications. [1, §4.3–4.4, §6]需小心閱讀的數字。主要百分比混用了兩種指標，即城市對的中位 M1 與流量加權的 Φ1，也混用了兩種設計，即在 40×40 上約 44% 的單一 motif 與表 1 的三區多 motif，而 40×40 基準只是單一快照，最小鏈路範圍則是 +Grid 仍能連通的最短範圍，被當作最壞情況其依據為拓樸可行條件[1, §4.3–4.4, §6]。
