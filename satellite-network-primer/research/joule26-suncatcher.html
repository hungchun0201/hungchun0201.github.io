Main result
主要結果

Suncatcher places accelerators in a close solar-powered formation to support high-rate optical links. The paper quantifies physical subsystems while leaving the cluster network for further design.

Suncatcher 將加速器放在緊密的太陽能編隊，以支援高速光學鏈路。論文量化了物理子系統，叢集網路則留待後續設計。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Nearly continuous sunlight motivates orbital compute, which then needs high-bandwidth interconnects. Close formation reduces optical divergence enough to support multiple commercial transceiver channels.

近乎持續的日照提供在軌運算的動機，運算接著需要高頻寬互連，緊密編隊則降低光學發散，支援多個商用收發器通道。

3. The design3. 設計

The bounded HCW solution creates a rotating and stretching lattice. Shape tuning limits perturbation drift, while coherent optics and spatial multiplexing use the short link distances.

有界 HCW 解形成旋轉並拉伸的晶格，形狀微調限制攝動漂移，相干光學與空間多工則利用較短鏈路距離。

6. Limits and open questions6. 範圍與待解問題

  • The cluster network remains to be specified. The paper specifies per-link rate, Fig. 2’s 8-neighborhood and possible connections between clusters over distances beyond 100 km. Optical-terminal count, switching architecture, wider topology, hop count, latency and bisection bandwidth remain unspecified. Chapter 11 and the two follow-up preprints examine these open choices. Google’s Palomar optical circuit switch serves as an analogy for parallel spatial streams [36, p. 3].叢集網路仍待指定。論文只給了每條鏈路的速率、Fig. 2 的 8 鄰居，以及叢集可再以相距超過 100 km 的鏈路組成「叢集的叢集」的說法，其餘架構輸入待指定：每顆衛星的光學終端數待指定、交換架構待指定、8 鄰居之外的拓樸待指定、跳數待指定、延遲預算待指定，對分頻寬因此也待指定，這正是第 11 章與兩篇後續預印本存在的原因。Google 的 Palomar 光路交換器只是作為平行空間光束的類比出現，其定位是類比[36, p. 3]。
  • ICI capacity changes the comparison. The paper describes TPU inter-chip interconnect as hundreds of Gbps per chip [36, p. 3]. Google Cloud lists v6e capacity at 800 GB/s bidirectionally, or 6.4 Tbps per chip company. One aperture therefore carries roughly one or two chips’ ICI capacity. A satellite with dozens of TPU chips faces a fabric boundary similar to terrestrial designs that keep tensor parallelism within hosts.ICI 的比較被低估了。論文說 TPU 晶片間互連為「每晶片數百 Gbps」[36, p. 3]，而 Google Cloud 的 v6e 文件列出每晶片雙向 800 GB/s，即 6.4 Tbps company，所以一個孔徑大約只承載一到兩顆晶片的 ICI，而搭載「數十顆 TPU 晶片」的衛星會遇到與地面設計相同的層級邊界，後者的解法是把張量平行留在主機之內。
  • Link distances use best-case constants. The 5 km, 1.25 km, and 0.32 km ranges exclude beam-quality loss, aperture truncation, and pointing jitter [36, p. 8]. Flight hardware must account for these losses. Figure A shows a large distance margin at 100–200 m spacing, while rate-versus-range models must retain those assumptions.最佳情況下的鏈路常數。5 km 的近場長度與 1.25 km、0.32 km 的多工距離都是忽略光束品質、孔徑截斷與指向抖動的共焦最佳情況[36, p. 8]，所以飛行鏈路能達到的會略少一些。如圖 A 所示，100–200 m 的間距留有很大餘裕，但任何建立在這些數字上的速率對距離模型，都會承襲它們的樂觀。
  • ΔV and thermal evidence have distinct scopes. Joule budgets formation maintenance below 30 m/s per year. During solar storms, tilted drag-compensation thrust can supply much of the station-keeping ΔV [36, p. 5]. Propellant mass per satellite and collision-avoidance allowance need further specification. Supplemental notes sketch a control approach using backpropagation through ODEs. The HBM limit is near 90 °C, and radiator emission near 50 °C decreases about 1.2% per kelvin [36, pp. 2, 7]. Google’s September 2026 post reports thermal-vacuum validation of heat pipes and radiators, with orbital validation pending company.只有總量的 ΔV，也在軌熱資料待補充。Joule 內文把編隊維持的預算定為每年低於 30 m/s，並指出太陽風暴期間，大部分軌道維持 ΔV 可以靠調整補償阻力損失的推力方向來提供[36, p. 5]，但每顆衛星的推進劑質量待指定、避撞預留待指定，除了補充說明中概述的「穿過 ODE 反向傳播」方法之外飛行控制律亦待指定。熱的方面，論文把 HBM 的上限定在約 90 °C，並指出 50 °C 附近的輻射器每低 1 K，輻射量約減少 1.2%[36, pp. 2, 7]，而 Google 自己 2026 年 9 月的文章說熱管與輻射器已在熱真空中驗證，但「仍待在軌驗證」company。
  • The prototype leaves network testing for the next mission. The specifications widely quoted for the MVP, four Trillium TPUs, about 1 kW of solar and chips powering down every 15–20 minutes, are press-reported (Payload. The New York Times via the IEEE ComSoc blog) and awaiting confirmation in Google’s posts. The first inter-satellite link test is the two-satellite Planet mission planned for 2027, which Planet and Google announced but the paper itself leaves unspecified.原型的網路測試留待後續任務。廣為引用的 MVP 規格，四顆 Trillium TPU、約 1 kW 太陽能、晶片每 15–20 分鐘斷電散熱，都是媒體報導（Payload。經 IEEE ComSoc 部落格轉述的紐約時報），Google 文章的確認待補充，而第一次星間鏈路測試是計畫在 2027 年進行的 Planet 雙衛星任務，這是 Planet 與 Google 的公告內容，論文本身列於論文範圍之外。
  • Network assumptions require their dates and scope. Short-range Tbps links differ in operating range from Starlink’s claimed sustained ≈100 Gbps per laser company. The static-neighbor lattice leaves 2–30 s laser acquisition outside its model. Starlink’s 15-second access schedule would affect user traffic that reaches the formation through that communications constellation. Ground access remains an open design area, with radio for the pilot and optical links planned later. The paper cites NASA’s 200 Gbps TBIRD as the optical-downlink reference [36, pp. 2, 7].網路讀者應該標上日期的假設。短程 Tbps 鏈路與 Starlink 宣稱每具雷射約 100 Gbps 的持續速率 company 屬於相異等級。現代終端 2–30 秒的雷射擷取時間列於模型之外，因為晶格被當成一組固定的鄰居。Starlink 的 15 秒排程適用於編隊外的接入星座，卻會支配任何經通訊星座抵達叢集的使用者流量。而地面段仍是待解，試行用射頻，光學鏈路「最終」才需要、詳細設計仍待補充，引用的現況是 NASA 的 200 Gbps TBIRD[36, pp. 2, 7]。
