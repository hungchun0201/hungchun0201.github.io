1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

The paper extends orbital computing from sensing CubeSats to communication megaconstellations through a networking question. After HotNets 2018, CoNEXT 2019 and Hypatia studied path latency, the group asks whether satellites could also host applications. Section 8.5 uses its paired mass, launch and ownership estimates as an orbital-server example.

本文從網路問題切入，把在軌運算從感測 CubeSat 擴展到通訊巨型星座。團隊繼 HotNets 2018、CoNEXT 2019 與 Hypatia 路徑延遲研究後，追問衛星承載應用的條件。第 8.5 節將質量、發射與持有成本估計並列，作為軌道伺服器範例。

The power scale differs from OEC. Denby and Lucia model a 7.5 W Jetson on a CubeSat, while this study budgets 350 W for a 64-core Starlink-hosted server. Later BUPT-1 measurements show 5–10 W boards saturating a 17 kg satellite’s thermal path, adding flight evidence to the comparison.

兩條路線有各自電力尺度：Denby 與 Lucia 為 CubeSat 的 Jetson 編列 7.5 W，本文則為 Starlink 的 64 核伺服器編列 350 W。後續北郵一號量到 5–10 W 板子使 17 kg 衛星傳導路徑飽和，為比較加入飛行證據。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Nearest-satellite propagation sets a geometric floor. Visibility determines which ground clients can use a satellite and how often access changes.

最近衛星傳播設定幾何下限，可見性則決定地面用戶可用衛星及存取更換頻率。

3. The design3. 設計

The hardware budget asks whether a communication satellite can support an ordinary server. Power, cooling, radiation and replacement cycles each add conditions beyond processor capacity.

硬體預算檢驗通訊衛星支援普通伺服器的條件，電力、散熱、輻射與更新週期各自增加處理器之外的需求。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The paper is a thought experiment. Propagation calculations and datasheet-based hardware estimates establish model bounds, while flight predictions require further evidence. Chapter 5 applies the same distinction to path and end-to-end delay.本文屬思想實驗，傳播計算與規格表硬體估計建立模型界限，飛行預測則需要進一步證據，第 5 章也以相同區分處理路徑與端到端延遲。
  • Later measurements give idle medians of 25–50 ms and a bent-pipe floor near 40 ms from PoPs and scheduling. An orbital server removes the PoP leg while retaining access scheduling, a later-observed mechanism outside the original model.後續量測給出 25–50 ms 閒置中位數與約 40 ms 彎管下限，來源包含 PoP 與排程。在軌伺服器移除 PoP 一段，仍保留存取排程，這是原模型以後才觀察到的機制。
  • The 1.5 kW solar estimate is rough, and thermal sizing remains open. A 350 W server must reject the same heat. Later 5–10 W BUPT-1 boards reached thermal limits [85], making conduction and radiator budgets necessary.1.5 kW 太陽能為粗估，熱控尺寸仍待決定，350 W 伺服器須排相同熱量。後續 5–10 W 北郵一號板子已達熱限制 [85]，因此需編列傳導與散熱板預算。
  • Radiation treatment uses an ISS-based argument. Chapter 8 contrasts Jetson TX2 proton-test interruptions with zero observed computational errors over six months at 490 km. Device and exposure conditions determine the comparison.輻射處理採國際太空站先例，第 8 章對照 Jetson TX2 質子中斷與 490 km 六個月的計算錯誤零觀察，元件與暴露條件決定比較範圍。
  • The implied $2,700/kg uses a secondary historical source, while Suncatcher assumes $3,600/kg. Recompute the ownership ratio with an explicit launch-price basis.隱含 2,700 美元/kg 採歷史二手來源，Suncatcher 則假設 3,600 美元/kg，持有成本比應依明確的發射價格重算。
  • The model uses the 2018 filing’s 4,409 satellites, with a 24×66 shell at 550 km. The later 72×22 layout changes visibility and handover intervals. ISL behavior remains an open model component.模型採 2018 申報 4,409 顆，其 550 km 殼層為 24×66，後續 72×22 改變可見性與換手間隔，ISL 行為仍是待建立的模型元件。
  • Sticky reduces migration frequency to a 164 s median interval. Bytes and time required to move server state through ISLs or ground paths remain open, connecting to Chapter 12’s inference systems.Sticky 將遷移頻率降為 164 s 中位間隔，經 ISL 或地面搬狀態所需位元組與時間仍待建立，連結第 12 章推論系統。
