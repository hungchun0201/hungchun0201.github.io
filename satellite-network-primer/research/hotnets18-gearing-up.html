Research agenda and quantitative assumptions
#Reported values and conditions

2Frankfurt–Washington latency is 46.4 ms over the contemporary Internet, 35.8 ms over Hibernia Express, 32.6 ms over great-circle fiber and 21.7 ms in vacuum.
3Dense LEO therefore projects about 35% below Internet latency and 16% below Hibernia [68, §3.3].

6Rough capacity estimates multiply about 12,000 satellites by 20 Gbps to obtain 240 Tbps, against 295 Tbps of fiber.

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

The paper develops a community agenda beyond a single constellation’s latency comparison. Its group later produced CoNEXT 2019 motifs, IMC 2020 Hypatia and the HotNets 2020 orbital-computing study. Chapter 5 follows its sequence from wiring through moving-route behavior and congestion control to capacity economics. Section 5.1 pairs its Frankfurt–Washington comparison with Handley’s New York–London work. Chapter 3 later contrasts the whole-constellation estimate with measurement-era uncertainty in per-satellite capacity.

本文把單一星座的延遲比較擴展為研究議程，作者群後續提出 CoNEXT 2019 motif、IMC 2020 Hypatia 與 HotNets 2020 在軌運算研究。第 5 章沿其順序，從接線、移動路由、壅塞控制談到容量經濟學。第 5.1 節將法蘭克福–華盛頓比較與 Handley 的紐約–倫敦工作並列，第 3 章則以量測時代的每衛星容量資料缺口對照整星座估計。

3. The design3. 設計

Polar geometry forces a trade between short cross-plane links and link lifetime. The density sweep shows how that topology changes routes and delay.

極軌幾何形成短跨面鏈路與壽命的取捨，密度掃描則顯示拓樸如何改變路由與延遲。

Topology and density-sweep results
#Reported values and conditions
1The paper proposes research directions grounded in geometric simulation.
2Four lasers connect fore, aft and adjacent-plane neighbors.
3Polar convergence shortens cross-plane distance and raises relative speed, so those links shut down near the poles, leaving isolated rings [68, §3.1].
4Topology stays static within each simulated minute, exposing the trade between short links and long lifetimes later addressed by motifs.
5In a 302-satellite polar model, shortest paths nearly always beat great-circle fiber with a median twelve hops [68, Fig. 3].
6Frankfurt–São Paulo median RTT falls from 121 ms at 102 satellites to 98 ms at 502 [68, §3.2].
7With 252 satellites, one pair varies about 5 ms around a 32 ms median [68, Fig. 7].
8The authors use this variation to motivate congestion-control work.
9Their economics combines the rough 240 Tbps estimate with manufacturing and launch assumptions to obtain $0.06/GB.

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The synthetic polar model has a specific scope. Its 102–502 satellites at 1,160 km follow polar geometry, while Starlink’s 53° shell retains inter-plane distance near 0.60 of the equatorial value. Polar shutdown behavior therefore informs Iridium-style and Telesat-polar designs (fact base Chapter 2).合成極軌模型有其適用範圍：1,160 km、102–502 顆採極軌幾何，Starlink 的 53° 殼層則保留約赤道值 0.60 的面間距離。因此極區關閉機制適用於 Iridium 式與 Telesat 極軌設計（事實庫第 2 章）。
  • Propagation delay describes geometric paths with zer o queue, node and acquisition costs. Ideal fiber at index 1.5 provides a lower bound. The implied ≈30 ms result measures a separate quantity from measured Starlink delays. These include a bent-pipe floor around 40 ms and idle medians of 25–50 ms (Part C, item 58).傳播延遲描述幾何路徑，佇列、節點與擷取成本為零，折射率 1.5 的理想光纖提供下限。推得約 30 ms 的結果與實測 Starlink 約 40 ms 彎管下限、25–50 ms 閒置中位數，各有量測範圍（C 部第 58 項）。
  • Capacity and cost depend on assumed per-satellite output and terrestrial aggregate capacity. The authors label estimates rough, and later sources still span a wide capacity range (Part C, items 21–22).容量與成本依賴每衛星輸出及地面總容量假設，作者將估計標為粗略，後續來源的容量範圍也仍很廣（C 部第 21–22 項）。
  • Topology updates once per minute, with four static ISLs and zero acquisition cost. Current satellites carry three terminals in an undisclosed arrangement (Part C, items 13, 55 and 56). The lifetime issue motivates later motif work.拓樸每分鐘更新，採四條靜態 ISL 與零擷取成本。目前衛星則有三具終端，排列仍待公開（C 部第 13、55、56 項），鏈路壽命問題促成後續 motif 研究。
  • Congestion-control discussion is a mechanism argument based on RTT variation. Transport testing arrives later in Hypatia, and measured 15-second scheduling adds a disturbance outside this model (Part C, item 57).壅塞控制討論是依 RTT 變化提出的機制論證，Hypatia 後續加入傳輸測試，實測15 秒排程則補上本模型範圍外的干擾（C 部第 57 項）。
  • Interpret each comparison under its definition. Dense-LEO delay uses percentages, city-pair medians use small synthetic constellations, and capacity compares downlink output with backbone capacity under separate definitions.各項比較須依其定義解讀：稠密 LEO 延遲採百分比，城市中位數採小型合成星座，容量則以各自定義對照下行輸出與骨幹容量。
