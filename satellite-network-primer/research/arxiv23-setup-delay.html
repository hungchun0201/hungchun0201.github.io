Laser Inter-Satellite Link Setup Delay: Quantification, Impact, and Tolerable Value雷射星間鏈路建立延遲：量化、影響與可容忍值

WCNC · arXiv 2301.05285Bhattacharjee, Chaudhry, Yanikomeroglu, Hu & Lamontagne · paper論文 preprint

Laser-link setup combines pointing and acquisition time. The model charges it once per path change because new links acquire in parallel. A one-second setup raises the example path's average latency substantially, while longer routes tolerate more setup time.雷射鏈路的建立延遲由指向與擷取時間組成。模型假設新鏈路平行擷取，因此每次路徑改變僅計一次建立延遲。一秒的建立時間便會大幅提高範例路徑的平均延遲，而較長路線能容忍較多建立時間。

Every topology and routing paper before this one treated a laser inter-satellite link as instantly available. This preprint is the first to put a price on that assumption. Those values are two to three orders of magnitude below the terminals that fly. These thresholds explain the value of long-lived links. Each dynamic-topology result in the primer requires a comparison between acquisition cost and path savings.在這篇之前的每一篇拓樸與路由論文都把雷射星間鏈路當成可以立即取用，而這篇預印本第一次為那個假設標上價格。這些值比實際飛行的終端低兩到三個數量級，這就是真實星座維持長壽命鏈路的量化理由，也是本教材中每一個動態拓樸結果都必須帶著這個代價來閱讀的原因。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

The measurement is a sweep of one parameter through a simulated network. Each path change incurs one delay. All newly required links acquire in parallel, so their attempts count together. The packet that triggers the change waits for the slowest of them.

Dijkstra runs every second for an hour. Each slot with a changed path adds one setup delay to that slot's latency. The hour's average is what the paper reports.

這項量測是在模擬網路中掃描一個參數。塑造這篇論文的建模決定是：延遲每次路徑改變計一次，新鏈路的平行擷取則合併計算，因為最短路徑改變時，它需要而所有需要新建的鏈路會同時建立，觸發改變的封包等待其中最慢的一條。一小時內每秒重跑 Dijkstra，路徑與前一秒相異的每個時槽就把一個建立延遲加到該時槽的延遲上，論文報告的是這一小時的平均。

3. The design3. 設計

The construction is easy to follow once the per-path-change rule is accepted. Satellite mean latency combines propagation, node delay and setup delay weighted by the fraction of changed-path slots. Longer laser range reduces hop count and path-change frequency. Both effects reduce mean latency. Fibre latency is fixed by geography. The tolerable delay is therefore the fiber margin divided by the path-change rate, and both numerator and denominator favor long routes and long laser ranges.一旦接受每次路徑改變計一次的規則，這個構造就很容易理解。衛星路徑的平均延遲是它的傳播與節點延遲（隨雷射距離增長而下降，因為更長的鏈路意味更少的跳數），加上建立延遲乘以路徑改變的時槽比例（同樣隨距離下降，因為更稀疏的跳序列較少改變）。光纖延遲由地理固定。因此可容忍延遲就是光纖餘裕除以路徑改變率，分子與分母都偏好長路線與長雷射距離。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The study is a simulation. The constellation snapshot is the November 2018 filing. This is superseded. The node-delay assumption is 1 ms per hop.

Fibre is modeled as light at 204,287,876 m/s along the terrestrial route. Thus, the comparison is propagation-only on both sides and ignores the measured Internet's extra routing and queuing. Capacity, traffic and link rate lie outside the model.

這項研究是模擬。節點延遲假設為每跳 1 ms。光纖被建模為沿地面路線以 204,287,876 m/s 行進的光，所以比較兩側都只算傳播，忽略實測網際網路額外的路由與排隊。容量、流量與鏈路速率皆位於模型範圍之外。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • Preprint status and a superseded shell. The WCNC 2023 listing awaits verification. Thus, every number here carries a preprint tag. The 24 × 66 shell was replaced by 72 × 22 in August 2019 (fact base Part C, item 12). The flown intra-plane hop is 1,969 km. The 1,500 and 1,700 km cases therefore fall short of that neighbor distance. Their path-change rates would differ.預印本身分與已被取代的軌道殼。WCNC 2023 的列名仍待驗證，所以此處每個數字都帶預印本標籤，而 24 × 66 軌道殼在 2019 年 8 月被 72 × 22 取代（事實庫 Part C 第 12 項）。在實際飛行的幾何中同軌道面跳距為 1,969 km，所以 1,500 與 1,700 km 的距離情況低於面內鄰居所需的距離，路徑改變率也會各異。
  • The penalty is for the most naive router. Recomputing Dijkstra every second and switching at every shortest-path change maximizes path changes. A router that holds a path until its links break, or TinyLEO's matching that favors long-lived links [39, §4], would pay setup delay far less often. Thus, the 98 ms-per-second penalty is an upper bound for the modeled geometry rather than a property of laser links.代價是針對最天真的路由器。每秒重算 Dijkstra 並在每次最短路徑改變時切換，會使路徑改變次數最大化。一個維持路徑直到其鏈路斷掉的路由器，或是 TinyLEO 偏好長壽命鏈路的配對[39, §4]，付建立延遲的次數會少得多，所以每秒 98 ms 的代價是對所建模幾何的上界，其適用範圍限於這套幾何與路由規則。
  • Vendor figures are second-hand and the CONDOR rate claim requires evidence beyond the product page. The paper relays 30 s and 2 s CONDOR values from Mynaric material. Direct datasheet verification remains pending (Part C, item 18 company ). The current CONDOR page lists 0.313–2.5 Gbps, with 10 Gbps upcoming (Part D, item 4). The associated "up to 100 Gbps" figure requires another source. Setup time and rate should both be treated as vendor claims of their date.廠商數字是二手的，且 CONDOR 的速率宣稱仍待產品頁以外的證據。CONDOR 的 30 秒與 2 秒數值是透過這篇論文對 Mynaric 資料的引用進入本教材，規格書證據則仍待補充（Part C 第 18 項 company），而與之一同流傳的「最高 100 Gbps」CONDOR 數字仍待目前產品頁以外的證據，該頁列的是 0.313–2.5 Gbps、10 Gbps 即將推出（Part D 第 4 項）。建立時間與速率都應視為帶有日期的廠商宣稱。
  • Node delay is 1 ms here and 10 ms in the companion paper. Both remain unmeasured (Part C, item 19). Node delay shifts the zero-setup baseline and fiber margin. Both 2.3 and 80.63 ms thresholds would change under the 10 ms assumption. This makes a long path's baseline tens of milliseconds slower and its margin over fiber smaller.這裡的節點延遲是 1 ms，姊妹論文是 10 ms。兩者都量測資料仍待補充（Part C 第 19 項）。這個選擇會移動建立延遲為零的基準，因而移動光纖餘裕，所以 2.3 與 80.63 ms 的可容忍值在 10 ms 假設下都會改變，因為那會使長路徑的基準慢上數十毫秒、對光纖的餘裕變小。
  • Fibre is propagation-only. The comparison uses light in glass as a strict baseline. Handley finds measured New York–London Internet latency 21 ms above great-circle fiber. [3, Fig. 7] Against real paths the satellite network would tolerate more setup delay. Equally, the satellite side omits the measured 40 ms bent-pipe floor of Chapter 3. Thus, both sides represent propagation-model estimates.光纖只計傳播。與玻璃中的光相比、實測網際網路則另有路由及排隊因素[3, Fig. 7]，是嚴格的選擇。對照真實路徑，衛星網路能容忍更多建立延遲。同樣地，衛星側省略了第 3 章實測的 40 ms 彎管地板，所以兩側都屬於端到端範圍以外的數字。
  • Terminal count and parallel acquisition are unstated here. The fact base leaves the terminals-per-satellite figure open for this preprint. The once-per-path-change rule assumes every new link on the path acquires simultaneously. This requires a free terminal at each end of each new link. With three terminals per current Starlink satellite (Part C, item 13 company) that spare capacity still requires a hardware feasibility check.終端數與平行擷取在此說明資料待補。這篇預印本每顆衛星的終端數仍待事實庫補充，而每次路徑改變計一次的規則假設路徑上每條新鏈路同時擷取，這要求每條新鏈路兩端各有一個空閒終端。以目前 Starlink 衛星每顆三個終端（Part C 第 13 項 company）來看，這種備用容量仍須核對硬體可提供的備用容量。
