Characterizing the Configuration of Starlink Queuing刻畫 Starlink 排隊機制的組態

ACM IMC 2026 · arXiv 2605.27717Garcia, Sundberg, Brunstrom (Karlstad University) · arXiv preprintarXiv 預印本 accepted to IMC 2026 read from arXiv

This digest explains how timed probes identify Starlink’s shared drop-front downlink queue. It shows why discarding the oldest packet reduces delivered-packet delay and gives loss-based senders an earlier congestion signal.讀完後，你能說明超過一千萬個硬體時間戳探測封包如何揭露 Starlink 下行瓶頸是一個約 1,400–1,600 封包、採前端丟棄與共享 FIFO 排程，CoDel 類控制器則在推論模型之外的單一佇列，以及為什麼丟前端佇列能降低延遲，卻提早把壅塞訊號交給基於遺失的傳送端。

Main result
主要結果

Timed UDP probes reveal a shared drop-front queue at Starlink’s downlink bottleneck. Discarding the oldest packet lowers delivered-packet delay and advances the loss signal.

定時 UDP 探測揭露 Starlink 下行瓶頸的共用丟前端佇列，丟棄最舊封包會降低送達封包延遲，並提早傳出遺失訊號。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Controlled bursts expose capacity, sharing and delay plateaus. The observed packet sequence supports a common packet-count queue with drop-front behavior.

受控突發揭露容量、共享與延遲高原，觀察到的封包序列支持以封包數為限、採丟前端行為的共用佇列。

3. The design3. 設計

A manually fitted queue simulator compares discard rules against packet-level delay and loss. Frame-modulated draining reproduces the loss positions and locates the queue before resource allocation.

人工擬合的佇列模擬器將丟棄規則與逐封包延遲、遺失比較，受幀調變的排空能重現遺失位置，將佇列定位在資源分配之前。

Item項目Values數值Conditions and source條件與來源
Latency comparison1 µsA Python queue model uses 1 µs time slots. Queue size, drain rate and drop discipline are manually fitted to each burst's delay and loss sequence [45, §6].
Evidence and scopeQualitative scopeTwo features of the empirical pattern decide the discipline.
Latency comparisonQualitative scopeUnder drop-tail, a full queue drops the arriving packet. Thus, the packets that do get through all wait the full queue length and the delay of successive delivered packets stays flat at the maximum.
Dataset and workloadQualitative scopeUnder drop-front, a full queue discards the packet at its head to admit the new arrival. Thus, a delivered packet is one that was retained until service. The per-packet delay trace shows a characteristic shape that the simulation reproduces only when the head is dropped [45, Figs. 3–4].
Latency comparison1,400–1,600The queue size then follows from the number of packets in flight when loss begins and from the plateau delay, giving the 1,400–1,600-packet range. The fact that the limit is in packets rather than bytes follows from the size being independent of packet length [45, §7, Figs. 5, 7].
Evidence and scope1.33 msMatching within-burst losses requires one access-enabled frame followed by three idle frames, synchronized to Starlink's 1.33 ms frame. This places the queue before resource allocation, while satellite-versus-gateway location remains unresolved [45, §6].
證據與範圍1 µs分析工具是一個 1 µs 解析度的 Python 時槽佇列模擬器，其參數，即佇列大小、排空速率與丟棄機制，以人工調整直到模擬出的逐封包延遲與遺失序列與某次突發的實測結果相符[45, §6]。

6. Limits and open questions6. 範圍與待解問題

  • The fit is manual. Queue size, drain rate and discipline were adjusted by eye until simulated and empirical traces matched, using visual comparison. The simulator ignores the drain-rate adaptation the authors themselves observed. Thus, the 1,400–1,600-packet range and the 85 versus 122 ms comparison are fitted descriptions rather than measured constants [45, §7–9].擬合是人工的。佇列大小、排空速率與丟棄機制靠目測調整直到模擬與實測軌跡相符，僅採目測誤差比較，而且模擬器忽略了作者自己觀察到的排空速率調整，所以 1,400–1,600 封包的範圍與 85 對 122 ms 的比較是擬合出的描述，其適用條件應隨設定註明[45, §7–9]。
  • The scope covers two downlink sites. Uplink queue behavior remains open, including the 10:1 asymmetry and roughly 30 Mbps saturation in the Fries study. SpaceX reports deploying fq_codel and resizing buffers in March 2024 company. The 2025 downlink observations show different behavior, so the relationship between those sources needs explanation.僅下行、兩地、一台主要終端。對上行佇列列於範圍之外，而 10:1 的方向差異與 Fries 等人看到的約 30 Mbps 飽和都在上行。SpaceX 也可隨時更改組態，就像它自稱在 2024 年 3 月部署 fq_codel 並調整緩衝區大小那樣 company。2025 年探測到的下行顯示共享 FIFO 排程及佇列長度式丟棄行為，與該說法存在差異，兩個來源都對這項差異的說明待補充。
  • CUBIC utilization needs a controlled test. Prior work reports CUBIC below one-quarter of BBR throughput and TCP at 39% of UDP. Early front-drop loss could explain those results and synchronize flows. Controller tests against the fitted queue remain open [45, §6, §9].CUBIC 利用率較低的原因仍待對照實驗驗證。作者引用先前 CUBIC 低於 BBR 吞吐量四分之一、TCP 只有 UDP 39% 的報告，主張丟前端佇列的提早遺失可以解釋這些現象並可能使流同步，但他們針對擬合佇列的壅塞控制器測試待完成[45, §6, §9]。
  • Packet-count limits have an unquantified real-time cost. Because the limit is in packets, small voice or game packets fill the queue with little byte volume, which the paper discusses qualitatively. The byte figure of 2.25 MB in the calc table is a derivation for 1,500-byte packets, a direct derivation for this primer.以封包數為限對即時流量的代價待量化。由於上限以封包數計，小的語音或遊戲封包會以很少的位元組量填滿佇列，論文提到卻待量測。計算表中 2.25 MB 的位元組數是對 1,500 位元組封包的推導，屬於本教材的直接推導。
  • The values use the preprint. The accepted IMC 2026 paper is represented here by arXiv 2605.27717v1. Proceedings section numbers and values may change. Scheduled reconfiguration supplies background context for the queue analysis.本導讀使用預印本版本。論文已被 IMC 2026 接受，但此處數字來自 arXiv 2605.27717v1，章節編號甚至數值在會議版可能相異，而 15 秒重組在本文中只作為背景，佇列行為量測另有其控制條件。
