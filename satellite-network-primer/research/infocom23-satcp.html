SaTCP: Link-Layer Informed TCP Adaptation for Highly Dynamic LEO Satellite NetworksSaTCP：以鏈路層資訊調適 TCP，面向高度動態的 LEO 衛星網路

IEEE INFOCOM 2023Cao & Zhang (UC San Diego) · paper論文 peer-reviewed

This digest explains how predicted handover windows let SaTCP retain its congestion window. It also examines the resulting throughput gain and the loss of fairness toward a competing CUBIC flow.讀完後，你能說明一個被告知下一次換手何時發生的傳送端，如何略過換手原本會觸發的視窗縮減、為什麼這讓模擬的 CUBIC 流從 34.3 Mbps 升到 138.1 Mbps，以及為什麼同一個機制會餓死共享鏈路的原版 CUBIC 流。

Main result
主要結果

SaTCP uses predicted handovers to retain CUBIC’s congestion window during disruption. Emulated throughput improves, while a competing unmodified flow loses much of its share.

SaTCP 依預測換手，在擾動期間保留 CUBIC 壅塞視窗，模擬吞吐量因而提高，共用鏈路的原版流則失去大部分份額。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Hard handovers interrupt access, while intermediate handovers alter routes and can discard in-flight packets. CUBIC’s recovery time determines how strongly repeated disruptions reduce throughput.

硬換手會中斷接入，中間換手則改變路由，可能丟失在途封包。CUBIC 的恢復時間決定了反覆擾動對吞吐量的影響。

3. The design3. 設計

Terminals and ground infrastructure predict disruptions and report them to the host. A kernel flag temporarily changes CUBIC’s response to loss and timeout, then restores its default behavior.

終端與地面設施預測擾動並回報主機，核心旗標暫時改變 CUBIC 對遺失及逾時的反應，再恢復預設行為。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

LeoEM precomputes a route and updates Mininet link properties along it. The experiment controls handover timing, link caps and queue size to isolate transport dynamics.

LeoEM 預先計算路徑，再更新沿途 Mininet 鏈路性質。實驗控制換手時序、鏈路上限與佇列大小，以觀察傳輸動態。

6. Limits and open questions6. 範圍與待解問題

  • Emulated evaluation and a fixed constellation model. Live observations comprise the motivating 300-second CUBIC download and a 200-second San Diego–Ohio RTT trace [67, §III-C, §VI-A, Figs. 1, 7]. The conclusion proposes routing with less frequent updates as future work [67, §VII]. Later, LeoCC authors tested SaTCP on three live Starlink terminals with their own reconfiguration detector. SaTCP performed similarly to CUBIC because random loss within each interval still reduced its window [74, §6.1–6.2]. LeoEM models four +Grid ISLs per satellite in the 72×22 filing. Actual Starlink satellites have three laser terminals, with topology details held by the operator (factbase Part C, items 13 and 55). Laser acquisition and setup delay remain outside the model (item 56).僅有模擬，且星座是凍結的。除了作為動機的 300 秒 CUBIC 下載與一段從聖地牙哥到俄亥俄的 200 秒 RTT 軌跡之外，實際 Starlink 上的測試仍待完成[67, §III-C, §VI-A, Figs. 1, 7]，結論把更新頻率較低的路由策略，實地測試則待完成列為下一步[67, §VII]。後來 LeoCC 的作者在三台實際 Starlink 終端上執行 SaTCP，用自己的重組偵測器驅動其凍結邏輯，結果它的表現與 CUBIC 相近，因為每個區間內的隨機遺失仍會觸發縮減[74, §6.1–6.2]。LeoEM 假設 72×22 申請文件下每顆衛星有四個 +Grid ISL，而目前的 Starlink 衛星配備三個雷射終端、拓樸待公開（事實庫 Part C 第 13、55 項），雷射擷取與建鏈延遲列於模擬範圍之外（第 56 項）。
  • The 150 Mb/s cap is an emulator link-cap setting. It is a tc setting applied to every link including ISLs. Thus, the ISL-path results describe dynamics rather than capacity, in line with factbase Part C item 60 on simulator link rates. The absolute Mbps figures require separate interpretation from measured Starlink throughput.150 Mb/s 上限是模擬輸入。它是套在包含 ISL 在內每條鏈路上的 tc 設定，因此 ISL 路徑的結果描述了所設容量下的動態，呼應事實庫 Part C 第 60 項對模擬器鏈路速率的提醒。絕對 Mbps 數字應以動態模型結果解讀，實測 Starlink 吞吐量另行比較。
  • Two baselines and two flows. The study compares CUBIC and BBR, with two flows in its fairness tests. At the 2.3 s setting, the competing CUBIC flow falls to 8.4 Mbps. This result establishes a fairness problem within that configuration. Garcia et al.’s later shared-queue measurements motivate broader tests of competing traffic.兩個基準、兩條流。只比較了 CUBIC 與 BBR，公平性只用兩條流測試，因此在 2.3 s 設定下把 CUBIC 餓到 8.4 Mbps，只是共享下行佇列中傷害的下限，而 Garcia 等人後來發現這個佇列採用共享 FIFO 排程。
  • Coordination across operator systems. The terminal, ground station, TT&C system, and host kernel exchange timing messages. Public TLE predictions refresh every three hours [67, §V-B–V-C]. LeoCC later used 10 ms ICMP probes from the user’s device to the point of presence. This approach places detection on the user’s device and retains a local software requirement [74, §4.1.1, §5.1].它需要基礎設施供應者掌控的基礎設施。終端、地面站、TT&C 系統與主機核心都必須參與並交換時間訊息，而預測依賴每三小時更新的公開 TLE[67, §V-B–V-C]。LeoCC 後來以使用者裝置每 10 ms 向網路接入點送出的 ICMP 探測取代這一串環節，把終端與地面站移出迴路，但仍需要使用者端的軟體[74, §4.1.1, §5.1]。
  • The emulator is generous to SaTCP. Reports are generated from the emulator's own handover times with only the TLE-derived timing error added. The 150 Mb/s cap with a queue of about 260 ms is chosen so that loss comes from handovers rather than queue overflow [67, §VI-A]. Real Starlink adds random loss of 0.5–6% inside each interval, which is where LeoCC's live test found SaTCP falling back to CUBIC-like throughput [74, §2.1, §6.2].模擬平台對 SaTCP 有利。回報是由模擬器自己的換手時刻產生，只加上由 TLE 推得的時間誤差，而 150 Mb/s 上限與約 260 ms 的佇列是刻意選來讓換手成為遺失來源，同時保留佇列容量餘裕[67, §VI-A]。實際 Starlink 在每個區間內還有 0.5–6% 的隨機遺失，LeoCC 的實地測試正是在這裡發現 SaTCP 退回類似 CUBIC 的吞吐量[74, §2.1, §6.2]。
  • Independent evaluation adds a delay cost. Mazilu et al. reproduce SaTCP’s throughput gain on LeoEM. They report single-flow delay inflation of 2.30× for SaTCP and 2.14× for CUBIC, with RTT unfairness in both protocols. These preprint results extend the original evaluation’s two metrics.獨立評估揭示延遲代價。Mazilu 等人在 LeoEM 上重現了吞吐量增益，卻量到 SaTCP 單流延遲膨脹 2.30 倍、CUBIC 2.14 倍，且兩者都對 RTT 公平性較低 preprint，這是原先兩項指標評估之外的發現。
