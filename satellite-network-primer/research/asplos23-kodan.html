Kodan: Addressing the Computational Bottleneck in SpaceKodan：解決太空中的運算瓶頸

ASPLOS 2023Bradley Denby, Krishna Chintalapudi, Ranveer Chandra, Brandon Lucia, Shadi Noghabi (Carnegie Mellon University, Microsoft Research) · paper論文 · open copy (NSF PAR)公開版本（NSF PAR） peer-reviewed

On-board inference creates a deadline for each camera frame. Kodan uses context-specialized models, tiling and selective processing to meet that deadline on embedded hardware. Its gain measures the high-value fraction of a saturated downlink.機上推論為每幀影像增加處理期限。Kodan 使用情境特化模型、影像切塊與選擇性處理，讓嵌入式硬體趕上期限。其增益衡量飽和下行資料流中的高價值比例。

Kodan starts where orbital edge computing left off. Before launch, Kodan groups a representative dataset into geographic contexts and trains specialized models. It selects tiles per frame to balance precision and execution time. It elides processing for tiles whose context is mostly high-value.

These are downlinked, or mostly low-value. These are discarded, with a selection logic that picks the combination for each tile [83, §3] . The execution times were measured on ground hardware and the orbit was simulated, so all results come from ground measurements and simulation.

Kodan 從軌道邊緣運算停下的地方開始。Kodan 的答案是在發射前依目標硬體改造應用：把代表性資料集聚類成地理情境並訓練特化模型，選擇每幀切成幾個圖塊以精確度換取時間，並對情境以高價值為主的圖塊直接下傳、以低價值為主的圖塊直接丟棄而略過處理，再由選擇邏輯為每個圖塊挑出組合[83, §3]。執行時間是在地面硬體上量測、軌道則是模擬的，所以論文中所有數字皆來自地面量測與模擬。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

Chapter 8 teaches computing in orbit as three walls that appear in sequence, and Kodan is the second wall. Denby and Lucia [60] identify the first bottleneck: a 200 Mbit/s station receives less data than the camera produces. On-board inference reduces that downlink demand. Kodan observes that the fix creates its own shortage.

The processor must fit satellite volume, mass, energy and cost limits while processing every frame. The study represents it with a 15 W Jetson AGX Orin. The camera keeps its original capture rate. Xing et al. [85] identify heat as the third bottleneck.

A few watts of sustained computation saturate small-satellite thermal paths before processor or radio limits. The three studies show the pattern repeated at larger scale in Part III. Relieving one resource limit exposes the next.

第 8 章把在軌運算教成三道依序出現的牆，Kodan 是第二道。第一道由同一位第一作者在 Denby 與 Lucia[60]記錄，是下行鏈路：200 Mbit/s 的地面站接收量低於奈米衛星相機產生的資料量，所以推論必須在機上進行。Kodan 觀察到這個解法製造了自己的短缺，因為現在必須對每一幀的每個圖塊跑分割網路的處理器，只能是塞得進衛星體積、質量、能源與成本預算的元件，論文以 15 W 模式的 Jetson AGX Orin 為代表，而相機仍依原速率產生影像。第三道牆是熱，隨 Xing 等人[85]而來，他們飛了商用板子，發現持續幾瓦的運算在觸及任何處理器或無線電限制之前，就已經讓小衛星的散熱路徑飽和。三篇合在一起讀，呈現出本教材第三部分在更大尺度上反覆出現的模式：每解除一種資源的限制，就暴露出下一種。

Kodan connects CMU orbital-edge research with Microsoft Research space systems. The latter later produced FedSpace, reviewed after Chapter 9. The shared model treats satellites as resource-limited nodes with intermittent connectivity. An onboard runtime allocates scarce resources between ground contacts.

Kodan 也是 CMU 軌道邊緣路線通往 Microsoft Research 太空系統興趣的橋樑，後者之後產出了第 9 章後導讀的 FedSpace 聯邦學習框架。共同前提是：衛星是一個弱小、間歇連線的節點，其稀缺資源必須由執行期自動來分配。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Kodan measures a mismatch between frame-arrival and compute clocks in three steps. The cote simulator models orbit, sensing, communication and ground infrastructure. Published Landsat performance validates the model. [83, §2.1]Kodan 量的是兩個時鐘之間的差距，論文分三步用 cote 模擬器把它建立起來。cote 模擬軌道、感測、通訊與地面段，作者以公開的 Landsat 效能數字驗證過它[83, §2.1]。第一步是下行鏈路：一圈軌道內，Landsat 地面段只能接收 2% 可得的萬像素高光譜觀測。而要每天觀測到每一個 Landsat 幀，星座至少需要 40 顆衛星[83, §2.1.1, Figs. 2–3]。成本的錨點則是 Landsat 任務的 8.55 億美元，作為單體衛星動輒數億美元的例子[83, §2]。

Measure or comparison參數或比較項目Values, conditions and evidence數值、條件與證據
Constellation configurationThe Landsat ground segment receives only 2% of available hyperspectral observations over one orbit. Frames have 10,000 pixels. Adding satellites raises downlinked frames from 5 with one satellite to 60 with sixteen, until stations saturate. A constellation needs at least 40 satellites to observe every Landsat frame each day [83, §2.1.1, Figs. 2–3] .
AccuracyCloud obscures about 67% of images. Of nearly 3,600 daily observable Landsat 8 frames, only a third are high-value. A bent pipe delivers less than 21% of observable high-value data. An ideal on-board filter with perfect accuracy and zero execution time would deliver 63%, three times as much [83, §2.1.2, Fig. 4] .
Evaluation conditionsThe third step is the processor. A cloud filter takes 98 s against a 22 s frame deadline. It processes only a fraction of frames, raising high-value downlink by 9%. [83, §2.1.3, Fig. 5]
Onboard processorsRelated work lists the 32-bit SPARC V8 LEON at 250 MHz, EO-1 Mongoose-V at 12 MHz, and RAD5500 at 466 MHz. After two or more decades, these processors "could be nearly half a century behind the state-of-the-art". The $855 million Landsat mission illustrates monolithic satellite costs of hundreds of millions of dollars. [83, §6] [83, §2]
軌道面配置在同一軌道面增加衛星，可以把每個週期下傳的幀數從一顆衛星的 5 幀提高到十六顆的 60 幀，但只到地面站飽和為止。
影像篩選收益第二步是過濾的價值：約 67% 的衛星影像被雲遮蔽，所以一顆 Landsat 8 一天可觀測的近 3,600 幀中只有三分之一是高價值的，彎管只送下這些可觀測高價值資料的低於 21%，而準確率完美、執行時間為零的理想機上過濾器可送下 63%，是三倍[83, §2.1.2, Fig. 4]。
評估條件第三步是處理器：一個實際的雲層過濾器每幀需要 98 秒，而每幀期限是 22 秒，所以直接部署只能過濾部分幀，下傳的高價值資料只增加 9%[83, §2.1.3, Fig. 5]。
評估條件構成問題背景的飛行履歷元件出現在相關研究一節，作者列出 LEON（達到 250 MHz 的 32 位元 SPARC V8 核心）、EO-1 上 12 MHz 的 Mongoose-V 與 466 MHz 的 RAD5500，並指出運作二十年以上之後，這類處理器「可能落後最先進技術近半個世紀」[83, §6]。

Data-value density measures the high-value share of bytes crossing a saturated downlink. For example, land pixels with clear skies provide value while ocean cloud tops may add little. A bent pipe downlinks whatever the camera saw and has the lowest density. An ideal on-board filter would downlink only valuable pixels. And a compute-bottlenecked satellite sits between them because it can filter only the frames it has time to process.

論文自己衡量利害的指標是資料價值密度，也就是通過飽和下行鏈路的位元組中，攜帶任務想要資訊的比例，例如陸地上晴空陸地像素，海洋雲頂則屬於低價值內容。彎管把相機看到的全部下傳，密度最低。理想的機上過濾器只下傳有價值的像素。運算受限的衛星介於兩者之間，因為它只能過濾來得及處理的那些幀。

3. The design3. 設計

Kodan's design is a one-time transformation before launch followed by a runtime that applies it [83, §3.1, Fig. 7] . Before launch, Kodan partitions representative data into contexts. Experts can define contexts such as ocean, desert and city. Position and orientation then locate the context on a map.

Alternatively, k-means groups label vectors containing geographic and weather features. A small classification network, the context engine, labels orbital tiles. The model treats its output as ground truth. [83, §3.2] It trains models for individual contexts or context groups. Each has measured target-hardware execution time and precision.

A specialized model can be smaller than the datacenter reference application while keeping or improving its accuracy [83, §3.3] . A cote simulation sweeps tiles per frame and processing elision for each context. It models target orbit, sensor, radio and ground infrastructure. The combination maximizing data-value density becomes the selection logic. [83, §3.4]

Kodan 的設計是發射前的一次性轉換，加上在軌套用它的執行期[83, §3.1, Fig. 7]。發射前，Kodan 把代表性資料集分成情境，可以由領域專家劃分，此時海洋、沙漠或城市之類的情境可由衛星位置與姿態投影到地圖上得知。也可以自動劃分，以 k-means 聚類記錄各樣本地理與天氣特徵的標籤向量，此時由一個小型分類網路，也就是情境引擎，在軌為每個圖塊標上情境，並把它的輸出當作真值[83, §3.2]。接著它訓練特化於單一情境或一組情境的模型，每個模型在目標硬體上的執行時間與精確度都已知，而特化模型可以比資料中心的參考應用更小，同時維持或提高準確率[83, §3.3]。最後，它對目標衛星的軌道、感測器、無線電與地面段做 cote 模擬，掃描每幀圖塊數與各情境的處理或略過選項，把使資料價值密度最大的組合保留為選擇邏輯[83, §3.4]。

At runtime, frames are divided into the selected tile count. The context engine labels each tile. Selection logic chooses raw downlink, discard or a context-specialized model. [83, §3.1] Tiling is the time knob. A 10,000 × 10,000-pixel frame becomes four 5,000-pixel tiles or sixteen 2,500-pixel tiles.

Each tile is resized to network input dimensions. Thus, the tile count sets the frame's processing time while fewer, larger tiles lose detail to decimation [83, §3, Fig. 6] . When mean runtime exceeds the frame deadline, Kodan trades precision for speed through coarser tiling and high-value-context elision. When runtime fits, idle time supports more precise specialized models.

Elision then targets contexts containing almost entirely high-value data. [83, §3.4]

在軌時，每一幀被切成選定數目的圖塊，情境引擎為每個圖塊標上情境，選擇邏輯再決定這個圖塊是直接下傳原始內容、丟棄，還是用其情境的特化模型處理[83, §3.1]。切塊是時間的旋鈕：一幀 10,000 × 10,000 像素的 Landsat 影像切成四塊 5,000 像素或十六塊 2,500 像素的圖塊，再逐塊縮放到網路的輸入尺寸，所以圖塊數決定了每幀的處理時間，而較少、較大的圖塊會因降取樣而失去細節[83, §3, Fig. 6]。策略跟著瓶頸走：應用平均超出每幀期限時，Kodan 以較粗的切塊與略過高價值情境，用較低精確度換取較短執行時間。趕得上期限時，它把閒置時間花在最精確的特化模型上，只略過幾乎全是高價值資料的情境[83, §3.4]。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The evaluation combines execution times measured on ground hardware with a simulated orbit, with flight validation outside its scope [83, §4] . Landsat 8 supplies orbit, camera and World Reference System ground-track captures. Ground stations retain Landsat positions and link characteristics. cote derives each deployment's frame deadline from orbit. Thus, the downlink is a simulated, saturated Landsat ground segment rather than a rate the paper states.

The design uses local computation and ground links only. The two baselines are the bent pipe and the direct deployment of each unmodified application [83, §4, §5.1] .

評估結合了在地面硬體上量測的執行時間與模擬的軌道，飛行驗證則位於範圍之外[83, §4]。軌道、相機與影像擷取都是 Landsat 8 沿其世界參考系統（WRS）地面軌跡的情形，地面段是 Landsat 的地面站及其位置與鏈路特性，cote 由軌道算出每個部署的每幀期限，所以下行鏈路是模擬的、飽和的 Landsat 地面段，其設定依據為模擬的地面段。星間鏈路位於設計範圍之外，兩個基準是彎管與直接部署原版應用[83, §4, §5.1]。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • Ground hardware and a simulated orbit. The motivation cites 12–466 MHz flight-heritage processors. Evaluation instead combines Orin, i7 and 1070 Ti execution times with a Landsat 8 cote simulation. The 15 W Orin is closest to CubeSat conditions. Radiation tests and flight validation remain pending for all three evaluation platforms. Thus, the single-event upsets and flight-processor behavior require separate evidence.地面硬體加上模擬軌道。瓶頸以 12–466 MHz 的飛行履歷元件來鋪陳，示範卻是把在 Orin、i7 與 1070 Ti 上量到的執行時間，結合 Landsat 8 的 cote 模擬。三者中 15 W 的 Orin 最接近立方衛星，但三者的輻射測試與飛行驗證皆仍待完成，所以單事件翻轉與飛行處理器表現皆須另行取得證據。
  • Dataset and orbit come from separate missions. The samples are Sentinel-2 images while the frames, deadlines and ground segment are Landsat 8's. The dataset's 48% high-value share, with the cited 67% global cloud fraction describing a separate quantity the paper cites in §2, sets the bent-pipe baseline. A cloudier distribution would lower the bent-pipe density and change how much elision gains.資料集與軌道差異。樣本是 Sentinel-2 影像，而影像幀、期限與地面段卻是 Landsat 8 的。決定彎管基準的是資料集 48% 的高價值比例，論文 §2 引用的全球 67% 雲量則屬於另一項統計。雲更多的分布會降低彎管密度，也會改變略過處理的增益。
  • Thermal budget absent. Kodan assumes a fixed compute capacity per frame. Xing et al. find a Raspberry Pi 4B above 75% load reaches throttling within about 50 minutes in orbit. Warming therefore reduces achievable runtime performance. Kodan leaves this effect outside its scheduler model. [85]熱預算位於模型範圍之外。Kodan 假設每幀的運算容量固定。Xing 等人[85]發現 Raspberry Pi 4B 在負載超過 75% 時約 50 分鐘內就在軌進入降頻區，所以執行期該據以規劃的期限會隨衛星升溫而縮短，Kodan 的排程器將此效應排除於模型之外。
  • Saturated downlink by assumption. The data-value-density metric only makes sense when the downlink is the binding constraint. Chapter 3 records 200 Gbps-class TBIRD downlinks. At that rate, raw-data bent pipes may again suit some missions. This would change the comparison baseline.下行飽和是假設。資料價值密度這個指標只在下行鏈路是束縛限制時才有意義。第 3 章顯示 TBIRD 已飛過 200 Gbps 等級的光學下行，在那種速率下某些任務的彎管可能重新變得有吸引力，比較基準也會隨之改變。
  • Local processing and ground links only. The decision is per satellite and per tile. Neighbor-assisted processing remains open. OEC coordinates work through formation, while Kodan leaves ISL rate, latency and topology outside its scope.星間鏈路位於設計範圍之外。決定是逐衛星、逐圖塊。過載衛星依 OEC 編隊排程把區塊交給鄰近衛星的可行性，仍待探討，所以 Kodan 將 ISL 速率、延遲及拓樸列於範圍之外。
  • The context engine is taken as ground truth. For automatically generated contexts the engine's output is treated as ground truth when the dataset is partitioned [83, §3.2]. The evaluation leaves unreported how often a tile's context is misclassified or what that costs in valuable tiles discarded. Cloud changes hourly, so map-based expert contexts would face the same question.情境引擎被當作真值。自動產生情境時，劃分資料集就把引擎的輸出當作真值[83, §3.2]，評估圖塊情境誤分類的頻率及因此丟棄的高價值圖塊量皆仍待報告。雲逐小時變化，所以依地圖劃分的專家情境也會遇到同樣的問題。
  • Data-value density within the transmitted stream. Data-value density divides high-value downlinked bytes by total downlinked bytes. Discarded or misclassified high-value tiles lie outside that denominator. The evaluation in §5 reports how clean the downlinked stream is rather than how much of the observable high-value data reaches the ground. This is the quantity §2 used to motivate the work.指標衡量傳輸資料流內的價值密度。資料價值密度是通過飽和鏈路的資料中高價值的比例，所以被略過處理丟掉或被過濾器標錯的高價值圖塊位於其分母範圍之外。§5 的評估報告的是下傳資料流有多乾淨，可觀測高價值資料的總送達量則屬於另一項指標，後者正是 §2 用來說明研究動機的量。
  • Seven applications of one kind. All seven workloads are cloud-filtering segmentation, so the 89–97% range speaks to one task family. Detection or change-tracking workloads with different value distributions could behave differently.七個同類應用。七個工作負載全是雲過濾分割，所以 89–97% 的範圍只對一類任務成立。價值分布相異的偵測或變化追蹤工作負載可能表現各異。
