1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

Chapter 12 compares three orbital-inference preprints whose ISL assumptions differ by over two orders of magnitude. SpaceMoE uses the optimistic rate assumption. It alone spreads a model across the whole constellation. CollabLLM-LEO chains five satellites through 0.5 Gbit/s links, while LLMSpace budgets carbon for one DGX-class payload.

SpaceMoE places mixture-of-experts modules across a thousand satellites. Its objective minimizes the expected path followed by token hidden states. [96] [97] Its output is a placement on a graph whose edges change with orbital motion. This makes network placement central to the problem. It is also the paper whose headline number turns out to say least about the network, for reasons that Section 3 makes concrete.

第 12 章把已發表的在軌語言模型推論研究歸成三篇預印本，它們對 ISL 能承載多少的假設相差超過兩個數量級，而 SpaceMoE 位於這個範圍樂觀的那一端，也是唯一把單一模型分散到整個星座的論文。CollabLLM-LEO[96]用 0.5 Gbit/s 鏈路串起五顆衛星，LLMSpace[97]為一個 DGX 等級的酬載估算碳排，SpaceMoE 則問：混合專家模型的專家應該如何擺放在一千顆衛星上，才能讓一個 token 的隱藏狀態以最短的期望路徑穿過各層。因此它是這組論文中最像網路問題的一篇，因為它的輸出是一張圖上的放置，而這張圖的邊會隨軌道運動出現又消失。它同時也是頭條數字對網路說明最少的一篇，原因會在第 3 節具體說明。

A mixture of experts transformer divides each layer's feed-forward block into experts. A small router activates only a few experts per token. Thus, the parameters that must be resident somewhere are many more than the parameters exercised for any one token. That property is what makes the idea attractive for a constellation.

This occurs because each satellite can hold a slice of a model too large for any single board while the per-token work stays bounded. It is also what makes the traffic distinctive. This occurs because the hidden state must visit whichever experts the router picks at each of the 32 layers.

混合專家（mixture of experts）是一種 transformer，它每一層的前饋區塊被拆成數個專家，由一個小型路由器對每個 token 只啟動其中少數幾個，因此必須常駐在某處的參數遠多於任何一個 token 實際用到的參數。這個性質正是它對星座有吸引力的原因：每顆衛星可以只存放一個大到任何單板都記憶體需求超出單板容量的模型的一片，而每個 token 的工作量仍有上限。這個性質也讓它的流量很尤其，因為隱藏狀態在 32 層中的每一層都必須拜訪路由器所選中的專家。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

The problem the authors pose is latency per generated token when the experts live on different satellites. The radiation-hardened single-board computer has realistic flight heritage. Chapter 12 compares it with CollabLLM-LEO's Orin-class modules and LLMSpace's H100-class payload. Its compute rate is three to four orders of magnitude below modern accelerators, strongly shaping the evaluation.作者提出的問題是：當專家分住在各異衛星上時，每產生一個 token 的延遲是多少。選用抗輻射單板電腦在飛行資歷上是務實的，第 12 章把它拿來與 CollabLLM-LEO 假設的 Orin 等級模組以及 LLMSpace 假設的 H100 等級酬載對照，但這也讓處理器比現代加速器低了三到四個數量級，而這個差距左右了評估所能顯示的一切。

3. The design3. 設計

The expert placement works at two levels. The first level creates one contiguous ring-based subnet per MoE layer. Each subnet has a central gateway satellite [78, Eqs. 17–18] . Thus, a token moves from layer to layer by stepping between neighboring subnets rather than by crossing the constellation. The second level assigns one layer's experts to subnet satellites to minimize expected latency.

Theorem 1 assigns the i -th most active expert to the i -th lowest-latency satellite. Per-layer cost is \(O(I\log I + V\log V)\) for \(I\) experts and \(V\) satellites. Frequent experts sit near the gateway, as popular items do in a cache. [78, Eqs. 19–20, 29–30, 33–34] [78, §IV-C, §V] The rarely used ones can afford to be far away, which Figure A draws on the left.

專家放置（expert placement）分兩層進行。第一層把星座切成環狀的層子網，每個 MoE 層對應一組相鄰的衛星，中心有一顆閘道衛星[78, Eqs. 17–18]，讓 token 在層與層之間是跨到相鄰的子網，使路徑集中於相鄰子網之間。第二層把一層的專家指派給該子網的衛星，這是一個最小化每層期望延遲的二元指派[78, Eqs. 19–20, 29–30, 33–34]，而 Theorem 1 證明最佳解是把第 i 常被啟動的專家放到第 i 低延遲的衛星，對 \(I\) 個專家與 \(V\) 顆衛星每層的成本為 \(O(I\log I + V\log V)\)[78, §IV-C, §V]。直覺就像快取：路由器最常選的專家應該離閘道一跳，很少用到的則可以放遠一些，圖 A 左半畫的就是這個概念。

The network model under that placement is simple on purpose. Link delay adds chord propagation to transmission time \(MQ_B/R\). The hidden state has dimension \(M\), quantization \(Q_B\) bits and link rate \(R\). The paper states that propagation dominates communication.

Hidden-state dimension \(M\), quantization \(Q_B\) and slot duration remain unspecified. [78, Eq. 5] [78, Eq. 6] Queueing and per-hop processing lie outside the model [78, §II-B] . What makes the result interpretable is a check that uses only the paper's inputs.

在這個放置之下的網路模型刻意簡化。鏈路上的延遲是弦長幾何的傳播時間[78, Eq. 5]加上一個傳輸項 \(MQ_B/R\)，其中隱藏狀態維度為 \(M\)、量化為 \(Q_B\) 位元、鏈路速率為 \(R\)[78, Eq. 6]。論文說通訊由傳播主導，但 \(M\)、\(Q_B\) 與時槽長度皆仍待補充，排隊與每跳處理則位於模型範圍之外[78, §II-B]。讓結果得以解讀的是一個只用論文自身輸入的檢查。因此這個放置相對隨機基準的增益，是「每層花多少跳」上的增益，量測的基準線則是處理器所設下的下限。

The evaluation is a simulation over sampled topology snapshots, with testbeds, flight hardware and accuracy results outside its scope, over eight lm-eval-harness datasets [78, §VII] preprint.評估是在取樣的拓樸快照上進行的模擬，測試平台、飛行硬體及準確度結果皆位於範圍之外，使用八個 lm-eval-harness 資料集[78, §VII]預印本。每顆衛星最多四條雷射 ISL，兩條同軌、兩條跨軌，也就是第 2 章經典的 +Grid 度數。ISL 速率寫成「≥ 100 Gbps」，延遲模型是弦長傳播加 \(MQ_B/R\) 傳輸，排隊、交換及每跳處理皆位於模型範圍之外。只有當兩顆衛星的角速率差低於 0.12 rad/s 時鏈路才可行[78, Eq. 2]。故障模型讓每條鏈路在每個快照以機率 0.95 的獨立 Bernoulli 事件存活，所有鏈路相同且在時間上相互獨立[78, Eq. 3]。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The headline is compute-bound. Because 0.96 of the 1.02–1.07 s is the processor, the "≥ 100 Gbps" ISL is barely exercised. The paper's statement that communication is dominated by propagation holds only because the compute is so slow. On an Orin-class or H100-class payload the same placement would become propagation-bound, and Chapter 12's Section 12.5 shows where that flip happens.頭條數字受運算限制。因為 1.02–1.07 s 中有 0.96 s 是處理器，「≥ 100 Gbps」的 ISL 在評估中僅占少量時間。論文說通訊由傳播主導，只是因為運算太慢才成立。在 Orin 等級或 H100 等級的酬載上，同樣的放置會變成受傳播限制，第 12 章第 12.5 節會指出這個翻轉發生在哪裡。
  • Baselines are random placements only. Every comparison is between placements of the same model on the same constellation. Ground-side serving by a terrestrial cluster lies outside the comparison. A dense model on one satellite is also outside its scope. The time per token still requires comparison with what a ground data center would deliver to the same user.基準只有隨機放置。每項比較都是同一模型在同一星座上的各異放置，所以論文將地面端服務替代方案列於比較範圍之外，即（由地面叢集回答請求）比較，單顆衛星上的稠密模型亦位於比較範圍之外，每 token 的時間仍待與地面資料中心對同一使用者的交付時間比較。
  • Energy and orbital dynamics outside the evaluation. The evaluation reports time only. Power, eclipse, battery discharge depth and thermal duty cycling lie outside the evaluation. Xing et al. measure duty cycling on a 17 kg bus. Sustained orbital operation therefore requires energy analysis. An overlapping-author perspective preprint lists battery degradation and thermal limits, with quantification pending. [106]能源與軌道動態皆位於評估範圍之外。評估只報告時間。功耗、日蝕、電池放電深度，以及 Xing 等人在 17 kg 平台上量到的熱工作週期皆位於範圍之外，所以若要判斷星座整圈軌道的持續運作能力，仍須加入能源分析。與本文作者部分重疊的一篇觀點預印本[106]把電池老化與熱限制列為挑戰，量化結果則仍待補充。
  • An optimistic failure model and queueing excluded. The 5% independent snapshot loss exceeds a production fabric's 0.057% monthly failure rate by two orders of magnitude. Correlated outages and reacquisition require separate modeling. [65, §2.3] This occurs because it excludes temporal correlation, re-acquisition delay after loss and handover dynamics. Together with the absence of queueing, this leaves out exactly the effects that bursty all-to-all expert routing would stress.樂觀的故障模型，排隊則位於模型範圍之外。每個快照 5% 的獨立鏈路損失比生產訓練網路每月 0.057% 的鏈路故障率差兩個數量級[65, §2.3]，但從另一方向看又太溫和，因為它將時間相關性、斷線後重新擷取延遲及換手動態排除於模型之外。加上排隊也位於模型範圍之外，它恰好遺漏了突發性全對全專家路由會加壓的那些效應。
  • Internal inconsistencies. Section IV-A and Table I size a Switch Transformer with "over 70 billion parameters" and 140 GB at FP16. Experiments instead use the 6.7 B model. The abstract and Table II imply at least a threefold reduction while §VII-B says "at least a twofold". Table I lists 10 GFLOPS where the text derives 7.28 from 10.4. And the introduction orders experts ascending where Theorem 1 uses descending (fact base Part C, item 38). The safe quotation is the Table II range with the 6.7 B model.內部差異。§IV-A 與 Table I 的系統規模估算用的是「超過 700 億參數」、FP16 下 140 GB 的 Switch Transformer，而所有實驗都用 6.7 B 的模型。摘要與 Table II 暗示至少三倍的減少，§VII-B 卻說「至少兩倍」。Table I 列出 10 GFLOPS，內文則由 10.4 推得 7.28。導論把專家遞增排序，Theorem 1 則用遞減（事實庫 Part C，第 38 項）。安全的引用方式是 6.7 B 模型的 Table II 範圍。
  • Missing quantities and assumptions to update. The hidden-state dimension, its quantization and the slot duration are unspecified. Thus, the transmission term requires those inputs for recalculation. The fact base leaves ground access outside its record. Thus, the 15-second user-link reconfiguration of Chapter 3 stays outside the model. And the four-ISL, "≥ 100 Gbps", zero-setup-delay link is an assumption that still requires an operator's published fleet specification.缺少的量與需要更新的假設。隱藏狀態維度、其量化與時槽長度都說明資料待補，所以傳輸項須取得這些輸入才能重算。地面接取段位於事實庫描述範圍之外，所以第 3 章使用者鏈路每 15 秒的重新配置位於模型範圍之外。而四條 ISL、「≥ 100 Gbps」、零建立延遲的鏈路，是仍待業者公布機隊規格支持的假設。
