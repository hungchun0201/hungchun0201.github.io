1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

Public FCC orbital parameters were the basis for Handley’s 2018 routing study. He inferred a plausible laser layout and tested whether ascent, inter-satellite hops and descent could beat ground fiber. The long-distance crossover became a reference for Bhattacherjee’s agenda paper, CoNEXT motifs, Hypatia and CCR’s transit economics. Section 5.1 develops this quantitative starting point.

公開 FCC 軌道參數是 Handley 2018 路由研究的基礎。他推得一種可行雷射布局，測試上下行與星間跳躍的路徑在何種條件下勝過地面光纖。長距交叉點成為 Bhattacherjee 議程、CoNEXT motif、Hypatia 與 CCR 經濟學的參考，第 5.1 節從這個量化起點發展。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

The crossover balances faster propagation against ascent, descent and grid stretch. Orbit height fixes both the geometric cost and satellite motion.

交叉點平衡較快傳播與上下行、網格伸長，軌道高度同時決定幾何成本與衛星運動。

3. The design3. 設計

Co-moving links form two meshes, and a repeatedly realigned bridge joins them. Source routing uses predicted geometry to choose global shortest paths.

同向鏈路形成兩網格，以反覆對準的橋接相連，來源路由再用預測幾何選全域最短路徑。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The 55 ms fiber value is an ideal great-circle bound, and 76 ms is measured Internet RTT. London–Johannesburg’s comparison with 182 ms also uses the Internet as baseline.55 ms 光纖值是理想大圓下限，76 ms 是實測網際網路 RTT。倫敦–約翰尼斯堡與 182 ms 的比較也採網際網路作基準。
  • Model delay includes propagation, with queueing, switching and capacity outside its scope. Multipath assumes equal radio and laser rates. Measured bent-pipe RTT includes gateways and PoPs, with about 40 ms floor and 25–50 ms idle medians (Part C, item 58). Seychelles ISL routing reached 181 ms against a direct-ISL estimate near 40 ms [35, §7.2]. Each comparison consequently needs its measurement definition.模型計傳播，佇列、交換與容量在其範圍之外，多路徑則假設無線電與雷射等速。實測彎管 RTT 包含閘道、PoP，約有 40 ms 下限與 25–50 ms 閒置中位數（C 部第 58 項）。塞席爾 ISL 路由曾達 181 ms，直接 ISL 估計則約 40 ms [35, §7.2]，因此各項比較須附量測定義。
  • The filed layout was superseded. November 2018 moved Phase 1 to 550 km, 24×66, then August 2019 changed it to 72×22 (Part C, item 12). Shorter ground legs and changed hop geometry make crossover altitude-dependent.申報布局後來更改：2018 年 11 月第一階段改為 550 km、24×66，2019 年 8 月再改為 72×22（C 部第 12 項）。地面腿縮短、跳躍幾何改變，使交叉點依賴高度。
  • Five lasers are Handley’s inference. The 2018 filing implies four, and the current technology page states three with an undisclosed topology (Part C, items 13 and 55). The two-mesh bridge remains a design-space baseline.五雷射來自 Handley 推論，2018 申報隱含四具，目前技術頁列三具，拓樸仍待公開（C 部第 13、55 項）。雙網格橋接因此屬設計空間的基準。
  • Link-5 downtime acknowledges acquisition qualitatively. Modern terminals require 2–30 s (Part C, item 18), and later work finds even 100 ms setup raises mean delay [32, §IV-B]. Fifteen-second scheduling belongs to later measured behavior outside this historical model (Part C, item 57).鏈路 5 停用以定性方式反映擷取。現代終端需 2–30 s（C 部第 18 項），後續研究顯示 100 ms 建立也會提高平均延遲 [32, §IV-B]。十五秒排程是後來實測行為，位於本歷史模型範圍之外（C 部第 57 項）。
  • The text describes 50 ms recomputation with 200 ms look-ahead and separately reports 10 ms as feasible. These are computational workload statements. Reordering, BBR-style control, failures and load-dependent routing remain research questions [3, §5].正文描述 50 ms 重算與 200 ms 前瞻，另報告 10 ms 可行，兩者都是計算負載陳述。重排、BBR 式控制、故障與負載相依路由仍屬研究問題 [3, §5]。
