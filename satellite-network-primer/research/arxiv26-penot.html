Circular relative orbits permit denser packing than Suncatcher's elliptical pattern. Three-dimensional packing becomes useful as cluster size grows. A Clos fabric requires satellites dedicated to switching. 1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

The Suncatcher paper fixes a link rate and a lattice but leaves the network inside the cluster unspecified. This preprint addresses both satellite arrangement and network wiring for the proposed cluster. The Part III companion is van Berkel's analysis of torus and Clos bisection and latency at 8,000 nodes. That analysis leaves orbital geometry open. In comparison, this paper has the geometry and rates outside the model. Read together, the two bracket what is known about an orbital cluster fabric.Suncatcher 論文確定了鏈路速率與晶格，叢集內部網路則保留為待定，而這篇預印本是唯一處理這個缺口所引出兩個問題的學術著作：衛星該怎麼排列、該怎麼連線。它在第三部分的搭檔是 van Berkel 的分析，後者提供了 8,000 個節點規模下環面與 Clos 架構的對分頻寬與延遲數字，軌道幾何則位於模型範圍之外。本文則處理幾何，速率則位於範圍之外，兩者合讀，正好框出目前對軌道叢集網路架構所知的範圍。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Four constraints hold throughout the orbit. Pairwise separation stays at least R_min to avoid collision. All satellites remain inside radius R_max, limiting maximum separation to 2R_max. Each satellite keeps an unobstructed solar view.

The fourth constraint preserves unobstructed links to stable nearest neighbors. The objective maximizes count for (R_min, R_max). Analysis uses a Hill frame around a reference orbit with a_c = 7,028 km. [102, Fig. 2] Mean relative orbital elements have zero relative semi-major axis. All satellites therefore share the cluster center's period.

Suncatcher loses packing space in three ways. The usable ellipse occupies only part of the full disc. Its 2:1 shape requires 2R_min spacing along one axis. The lattice is square rather than hexagonal [102, Fig. 4] .

問題被表述為一個最佳化，每顆衛星在整圈軌道中都必須滿足四個限制：與其他每顆衛星至少相距 R_min 以免碰撞。留在半徑 R_max 的球內，使最大間距為 2R_max。對太陽有視線暢通的視野。並與一組穩定的最近鄰居保持視線暢通的鏈路。

目標是在給定 (R_min, R_max) 下使衛星數最大[102, Fig. 2]。分析在叢集中心的 Hill 座標系中進行，參考軌道 a_c = 7,028 km，使用平均相對軌道根數，相對半長軸為零，使每顆衛星與中心有相同週期。以這些條件衡量，Suncatcher 的基準在三方面浪費空間：可用區域是內接於球的橢圓，可用面積小於完整圓盤。因為橢圓長是高的兩倍，沿一軸的間距必須是 2R_min。

而且晶格是正方配置，相較六角配置有較大空隙[102, Fig. 4]。

3. The design3. 設計

The planar improvement rests on one property of relative motion. The three-dimensional design stacks inclined grids along-track. Staggering keeps adjacent planes R_min apart. Satellites leaving the R_max sphere at any time are removed.平面設計的改進建立在相對運動的一個性質上。當每顆衛星的相對傾角與相對離心率比值相同、節點也相同時，衛星會共用一個平面。三維設計把這種傾斜網格沿軌堆疊，彼此錯開以讓相鄰平面保持 R_min 間距，並刪去任何時刻離開 R_max 球的衛星。

Network mapping requires each link to remain unobstructed throughout the orbit. Small planar satellites satisfy this by construction relative to R_min. 3-D clusters require numerical verification ("Integer Programming Formulation"). The alternative is a VL2-like Clos of L layers in which each switching satellite has at most k simultaneous links. A Gurobi feasibility problem assigns one virtual Clos node to each satellite. Virtual edges require line of sight between their mapped pairs. [102, Eq. 7]網路的部分問的是哪些架構能放進視線圖：兩顆衛星之間的鏈路只有在整圈軌道中皆維持暢通視線時才算數。當衛星相對 R_min 很小時，平面叢集依構造即滿足此性質，三維叢集則必須數值驗證（"Integer Programming Formulation"）。替代方案是 L 層的類 VL2 Clos，每顆交換衛星最多有 k 條同時鏈路。每顆衛星透過一個僅求可行解的 Gurobi 問題被指派一個虛擬 Clos 節點，唯一的限制是僅能把虛擬邊放在具備視線的衛星對上[102, Eq. 7]。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The whole evaluation is analysis and numerical simulation, with bench and flight components outside its scope. Orbits are propagated as Keplerian motion by advancing each satellite's mean anomaly linearly in time from mean relative orbital elements. Thus, the propagation model excludes J₂ and drag. The worst-case solar model uses a circular disc of radius R_sat. Its Sun vector rotates once per orbit, about 8° from the Hill-frame z axis. [102, Eqs. 1–5]

ISL rate lies outside the model. A link is a binary line-of-sight entry that must hold over the whole orbit, with maximum range and link budget outside the model. Latency lies outside the model, only scaling exponents for mesh diameter and mean path length. Failure modeling lies outside the study.

Hardware enters through the disc approximation and port count k.

整個評估都是分析與數值模擬，實驗台與飛行部分皆位於範圍之外。軌道以克卜勒運動傳播，從平均相對軌道根數出發、讓每顆衛星的平近點角隨時間線性前進，所以傳播模型排除 J₂ 與大氣阻力。日照模型是半徑 R_sat 的最壞情況圓盤，太陽向量每圈繞一條偏離 Hill 座標系 z 軸約 8° 的軸旋轉一次[102, Eqs. 1–5]。ISL 速率位於模型範圍之外：一條鏈路就是一個必須在整圈軌道中成立的二元視線項，最大距離與鏈路預算皆位於模型範圍之外。

延遲位於模型範圍之外，只有網格直徑與平均路徑長的尺度指數。拓樸包括平面中的六角最近鄰網格、三維中的 8 最近鄰晶格，以及 L 層的類 VL2 Clos 架構，而胖樹與環面位於比較範圍之外。失效位於模型範圍之外，硬體描述則限於圓盤近似與埠數 k。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • Geometric feasibility with link performance outside its scope. The authors state that link range, data rate, acquisition and re-pointing are outside the model. Thus, a Clos edge between the two farthest satellites counts the same as one between neighbors. The Clos results are upper bounds on topological feasibility rather than designs with a bisection bandwidth.評估聚焦於幾何可行性，鏈路性能則位於範圍之外。作者說明鏈路距離、資料速率、擷取與重新指向都在模型之外，所以最遠兩顆衛星之間的 Clos 邊與相鄰衛星之間的邊算法相同，Clos 的結果是拓樸可行性的上限，對分頻寬則須另行計算。
  • Port counts far above today's terminals. The sweep allows k = 4–12 simultaneous links per switching satellite. Current Starlink satellites carry 3 laser terminals, while Suncatcher leaves terminal count unspecified. Hardware limits are therefore likely to make k the binding constraint.埠數遠高於現今的終端。掃描允許每顆交換衛星 k = 4–12 條同時鏈路，而目前的 Starlink 衛星只搭載 3 具雷射終端，Suncatcher 則終端數仍待說明，所以一旦把硬體放進模型，k 很可能就成為束縛條件。
  • Keplerian propagation. The propagation model excludes J₂ and drag. Suncatcher uses a 2:1.0037 correction to keep J₂ drift below 3 m/s/yr per km. Its Joule version budgets formation-maintenance ΔV under 30 m/s per year. Circular-lattice and 3-D-stack ΔV estimates are still needed for a propellant comparison. [36, p. 5]克卜勒傳播。傳播模型排除 J₂ 與大氣阻力，雖然 Suncatcher 需要 2:1.0037 的修正才能把 J₂ 漂移壓在每公里每年 3 m/s 以下，其 Joule 版本並把編隊維持的 ΔV 預算定為每年低於 30 m/s[36, p. 5]，而圓形軌道晶格與三維堆疊的 ΔV 皆仍待估計，所以比較兩者推進劑需求時仍須這些估計。
  • Switch satellites displace compute. At k = 12 and 367 satellites, about 45% become Clos switches (calculation table). This compute loss should accompany the reported 4.5× packing gain.交換衛星取代運算。Clos 之所以可行，是因為把叢集中相當大的比例變成交換節點，367 顆在 k = 12 時約 45%（計算表），一篇以排進更多運算為目的的論文應該把這一點與 4.5 倍的密度增益放在一起權衡。
  • Baselines and comparisons left out. Fat-tree and torus fabrics remain outside the comparison, the mesh is characterized only by exponents. The 3-D line-of-sight property must be checked numerically, so absolute hop count, diameter and bisection remain unreported for any design.遺漏的基準與比較。胖樹與環面架構位於比較範圍之外，網格只以指數描述，三維的視線性質必須數值檢查，所以各設計的絕對跳數、直徑與對分頻寬皆仍待補充。
  • What the static geometry does well. Circular relative orbits keep pairwise distances and formation-relative partner directions fixed. This avoids the 2–30 s reacquisition delay emphasized in constellation studies. The 15-second Starlink schedule is irrelevant inside such a formation. The status remains an unreviewed v2 preprint with a venue still pending.固定幾何做得好的地方。因為圓形相對軌道讓每個距離保持固定，平面叢集的鏈路在編隊內維持固定對象方向，避開了現代終端 2–30 秒的擷取延遲，這是星座文獻大多忽略的。Starlink 的 15 秒排程在這種編隊內部位於此編隊的模型範圍之外，而本文目前仍是審查狀態待確認、發表場合仍待確認的 v2 預印本。
