1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

Chapter 12 compares three inference preprints with opposing resource assumptions. CollabLLM-LEO and SpaceMoE occupy opposite ends of that comparison. SpaceMoE assumes a slow rad-hard processor and "≥ 100 Gbps" ISLs. CollabLLM-LEO assumes a fast Orin-class processor and 0.5 Gbit/s ISLs.

Thus, its whole contribution is directed at shrinking what crosses the link. The paper is also the closest of the three to the orbital edge computing tradition of Chapter 8. This occurs because the data it processes is born on a satellite, an Earth-observation image from a sensing satellite.

The study compares processing satellite-generated images through an orbital chain with downlinking raw images for ground processing. That framing places it beside Kodan and the OEC work in spirit. Its pipeline-parallel split of a transformer places it with the language-model papers in method.

第 12 章把三篇推論預印本並列，是因為它們對兩項關鍵資源做了相反的假設，而 CollabLLM-LEO 正是 SpaceMoE 的鏡像。SpaceMoE 假設慢速的抗輻射處理器配上「≥ 100 Gbps」的雷射鏈路，CollabLLM-LEO 則假設快速的 Orin 等級處理器配上 0.5 Gbit/s 的鏈路，所以它的整個貢獻都在縮小跨越鏈路的東西。這篇論文也是三篇中最接近第 8 章在軌邊緣運算傳統的一篇，因為它處理的資料誕生在衛星上，是感測衛星拍下的地球觀測影像，而它回答的問題是：這張影像應該先在軌道上由一串鄰居處理再往下送，還是下傳原始影像後在地面處理。這個框架在精神上把它放在 Kodan 與 OEC 旁邊，而它對 transformer 的管線平行拆分，則在方法上把它歸入語言模型論文之列。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

The problem is that a large model exceeds the available capacity on one satellite and a raw image is expensive to downlink. Thus, the authors split the model over several satellites and must then pay for the tensors that cross every split. Each computing satellite has 8 GB of memory [96, Tab. II] . Thus, the largest model exceeds one board's memory and therefore requires splitting.

This is the situation the paper is built for. "LLM" in the title refers to the transformer family and pipeline technique. Chapter 12 therefore treats the workload as image inference. This occurs because a vision transformer emits an activation row per image patch.

This makes its boundary tensors image-sized rather than token-sized.

問題在於大模型超出單顆衛星記憶體容量，而原始影像下傳的代價又高，所以作者把模型拆到數顆衛星上，接著就必須為跨越每個拆分點的張量付出代價。每顆運算衛星有 8 GB 記憶體[96, Tab. II]，所以最大的模型超出單板記憶體容量，必須拆分，這正是論文所設想的情境。因此標題中的「LLM」在本文指 transformer 家族與管線技術，工作負載則為視覺模型。第 12 章須保留此區分：視覺 transformer 對每個影像區塊輸出一列激活值，邊界張量依影像規模增加，語言模型的解碼張量則依 token 定義。

3. The design3. 設計

The system has three parts that fit together. The sensing satellite \(S_0\) captures an image. The chain contains \(K\) computing satellites. Satellites \(S_1 \ldots S_K\) each run one contiguous layer block.

Candidate cuts form a DAG weighted by compute and transmission time. A shortest path selects the split points. Multithreading overlaps next-image computation with transmission of the previous tensor. [96, §III-A, §V] [96, §III] Every boundary tensor passes through activation compression. A Gumbel-Sigmoid mask relaxes the keep-or-drop decision.

Training uses a straight-through estimator, sparsity loss and temperature annealing. Retained elements receive 8-bit quantization, followed by entropy coding. [96, Eqs. 1–5] [96, Eq. 6]

系統由三個互相配合的部分組成。第一是鏈：感測衛星 \(S_0\) 拍攝影像，\(K\) 顆運算衛星 \(S_1 \ldots S_K\) 各自執行一段連續的層，拆分點的選法是把每個候選切點建模為有向無環圖中的一條邊，以運算與傳輸時間為權重並取最短路徑[96, §III-A, §V]，並以多執行緒讓運算與傳輸重疊，使一顆衛星在送出上一個張量的同時處理下一張影像[96, §III]。第二是在每個邊界施加的激活值壓縮（activation compression）：先以 Gumbel-Sigmoid 遮罩將張量稀疏化，這是「保留或丟棄」決策的可微分鬆弛，以直通估計器、稀疏損失與溫度退火訓練[96, Eqs. 1–5]，再把保留下來的元素量化到 8 位元[96, Eq. 6]，最後做熵編碼。

The reason this design earns its gain is specific to its data and its baseline. Compression attacks the transmission term of a link, the bytes divided by the rate, and leaves the propagation term untouched. Thus, it pays off when tensors are large and links are slow. This is exactly the regime of image-sized vision-transformer activations on a 0.5 Gbit/s ISL.這個設計之所以有收益，與它的資料和基準密切相關。壓縮處理的是鏈路的傳輸項，也就是位元組數除以速率，傳播項則完全維持原水準，所以它在張量大、鏈路慢時才划算，而這正是影像大小的視覺 transformer 激活值跑在 0.5 Gbit/s ISL 上的情況。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The evaluation is a hybrid of measured compute and simulated network. The topology is a chain of five satellites drawn from a single-plane Walker Delta of twelve satellites. Thus, every ISL is intra-plane and the neighbors keep a fixed separation, which avoids re-pointing and handover by construction. The links enter the model through their rates.

Queueing, retransmission and link-failure models lie outside this paper. Among the three inference preprints, the fact base identifies link-failure modeling only in SpaceMoE. Flight hardware, thermal behavior and battery dynamics lie outside the evaluation.

評估是實測運算與模擬網路的混合。拓樸是從十二顆衛星的單一軌道面 Walker Delta 中取出的五顆衛星鏈，因此每條 ISL 都是同軌鏈路，相鄰衛星保持固定間距，在設計上就避開了重新指向與換手。鏈路透過其速率進入模型，事實庫將排隊、重傳與鏈路故障列於本文模型範圍之外，這與「三篇推論預印本中只有 SpaceMoE 模擬鏈路故障」的發現一致。飛行硬體、熱行為與電池動態皆位於評估範圍之外。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The baseline sends satellite data to the ground for processing. The comparison is between processing satellite-born data in orbit and downlinking it first. Ground-origin requests lie outside the comparison. Their inputs start in terrestrial data centers, requiring a separate comparison with a ground-side serving design.基準是先下傳衛星資料，地面端服務方案則屬於另一種比較範圍。比較的是「在軌處理衛星產生的資料」與「先下傳」，所以起源於地面之請求的地面端服務替代方案，位於論文比較範圍之外。在那種情況下輸入從地面資料中心開始，軌道必須和它競爭才能證明自己的價值。
  • Energy is measured as power modes, outside the model with the orbit. The 15/30/50 W timings come from laboratory Jetsons. Eclipse, battery depth of discharge and thermal throttling lie outside the model. Xing et al. observed throttling within 50 minutes on a 5 W board in orbit. The evaluation leaves joint energy and orbital dynamics open. Chapter 12 records Orin-class CubeSat flights, while sustained 50 W operation still requires flight evidence.能源評估依固定功率模式量測，與軌道動態的聯合模型則仍待建立。15/30/50 W 模式是在實驗室的 Jetson 上計時，日蝕、電池放電深度，以及 Xing 等人在軌量到 5 W 板子 50 分鐘內就降頻的熱效應皆位於範圍之外，所以評估將能源與軌道動態的聯合模型留待後續。第 12 章也指出 Orin 等級模組曾在 CubeSat 上飛行，持續 50 W 的工作週期則仍待飛行證據。
  • A gentle network. A single-plane chain keeps neighbor distances fixed. Thus, the model holds pointing and links fixed, sets setup delay to zero and excludes handovers, failures and queueing. The 0.5 Gbit/s rate is conservative against the "≥ 100 Gbps" of SpaceMoE. This makes the three papers disagree by more than 200× on what an ISL carries.溫和的網路。單一軌道面的鏈讓相鄰距離固定，所以重新指向、建立延遲、換手、鏈路故障及排隊皆位於模型範圍之外。0.5 Gbit/s 的速率相對 SpaceMoE 的「≥ 100 Gbps」相當保守，使三篇論文對 ISL 承載量的假設相差超過 200 倍。
  • Internal inconsistencies. Table II gives a 6 Gbit/s ground link, while Fig. 4 uses 0.2–0.8 Gbit/s. A delay reduction of "103%" in Fig. 12 exceeds the mathematical limit. The model names switch between ViT-B/L/H/G and ViT-Tiny/Base/Large. And the Top-k loss is 4.1% in the text against about 0.3% in Table IV (fact base Part C, item 39). The safe quotations are the 42% at 800 Mbit/s and the greater-than-20× compression.內部差異。地面鏈路在 Table II 為 6 Gbit/s、在 Fig. 4 為 0.2–0.8 Gbit/s。Fig. 12 的「降低 103%」會導致負延遲。模型名稱在 ViT-B/L/H/G 與 ViT-Tiny/Base/Large 之間切換。Top-k 損失在內文為 4.1%、在 Table IV 約為 0.3%（事實庫 Part C，第 39 項）。可安全引用的是 800 Mbit/s 時的 42% 與超過 20 倍的壓縮。
  • Vision-transformer image classification. The workload is single-pass image classification. Autoregressive decoding, KV-cache behavior and per-token latency lie outside its scope.
