If you read nothing else

Pan, Zhao and Cai measured Starlink from Victoria, British Columbia, for almost a year using owned and user-contributed dishes. Their tools included ping, traceroute, mtr, iPerf3, RIPE Atlas, the dish's gRPC interface and public looking glasses. The results describe service cells, polled access channels, gateways and tunnels that carry traffic to PoPs under AS14593 [44, §III-A, Fig. 4]. Propagation and MAC polling explain the minimum access RTT. The paper appeared at IEEE PIMRC 2023, and its cell-size and user-density estimates carry explicit rule-of-thumb qualifications. Its arXiv text provides limited numerical latency and throughput evidence.

Pan、Zhao 與 Cai 在卑詩省維多利亞持續近一年量測 Starlink，使用自有及使用者提供的終端。工具包含 ping、traceroute、mtr、iPerf3、RIPE Atlas、終端 gRPC 介面及公開 looking glass。研究重建服務格、輪詢通道、閘道，以及將流量送至 PoP 並透過 AS14593 接入網際網路的隧道[44, §III-A, Fig. 4]。傳播與 MAC 輪詢共同解釋最小存取 RTT。論文發表於 IEEE PIMRC 2023，細胞大小與使用者密度皆附經驗法則限定，arXiv 文本提供的延遲及吞吐量數值則較少。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

The paper reconstructs Starlink's ground architecture and supplies Chapter 3's terminal-to-Internet model. Li et al.'s self-driving constellation study appeared at MobiCom 2023. The Victoria group later published LENS at MMSys 2024, extending this architectural work with dishes across several PoPs, including an ISL-dependent Seychelles terminal. Its PoP census sits between Michel et al.'s earlier European observations and the later IPv6 scan. Its operator-derived cell estimates supply the primer's traceable geometry figures, while smaller community-map hexagons still require a primary source.這篇論文重建 Starlink 地面架構，是第 3 章從終端至網際網路模型的主要來源。Li 等人的自駕星座研究則發表於 MobiCom 2023。同一維多利亞團隊後續於 MMSys 2024 發表 LENS，將架構研究延伸至多個 PoP 的終端，其中包括依賴 ISL 的塞席爾終端。這份 PoP 清單銜接 Michel 等人較早的歐洲觀察與後續 IPv6 掃描。源自營運商的細胞估計提供本教材可追溯的幾何數據，社群地圖中較小的六角格尺寸則仍需原始來源。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Limited operator disclosure requires researchers to reconstruct the access architecture through external observations. The starlink-grpc-tools scripts read terminal state from gRPC, while traceroute and mtr expose private addressing between the dish, gateway and PoP. iPerf3 tests against PoP servers isolate access performance, RIPE Atlas extends user coverage, and looking glasses expose AS14593 from outside [44, §II-B]. The campaign observed a large constellation with a low-orbit serving shell [44, §II-A]. All round residential and rectangular roaming dishes were in Victoria, initially using the Seattle PoP. Figure 3 places latency above fiber, DSL and cable, near LTE and far below Intelsat GEO, with lower delays for business-plan users.

營運商公開資訊有限，研究者需從外部觀測重建存取架構。starlink-grpc-tools 透過 gRPC 讀取終端狀態，traceroute 與 mtr 揭露終端、閘道及 PoP 間的私有定址。對 PoP 伺服器執行 iPerf3 可分離存取效能，RIPE Atlas 擴展使用者涵蓋，公開 looking glass 則呈現外部所見的 AS14593[44, §II-B]。量測當時的星座已有大量衛星，由低軌道殼層提供服務[44, §II-A]。圓形住宅終端與矩形漫遊終端均位於維多利亞，起初連至西雅圖 PoP。Fig. 3 顯示其延遲高於光纖、DSL 與有線網路，接近 LTE，且遠低於 Intelsat GEO，商務用戶的延遲也低於漫遊用戶，呈現服務等級差異的早期跡象。

3. The design3. 設計

The reconstruction connects users in service cells to shared channels and gateways. Cell geometry and density follow the paper's stated rules of thumb [44, §III-A]. A SpaceX patent describes satellite polling for uplink grants, which adds MAC delay to propagation. Terminals reach gateways through one or possibly several satellites, with ISL equipment present on only part of the fleet. Gateways use private addresses unique within each PoP, and tunnels carry user traffic to AS14593 [44, §III-B, III-C]. The regional PoP inventory [44, §III-C, Fig. 4] and half-differences of consecutive minimum RTTs provide the backbone map [44, Fig. 5].

重建架構從服務格內的使用者，連到共享通道與閘道。細胞幾何及使用者密度採論文標示的經驗法則[44, §III-A]。SpaceX 專利描述衛星以輪詢授予上行權限，因此 MAC 延遲會加在傳播時間之上。終端透過一顆或可能多顆衛星連到閘道，僅部分衛星具備 ISL 設備。閘道在每個 PoP 內使用唯一的私有位址，使用者流量則經隧道抵達 AS14593[44, §III-B, III-C]。各地區 PoP 清單[44, §III-C, Fig. 4]，加上連續跳點最小 RTT 差的一半，共同構成骨幹地圖[44, Fig. 5]。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The study directly measures dishes in Victoria, served by the Seattle PoP [44, §II-B]. Traceroute and mtr reveal addressing and PoP identities, while the terminal's carrier-grade NAT range awaits independent verification. Gateway encryption requires inference from private addresses. Inspection and operator materials support the typical gateway layout and approximate cell size, while a SpaceX patent supplies the polling scheme. Figure 5 estimates one-way backbone delay from half the minimum RTT difference, assuming symmetric paths. Figure 2 compares high-performance and version-3 dishes against a PoP server, but its ambiguous wording limits quantitative throughput use.

研究直接量測由西雅圖 PoP 服務、位於維多利亞的終端[44, §II-B]。traceroute 與 mtr 提供定址及 PoP 身分觀察，終端的電信級 NAT 範圍則待獨立驗證。閘道鏈路加密，因此閘道端路徑須由私有位址推論。檢視與營運商資料支持典型閘道配置及近似細胞大小，輪詢機制則來自 SpaceX 專利。Fig. 5 將最小 RTT 差的一半作為骨幹單向延遲，前提是路徑對稱。Fig. 2 比較高效能與第三版終端對 PoP 伺服器的吞吐量，措辭模糊，因此量化引用範圍有限。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待研究的問題

  • The observations cover one city, one PoP and a small dish sample. All terminals were in Victoria behind Seattle, so Figure 3 describes regional latency. The authors also identify evolving deployment, IPv4-only coverage, stale GeoIP and gateway encryption as limits. Limited numerical performance reporting makes this primarily an architectural source.觀察涵蓋一座城市、一個 PoP 與少量終端。所有終端位於西雅圖 PoP 之後的維多利亞，因此 Fig. 3 描述的是區域延遲。作者也列出部署變動、僅涵蓋 IPv4、GeoIP 過時與閘道加密等限制。效能數值有限，因此本教材主要引用其架構結果。
  • Cell geometry, user density and gateway layout carry rule-of-thumb or typical-case qualifications. The channel-sharing scheme comes from a patent. Retain those source qualifications when setting model parameters. Per-satellite capacity requires an additional calculation.細胞幾何、使用者密度與閘道配置皆附經驗法則或典型情況的限定，通道共享機制則來自專利。設定模型參數時應保留這些來源條件，每顆衛星的容量還需另外計算。
  • The paper acknowledges possible multi-satellite paths at architecture level. Separating bent-pipe and laser-relay traffic requires dedicated measurements and satellite equipment identification. Starlink's in-orbit ISL topology remains confidential.論文在架構層級承認可能存在多衛星路徑。區分彎管與雷射中繼流量，仍需專門量測及衛星設備辨識，Starlink 在軌 ISL 拓樸也仍屬保密資訊。
  • Polling explains the paper's access-delay floor. Tanveer et al. later identified scheduled reassignment, which adds a separate mechanism for changes in observed RTT. Both help explain the gap between propagation and measured delay.輪詢解釋了本文的存取延遲下限，Tanveer 等人後續辨識的排程式重新配置，則提供實測 RTT 變動的另一項機制。兩者共同影響傳播與實測延遲之間的差距。
  • Later scans show substantial PoP growth, and SpaceX reports a larger gateway antenna inventory. The source counts and dates appear in the tables. This expansion gives every PoP-dependent latency observation a deployment-specific scope. company後續掃描顯示 PoP 大幅增加，SpaceX 也聲明閘道天線總量更大，表格列出各來源的數量與日期。這些擴建使每項依賴 PoP 的延遲觀察，都具有特定部署時期的適用範圍。company
  • Publication record: IEEE PIMRC 2023, DOI 10.1109/pimrc56721.2023.10294034. The research record uses Crossref metadata, with direct IEEE Xplore verification still pending.發表紀錄為 IEEE PIMRC 2023，DOI 10.1109/pimrc56721.2023.10294034。研究紀錄採用 Crossref 書目，IEEE Xplore 頁面仍待直接查核。
