Mind the Misleading Effects of LEO Mobility on End-to-End Congestion Control小心 LEO 移動性對端到端壅塞控制的誤導效應

ACM HotNets 2024Lai, Li, Wu, Li, Liu, Liu, Xie, Li, Liu (Tsinghua University) · paper論文 peer-reviewed (workshop)

This digest explains how scheduled capacity changes cause congestion-control overshoot or underuse. It then examines event-aware sample resets in reproducible trace replay.本導讀說明排程容量變化如何造成壅塞控制過衝或低利用率，再檢視可重現軌跡重播中的感知事件樣本重設。

Scheduled path changes can make both aggressive and conservative controllers misread their recent samples. Resetting estimates at an event provides a targeted response.

排程路徑變化會使積極與保守控制器誤讀近期樣本，在事件時重設估計提供針對性處理。

Live diagnosis and replay outcomes
#Reported values and conditions
1Lai et al. measured New Jersey and Madrid terminals at about 32/187 and 30/357 Mbps up/down, with RTTs of 59 and 27 ms.
2One three-minute capacity trace ranges from 10 to 65 Mbps [56, §3.1].
3Each of nine controllers receives over thirty two-minute live tests.
4Verus, VIVACE and BBRv1 overshoot, while CUBIC, Vegas, BBRv3, Sprout and Copa underuse capacity.
5Copa averages 20% utilization and 6.84 Mbps, and BBRv3 stays below 65% on lossy paths [56, §3.2–3.3].
6Across a month, over 80% of :12/:27/:42/:57 events change capacity over 10 Mbps, about 20% at least 40 Mbps.
7Outside those times, 90% of changes stay under 5 Mbps [56, §4, Fig. 7].
8In Mahimahi replay, event-aware BBRv1 reduces mean RTT from 60.30 to 42.11 ms, and Copa raises throughput from 6.84 to 11.93 Mbps [56, Tab. 1].
9Prototype evidence concerns replayed Starlink bent-pipe traces.

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

The paper connects unused-capacity measurements with the group’s LeoCC kernel controller, published at SIGCOMM 2025. Tanveer et al. established fifteen-second latency boundaries [13], and Mohan et al. observed them with one visible satellite [49]. Lai et al. examine the transport consequences across controller families and show benefits from event timing. SaTCP reaches a related conclusion for predicted handovers in emulation. This study instead uses measured global reconfiguration, later described as a “Path Phase Boundary” in draft-lai-ccwg-lsncc (fact base Chapter 6).

本文連結低利用率量測與團隊在 SIGCOMM 2025 發表的 LeoCC 核心控制器。Tanveer 等人建立十五秒延遲邊界 [13]，Mohan 等人在單一可見衛星下也觀察到 [49]。Lai 等人研究各控制器家族的傳輸後果，並展示事件時序的效益。SaTCP 在預測換手模擬中得到相關結論，本文則採實測全域重配，後來由 draft-lai-ccwg-lsncc 稱為「路徑相位邊界」（事實庫第 6 章）。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

The measurements connect large capacity steps to known reconfiguration boundaries. Controller behavior then depends on how stale estimates influence sending after a step.

量測把大容量階躍連到已知重配邊界，控制器行為則取決於過時估計如何影響階躍後傳送。

3. The design3. 設計

Both prototypes refresh their path model after reconfiguration. Their terminal-side detection is adequate for the experiment, with finer and remotely usable feedback needed for deployment.

兩原型都在重配後更新路徑模型，終端側偵測支援實驗，部署則需更細緻、可回傳遠端的回饋。

Event-aware sample-reset design
#Reported values and conditions
1The proposed design uses reconfiguration timing to separate scheduled path changes from congestion [56, §5].
2BBRv1 combines Kalman-filtered bandwidth and RTT estimates with a reset of pre-event samples.
3This removes the previous phase’s peak from the current path model.
4Copa similarly resets old RTT samples and uses about twenty RTTs to establish its new queueing-delay target [56, §5].
5Global events use the clock, and local events use one-second gRPC maps.
6The authors regard that granularity as coarse for production.
7LeoCC later replaces it with end-host 10 ms ICMP probing, detecting response gaps above 45 ms within tens of milliseconds.
感知事件的樣本重設設計
#數值、條件與證據
1設計利用重配時序區分排程路徑變化與壅塞。
2BBRv1 結合 Kalman 頻寬、RTT 估計，並重設事件前樣本，使舊相位峰值離開目前路徑模型。
3Copa 也重設舊 RTT，以約二十 RTT 建立新佇列延遲目標 [56, §5]。
4全域事件用時鐘，局部用每秒 gRPC 地圖，作者認為正式部署需要更細粒度。
5後續 LeoCC 改採端點每 10 ms ICMP，從超過 45 ms 回應空隙在數十毫秒內偵測。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

Live tests diagnose the problem, while recorded replay compares prototype responses under matched conditions. These two evidence layers support separate claims.

實測診斷問題，錄製重播則在相同條件比較原型，兩層證據各支持相應主張。

Evaluation layers and trace scope
#Reported values and conditions

3Prototype evaluation extends Mahimahi with recorded capacity traces, giving patched and original controllers identical conditions [56, §5].
4The observed fifteen-second structure enters through traces, with a separate constellation model unnecessary.
5Each algorithm has over thirty two-minute live tests under similar off-peak and weather conditions.
6Reconfiguration statistics use a month of capacity data.

2原型以錄製容量擴展 Mahimahi，使修補與原版面對相同條件 [56, §5]。

4每演算法在相似離峰、天氣時段有逾三十次兩分鐘實測，重配統計採一個月容量資料。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • Both prototypes are evaluated in Mahimahi replay. The −30.2% RTT and +74.4% throughput results therefore describe two algorithms under recorded conditions, with live deployment requiring further evaluation [56, §5, §7].兩個原型均在 Mahimahi 重播評估，因此 −30.2% RTT、+74.4% 吞吐量描述兩演算法的錄製條件，實際部署仍需進一步評估 [56, §5, §7]。
  • Local detection depends on one-second dish gRPC maps, while global detection uses the boundary clock. Remote senders require event feedback from a terminal-side component. LeoCC later supplies this through end-host probes and TCP ACK options.局部偵測依賴每秒天線 gRPC 地圖，全域依邊界時鐘，遠端傳送端需要終端側元件回報事件。後續 LeoCC 以端點探測與 TCP ACK 選項提供這項回饋。
  • The 10–65 Mbps range describes one three-minute trace, while 187 and 357 Mbps describe campaign download averages. Further source detail is required to connect them. Live per-controller numeric reporting covers Copa and BBRv3, with a full numeric comparison and misclassification counts left open.10–65 Mbps 描述單一三分鐘軌跡，187、357 Mbps 則是全程下載平均，兩者連結需要更多來源細節。實際逐控制器數值涵蓋 Copa、BBRv3，完整比較與誤判次數仍待提供。
  • Evidence covers Starlink bent pipes at two northern mid-latitude sites. ISL-routed and polar users, such as McMurdo, require separate measurements of scheduling and capacity variation.證據涵蓋北半球中緯度兩站的 Starlink 彎管，ISL 路由與麥克默多等極區使用者，需要各自的排程及容量變化量測。
  • The interval is attributed to SpaceX’s FCC petition and earlier measurements. Its generating algorithm, alongside scheduler and queue details, remains undisclosed (fact base Part D, item 13 and Chapter 3).間隔歸因於 SpaceX FCC 請願書與早期量測，其產生演算法與排程、佇列細節仍待公開（事實庫 Part D，第 13 項及第 3 章）。
