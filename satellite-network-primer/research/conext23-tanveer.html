1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

Earlier studies described Starlink latency distributions. Kassem et al. attributed their 2022 loss clusters to handover. Tanveer et al. connected those observations to a global fifteen-second reassignment cadence. Mohan, Lai, Izhikevich, Casparsen and Fries later used its :12/:27/:42/:57 boundaries. The arXiv copy retains a stale conference header, which can mislead venue citations.

Crossref places DOI 10.1145/3624354.3630586 in the CoNEXT 2023 Companion short-paper track. Claims therefore need that publication scope. Chapter 3 uses the scheduling result, Chapter 1 the elevation preference beside visible-satellite counts, and Chapter 7 the contrast with simulator handover policies.

早期研究描述 Starlink 延遲分布，Kassem 等人則把 2022 年的遺失叢集歸因於換手。Tanveer 等人把觀察連結到全球十五秒重配節律，Mohan、Lai、Izhikevich、Casparsen 與 Fries 後來也使用 :12/:27/:42/:57 邊界。arXiv 保留過期會議頁首，容易影響場合引用，Crossref 則把 DOI 10.1145/3624354.3630586 列於 CoNEXT 2023 Companion 短文軌，主張須連同此發表範圍解讀。第 3 章使用排程結果，第 1 章把仰角偏好與可見衛星數並列，第 7 章則對照模擬器的換手策略。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Track reconstruction combines consecutive map differences with orbital predictions. Clock alignment lets the study examine satellite choice and path delay together.

軌跡重建結合連續地圖差分與軌道預測，時鐘對齊則讓研究聯合檢視衛星選擇與路徑延遲。

Identification and measurement parameters
#Reported values and conditions
1Starlink’s serving-satellite identity and selection policy are hidden from ordinary terminal diagnostics.
2The study reconstructs identity from the gRPC obstruction map: a 123 × 123 polar image centered at pixel (62, 62).
3A 45-pixel radius spans 25° elevation at the rim to 90° at the center, with north at zero azimuth.

3. The design3. 設計

Common wall-clock boundaries support global scheduling. Selection preferences vary with elevation, azimuth and obstruction, under the northern sites’ GEO-arc geometry.

共同時鐘邊界支持全球排程，選擇偏好則隨仰角、方位與遮蔽而變，適用於北方站點的 GEO 弧幾何。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The study measures the production user-access path directly. Four bridge-mode terminals sit behind Raspberry Pis at northern mid-to-high latitudes, where GEO-arc exclusion applies. Their sites are Western Europe and the north-eastern, midwestern and north-western United States. PoP-colocated servers minimize terrestrial delay [13, §3].

Public SGP4 elements have kilometer-scale position error, and fifteen-second map sampling limits track resolution. Collection duration and latency-sample count require clarification. Text and caption also assign Fig. 2 to separate terminals, midwestern US and Europe, leaving a source inconsistency.

研究直接量測正式網路的使用者存取路徑。四支橋接終端接在 Raspberry Pi 後方，位於適用 GEO 弧排除的中高北緯，依序是西歐及美國東北、中西部、西北部。PoP 同址伺服器縮小地面延遲 [13, §3]。公開 SGP4 元素有公里級位置誤差，十五秒遮蔽圖取樣限制軌跡解析度。蒐集時長與延遲樣本數仍待說明，正文與圖說也各把 Fig. 2 指為美國中西部、歐洲終端，形成來源差異。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The four-terminal evidence concerns northern mid-latitudes, with duration and sample count awaiting clarification. GEO-arc geometry predicts other elevation preferences near the equator and in the southern hemisphere, which require separate tests. Fries et al. later found 69.3% boundary-aligned handovers at McMurdo against at least 89% in Germany, the Netherlands and Norway, establishing geographic differences.四支終端的證據限於北半球中緯度，時長與樣本數仍待說明。GEO 弧幾何預測赤道附近與南半球的仰角偏好會有差異，需另行測試。Fries 等人後來量到 McMurdo 邊界對齊換手為 69.3%，德、荷、挪至少 89%，建立了地理差異。
  • The paper establishes cadence through 20 ms probing. Casparsen et al.’s 2026 measurements use a 500 Hz sampling rate to resolve transitions. The initial rise reaches about 74 ms above the mean within 140 ms. Another rise occurs in the final 75 ms. The primer attributes that shape to their preprint. preprint本論文以 20 ms 探測建立節律。Casparsen 等人在 2026 年用 500 Hz 解析轉換：開頭 140 ms 內約高出均值 74 ms，最後 75 ms 另有上升。本教材將這項形狀歸於該預印本。preprint
  • Scheduler internals remain inferred. Round-robin allocation, GEO-arc rationale and global control fit external observations. SpaceX’s March 2024 engineering statement names per-beam “fronthaul scheduling” as a delay source, while further algorithm detail awaits publication. company排程器內部仍屬推論，輪詢分配、GEO 弧理由與全球控制模型皆用來解釋外部觀察。SpaceX 2024 年 3 月工程聲明把逐波束「前傳排程」列為延遲來源，更多演算法細節仍待公開。company
  • Identification requires ten-minute resets to keep XOR tracks separate. This bounds continuous natural-handover tracking, while dynamic time warping absorbs kilometer-scale SGP4 error rather than measuring it independently.辨識每十分鐘重置終端以分離 XOR 軌跡，因此連續追蹤自然換手的時長受限。動態時間規整吸收公里級 SGP4 誤差，獨立誤差量測仍待完成。
  • The scheduling interval measures fifteen-second reassignment. A handover’s own delay cost requires a separate measurement. Fries and Garcia later distinguish reconfiguration spikes, dish handovers and drop-front queue behavior in 2026.排程間隔衡量十五秒重新指派，換手本身的延遲成本需要另行量測。Fries 與 Garcia 在 2026 年進一步區分重配尖峰、終端換手與前端丟棄佇列。
  • This CoNEXT 2023 Companion paper describes one scheduler version. SpaceX reports later scheduling and buffering changes reducing US peak-hour median latency from 48.5 to 33 ms at the PoP. Continued elevation and azimuth preferences after those changes require testing. companyCoNEXT 2023 Companion 短文描述當時版本的排程器。SpaceX 報告後續排程、緩衝調整使美國尖峰 PoP 中位延遲從 48.5 降至 33 ms，調整後的仰角與方位偏好仍需測試。company
