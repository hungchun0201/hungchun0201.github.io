2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

The problem is that satellite networks are wired as meshes. A laser terminal points to one partner, and a satellite has few terminals. Terrestrial training clusters instead use Clos fabrics. Its bisection grows with the number of nodes.

Bisection intensity measures training floating-point operations per byte crossing the bisection. It connects compute demand to network supply. The question becomes whether the torus can deliver bytes fast enough to keep the accelerators busy.

問題在於衛星網路是以網格方式佈線，因為一具雷射終端一次只能對準一個夥伴，而每顆衛星只搭載少數幾具。訓練叢集則以 Clos 架構佈線，其對分頻寬隨節點數成長。連接兩者的量是本文所稱的對分強度，也就是一個訓練步驟中每個必須跨越對分的位元組所執行的浮點運算數，問題於是變成：環面送出位元組的速率可支撐多少加速器運算。

3. The design3. 設計

The method is a two-dimensional roofline. A conventional roofline limits throughput by peak compute or memory bandwidth times arithmetic intensity. The network version uses bisection bandwidth times bisection intensity as its second ceiling. [101, §IV-C]方法是二維的 roofline。在傳統 roofline 中，可達吞吐量是峰值運算與「記憶體頻寬乘以算術強度」兩者中較小者。這裡把同樣的建構套用到網路上，以「對分頻寬乘以對分強度」作為第二道天花板[101, §IV-C]。延遲是跳數乘以每跳延遲 D：Clos 為 log(N)·D、D = 1 µs。

Training comparisonTraining costs k = 6 FLOP per parameter per token. Each parameter's all-reduce gradient occupies about c = 12 bytes. One gradient exchange serves a global batch of about one million tokens. This gives I_bisect = (k/c)·B_tokens ≈ 500 kFLOP/B. The calculation assumes only data-parallel all-reduce traffic crosses the bisection. [101, Eqs. 2–3]
Link or data rateTable 3 gives Clos bisection ½·N·W·B_l. The 2-D torus gives 2·N^{1/2}·B_l, and the 3-D torus gives 2·N^{2/3}·B_l. Here B_l is per-link rate.
Latency comparisonLatency is hop count times per-hop delay D. Clos uses log(N)·D with D = 1 µs, giving 4 µs. The 2-D torus uses N^{1/2}·D, giving 1,800 µs at D = 20 µs. The 3-D torus uses (3/2)·N^{1/3}·D, giving 600 µs at the same delay. [101, Tab. 3]
訓練比較對分強度由訓練算術推得：每個參數每個 token 花 k = 6 FLOP，每個參數的梯度在 all-reduce 中佔約 c = 12 位元組，而約一百萬個 token 的全域批次共用一次梯度交換，所以 I_bisect = (k/c)·B_tokens ≈ 500 kFLOP/B，前提是作者明言的假設：資料平行的 all-reduce 是唯一跨越對分的流量[101, Eqs. 2–3]。
對分頻寬公式對分頻寬來自 Table 3 的拓樸公式：Clos 為 ½·N·W·B_l、二維環面為 2·N^{1/2}·B_l、三維環面為 2·N^{2/3}·B_l，其中 B_l 為每條鏈路的速率。
評估條件二維環面為 N^{1/2}·D、三維環面為 (3/2)·N^{1/3}·D，D = 20 µs。
評估條件得到 4、1,800 與 600 µs[101, Tab. 3]。

Placed on these rooflines, the two workloads separate cleanly. Inference that fits inside one satellite keeps all traffic local to its satellite. Thus, its roofline is identical to the terrestrial one [101, §IV-D, Fig. 7] . The network ceiling limits torus-distributed training.

All-reduce crosses the bisection once per dimension. Bisection bandwidth falls two to three orders of magnitude, raising training cost "100+" times. Google's proposed 10 Tb/s links form a dotted roofline. Even these Suncatcher-class links leave a network gap. [101, §IV-E, §V, Fig. 8] The paper proposes a bi-planar "Clos satellite constellation" with communication satellites switching for compute satellites.

It describes the idea and leaves analysis for later work. [101, §V]

放到這些 roofline 上，兩種工作負載清楚分開。能放進單一衛星的推論所有流量皆留在單一衛星內，所以它的 roofline 與地面完全相同[101, §IV-D, Fig. 7]。分散在環面上的訓練則受網路天花板限制，而且因為環面 all-reduce 每個維度都要跨越一次對分，結果是對分頻寬損失二到三個數量級、訓練成本提高「100 倍以上」。Google 所設想的每條自由空間光學鏈路 10 Tb/s 被畫成虛線 roofline，用來顯示即使是 Suncatcher 等級的鏈路也只能縮小、差距仍持續存在[101, §IV-E, §V, Fig. 8]。本文最後提出一種雙平面的「Clos 衛星星座」，由獨立的通訊衛星擔任運算衛星的交換節點，這個構想只被描述分析則仍待完成[101, §V]。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

This is a purely analytical paper: its evidence consists of analytical formulas, with simulation, emulation, bench measurements and flight data outside its scope. Every result comes from closed-form topology formulas placed on roofline plots. The qualitative failure model covers permanent satellite failures, rebalancing, rerouting, debris and Kessler cascades. Direction-dependent capacity complicates repair, while availability figures remain open. [101, §IV-A]這是一篇純分析的論文：證據範圍為解析公式，模擬、模擬器、實驗台量測及飛行資料皆位於範圍之外，每個結果都來自放在 roofline 圖上的封閉形式拓樸公式。失效模型是定性的：衛星可能永久失效，迫使在各方向容量各異的網路上重新平衡與重新路由，並點名太空碎片與 Kessler 連鎖效應為風險，但可用度數字仍待補充[101, §IV-A]。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The 2-D torus formula is inconsistent. The text gives 2-D bisection as N^{1/2}·B_l. Table 3 gives 2·N^{1/2}·B_l, a factor of two higher (factbase Part C, item 41). The table's 2.25 TB/s follows from its own formula. Thus, this primer uses Table 3 and notes the factor.二維環面公式差異。內文把二維環面對分頻寬寫成 N^{1/2}·B_l，而 Table 3 使用 2·N^{1/2}·B_l，相差兩倍（事實庫 Part C 第 41 項）。表中的 2.25 TB/s 由其自身公式推得，所以本教材採用 Table 3 並註明這個倍數。
  • The node is a rack on one side and a satellite on the other. The 12,880× gap compares 8,000 racks with 72 links each against 8,000 satellites with 4–6. Thus, most of the ratio is the port count. How many accelerators a satellite holds, and therefore whether 8,000 satellites equal 8,000 racks, remains unestablished.一邊的節點是機架，另一邊是衛星。12,880 倍的差距比較的是每個 72 條鏈路的 8,000 個機架，與每顆 4–6 條鏈路的 8,000 顆衛星，所以比例大多來自埠數。一顆衛星容納多少加速器、因而 8,000 顆衛星與 8,000 個機架的對應關係，仍待論證。
  • The per-hop delay is an assumption. Per-hop node delay still requires satellite-network measurements (factbase Part C, item 19). The 20 µs assumption drives the 1,800 and 600 µs results. Light-time across a 2 km cluster is only about 6.7 µs.每跳延遲是假設。衛星網路的每跳節點延遲仍待公開量測證據（事實庫 Part C 第 19 項），所以驅動 1,800 與 600 µs 數字的 20 µs 是建模選擇，而光跨越 2 km 叢集只需約 6.7 µs。
  • Orbital geometry and a Clos alternative remain open. Flight feasibility remains open for 89 × 90 and 20 × 20 × 20 tori. Stable wrap-around line of sight is "particularly challenging". Its bi-planar "Clos satellite constellation" is proposed with quantification still pending, whereas Pénot and Balakrishnan show a Clos is topologically feasible at L ≥ 3.軌道幾何與 Clos 替代方案仍待分析。本文仍待檢查 89 × 90 或 20 × 20 × 20 的環面維持穩定視線飛行的可行性，包括它自己稱為「尤其具挑戰性」的環繞鏈路。而它提出的雙平面「Clos 衛星星座」量化結果亦仍待補充，Pénot 與 Balakrishnan 則證明 Clos 在 L ≥ 3 時拓樸上可行。
  • Only data-parallel traffic crosses the bisection. Tensor- and pipeline-parallel traffic is assumed to stay local. Terrestrial tensor parallelism stays within a host. Applying this to orbit requires evidence that a satellite carries enough accelerators.只有資料平行流量跨越對分。假設張量平行與管線平行流量留在本地，這是地面上把張量平行留在主機內的做法，但仍須證據確認一顆衛星容納的加速器足以讓這個假設成立。
  • Outdated or unstated link assumptions. Four to six ISLs exceed the 3 terminals on current Starlink satellites. The 100 Gb/s laser rate is a company claim relayed by journalists. The 2–30 s acquisition delay of modern terminals and the re-pointing it implies for 32,000–48,000 beams are outside the model. The 15-second Starlink schedule lies outside the model. And failure rates remain unspecified, although Chapter 10 contrasts terrestrial clusters' 0.057% monthly link failures.過時或說明資料待補的鏈路假設。每顆衛星 4–6 條 ISL 超過目前 Starlink 衛星的 3 具終端。每具雷射 100 Gb/s 是記者轉述的公司宣稱 company。現代終端 2–30 秒的擷取延遲，以及它對 32,000–48,000 道光束重新指向的含意皆位於模型範圍之外。Starlink 的 15 秒排程位於模型範圍之外。也失效率仍待補充，雖然第 10 章對比了地面叢集每月 0.057% 的鏈路失效。
