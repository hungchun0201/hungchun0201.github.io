StarryNet: Empowering Researchers to Evaluate Futuristic Integrated Space and Terrestrial NetworksStarryNet：讓研究者能評估下一代整合式天地網路

This digest explains StarryNet’s container-based digital twin, its resource costs, and the scope of its validation. It also shows how user-selected ISL capacity affects the results.讀完後，你能說明星座的容器「數位分身」是什麼、為什麼在 4,408 顆模擬衛星上跑真實路由程式要花數分鐘建置與七台伺服器的三分之一資源，以及為什麼 StarryNet 的星間鏈路容量是你由使用者輸入。

Main result
主要結果

StarryNet runs real network stacks and optional flight hardware within a container-based constellation twin. Public inputs define geometry, while users supply the ISL capacity.

StarryNet 在容器星座分身中執行真實網路堆疊，也可接入飛行硬體，公開輸入決定幾何，ISL 容量則由使用者指定。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

StarryNet links data-based constellation models to real protocol implementations. Its later use in routing studies makes model scope and external validation central to interpreting results.

StarryNet 將資料驅動星座模型與真實協定實作連接起來，後續路由研究使用它時，模型範圍與外部驗證便成為解讀重點。

3. The design3. 設計

The observer gathers public inputs, the synchronizer computes geometry and link conditions, and the orchestrator applies them to containers. Hardware can replace an emulated node through the same interface.

觀察器蒐集公開輸入，同步器計算幾何及鏈路條件，協調器再將其套用到容器，硬體也能經相同介面替換模擬節點。

6. Limits and open questions6. 範圍與待解問題

  • ISL capacity is user-configured because Starlink lasers were "still under internal test" [40, §4.3.1, §8]. Acquisition delay and range cutoff remain unspecified. The default +Grid therefore has four permanently available lasers at the selected rate. Current Starlink has three terminals and proprietary topology [39, §6.1]. Chapter 5 treats this wiring as a legacy baseline.星間鏈路是一個由使用者填入的空格。因為 Starlink 的雷射「仍在內部測試」，所以預設容量待指定，論文也雷射建鏈延遲與 ISL 距離上限待指定[40, §4.3.1, §8]，因此每一個 StarryNet 的 ISL 研究實際評估的，都是四條永遠亮著、速率由實驗者任意輸入的 +Grid。目前的 Starlink 衛星只有三個終端且拓樸待公開[39, §6.1]，這把預設佈線放進了第 5 章的過時欄位。
  • The live comparison uses one bent-pipe terminal with trace-fitted ground rates. CDF overlap is assessed visually, with printed error pending [40, §6.2, Figs. 6–7]. The 15-second schedule governing measured variability lies outside the model [13]. Agreement therefore covers the propagation-plus-queue envelope, while temporal structure needs separate validation.擬真度的宣稱是定性且狹窄的。真實對照是單一終端在彎管路徑上、地面鏈路依追蹤資料設定，以肉眼判斷 CDF 重疊且誤差待補充[40, §6.2, Figs. 6–7]，而主導實測變動的 15 秒排程[13]列於模型範圍之外，所以吻合的是傳播加佇列的包絡，，使用者實際經歷的時間結構則需另行驗證。
  • Xeoverse reports up to 79 s per simulated second at 660 satellites, with 1,323 threads. A Linux bridge limit caps one machine near 1,023 containers [80, §V-A]. Cionca et al. find mainline Docker-CLI setup "almost 10x longer" than alternatives. A ten-hop ISL path gives "very unstable" 200–300 ms RTT. StarryNet-dev performs best using Netlink, with its root filesystem supplied separately [81] preprint.獨立的重新評估顯示速度與穩定性受限。xeoverse 量到 StarryNet 在 660 顆衛星時模擬一秒最多要 79 s，產生 1,323 個執行緒，而且因為 Linux bridge 的限制在單機上的規模上限約為 1,023 個容器[80, §V-A]，Cionca 等人則發現主線 StarryNet 以 Docker CLI 啟動比其他模擬平台「慢了近 10 倍」，一條十跳的 ISL 路徑顯示 200–300 ms 且「大幅變動」的 RTT，而以 Netlink 為基礎的 StarryNet-dev 分支表現最好，根檔案系統則需另行提供[81]preprint。
  • Cionca et al. find each existing emulator differs from real-world measurements. They also find constellation update delays can exceed fixed bounds [81] preprint. Updating thousands of tc rules every 1–3 s therefore requires timing validation. The emulated state must be checked against the state computed by the synchronizer.同一比較發現，每個現有模擬平台的結果都與真實量測存在差距，且星座更新延遲可超出固定界限[81]preprint，這直接套用了第 7 章的盲點：一個每 1–3 s 在數千個容器上更新 tc 規則的模擬平台，需要時序驗證才能確認在任一瞬間模擬出的網路處於同步器算出的狀態。
  • Public TLE position error can reach 12 km. Virtualization has limited fidelity for spectrum adaptation and dish detection, and cluster resources bound scale [40, §8]. Cite the 86–313 ms routing latencies and 51–97.5% reachability as OSPF behavior on a modeled topology.作者自己的限制說得坦白：公開 TLE 誤差可達 12 km，虛擬化重現頻譜調適或天線偵測時間等實體層行為的能力有限，規模受叢集資源限制[40, §8]。因此路由案例研究中 86–313 ms 的延遲與 51–97.5% 的可達性，應該被引用為建模拓樸上的 OSPF 行為，其證據範圍限於該模型。
  • The authors leave live-comparison error, ISL rate, per-hop delay and wall-clock cost per simulated second unspecified. Later studies measure computational cost independently.缺少的數字：真實對照的毫秒誤差、ISL 速率、每跳處理延遲，以及作者叢集每模擬秒的實際耗時都待補充，而後續論文因此自行量測這些數值。
