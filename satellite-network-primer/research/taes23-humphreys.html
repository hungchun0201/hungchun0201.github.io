1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

Chapter 3 uses this paper for Starlink user-link waveform parameters. The primer’s source search identified it as the sole peer-reviewed waveform description. SpaceX retains its proprietary air-interface specification. The paper belongs beside the laser ISL and TBIRD optical-downlink studies. Together, these studies define physical links before protocols and scheduling enter the model.

The authors study navigation and use Starlink’s dense synchronization sequences as signals of opportunity for positioning. They carefully measure timing, frequency and modulation for navigation receivers. User sharing, frame payload occupancy and error-correcting codes remain outside that scope. Those questions are picked up later by networking papers that time Starlink bursts from the user side. The primer reads the two kinds of evidence together.

第 3 章所有關於 Starlink 使用者鏈路能有多快的數字，最後都建立在這篇論文上，因為本教材的搜尋以此篇作為唯一找到的同儕審查波形描述。SpaceX 的空中介面規格仍屬專有資料。它屬於鏈路物理這組導讀，與雷射星間鏈路論文及 TBIRD 光學下行並列，因為它對衛星與使用者之間的射頻段，回答了那些論文對光學段所回答的同一類問題：在任何協定或排程器放上去之前，實體層究竟是什麼。作者研究導航，希望將 Starlink 密集的同步序列當作機會訊號來定位。因此論文依導航接收機的需求，仔細量測時序、頻率與調變。

使用者共享數量、幀內資料比例與錯誤更正碼則屬於其他研究範圍。這些問題後來由從使用者端量測 Starlink 突發時序的網路論文接手，而本教材把這兩類證據放在一起讀。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

A research dish captures the wideband signal, while Doppler changes both frequency and signal timing. Recovered periodicities provide an internal consistency check.

6. Limits and open questions6. 範圍與待解問題

  • The primer calculates a rate ceiling. The 456 Mbps ceiling assumes 4QAM user bits on all 1,020 subcarriers across 298 data symbols in Table II. Throughput, coding rate, and frame-control overhead need separate evidence. Measured physical rates of 373.3 and 430.5 Mbps reach 82–94% of this ceiling. Coding overhead could explain the difference, with direct coding measurements needed to establish its contribution.速率上限來自教材計算。每頻道 456 Mbps 是從 Table II 計算出的上限，前提是全部 298 個資料符號上的 1,020 個子載波都以 4QAM 承載使用者位元，而吞吐量、碼率及幀內控制開銷比例需要另外的證據。實測的 373.3 與 430.5 Mbps 實體層速率位於上限的 82–94%，與編碼開銷相符，但直接的編碼量測仍待補充。
  • Downlink only. The uplink waveform, the user terminal's transmit power, its gain over temperature and its element count remain outside the sources found for this primer. The fact base marks these quantities as requiring evidence before quotation. Any model of the return link therefore rests on assumptions that should be declared as such.只有下行。上行波形、使用者終端的發射功率、增益溫度比與陣元數都在本教材找到的證據範圍之外，事實庫將這些量列為引用前須補充證據的項目。因此任何回程鏈路的模型都建立在應明白宣告的假設上。
  • Per-channel and per-satellite capacity have separate scopes. Scaling the 456 Mbps ceiling requires channels per beam, beams per satellite and user scheduling within a frame. These inputs need separate evidence. That number remains contested in Chapter 3, between a 6.5 Gbps physics model and company-derived figures an order of magnitude higher.頻道容量與衛星容量各有範圍。將 456 Mbps 上限換算成衛星容量，需要每波束頻道數、每衛星波束數及幀內使用者排程，這些輸入仍需另外取得證據。該數字在第 3 章中仍有爭議，介於 6.5 Gbps 的物理模型與高出一個數量級的公司衍生數字之間。
  • Receiving hardware determines the SNR context. The clean 15 dB constellations come from a 40 dBi parabolic dish. Ordinary phased-array terminals can experience lower SNR. Lower SNR and a deliberate robustness trade-off could explain the 4QAM-heavy mix relative to the 15 dB Shannon bound.接收硬體決定了訊雜比的解讀條件。乾淨的 15 dB 星座來自一支 40 dBi 拋物面天線，一般相位陣列終端看到的訊雜比可能較低，這是觀測到以 4QAM 為主的組合讓鏈路遠低於 15 dB 所允許之 Shannon 界限的一種解釋，另一種是刻意以容量換取強健性。
  • Frame time versus schedule time. The 1.333 ms frame is the physical layer's clock, and Starlink’s global scheduler has a separate 15-second reconfiguration interval, established through other measurements. Garcia et al.'s later simulator reproduced observed loss placement with one frame of access in every four, which links the two scales in a separate study.幀時間與排程時間。1.333 ms 的幀是實體層的時鐘，Starlink 全域排程器則有獨立的 15 秒重新配置間隔，證據來自其他量測。Garcia et al. 之後的模擬器以每四幀中一幀可存取的模式重現了觀測到的遺失位置，把兩個尺度連結起來，這項結果來自另一篇研究。
