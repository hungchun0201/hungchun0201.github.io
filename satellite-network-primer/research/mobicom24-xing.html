Deciphering the Enigma of Satellite Computing with COTS Devices: Measurement and Analysis解開 COTS 裝置衛星運算之謎：量測與分析

ACM MobiCom 2024Ruolin Xing, Mengwei Xu, Ao Zhou, Yiran Zhang, Shangguang Wang (BUPT), Qing Li (Peking University), Feng Qian (USC) · paper論文 peer-reviewed

This digest explains the conductive heat path that limits commercial boards in orbit and how thermal measurements set their duty cycle. It also defines the evidence scope of the campaign’s zero single-event error count.讀完後，你能說明為什麼一塊 5 W 的單板電腦在被冰冷太空包圍的衛星裡會過熱、讀懂決定商用運算在軌工作週期的兩條熱曲線，並說出六個月運算錯誤計數為零的證據範圍。

Main result
主要結果

BUPT-1’s commercial boards show how conductive and radiative heat paths limit sustained orbital compute. Their energy and zero-error observations retain the experiment’s hardware and orbital scope.

BUPT-1 的商用板卡顯示，傳導與輻射散熱路徑會限制持續在軌運算，能源及零錯誤觀察也應依實驗硬體與軌道範圍解讀。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

The experiment compares commercial boards in BUPT-1 with a matching ground thermal structure. Controlled compute and idle periods provide the basis for temperature and energy comparisons.

The workloads are deliberately ordinary. The Pi ran stress tests on one to four cores and the SSD-MobileNetV1, YOLO-Fastest, YOLOv3 and YOLOv5-Lite detectors. The Atlas ran JPEG encoding, ResNet-50 and YOLOv3 at one or four threads across its four power levels [85, Tab. 2]. The quantity that turned out to matter is the relevant quantities are the chip and surface temperatures they produced as a function of time. 3. The design3. 設計

The Pi’s chip-to-board path limits cooling, while the Atlas transfers more heat into the satellite body. Their operating limits therefore occur at separate parts of the heat path.

Pi 的晶片至板卡熱路徑限制了冷卻，Atlas 則將較多熱傳入衛星本體，因此兩者的操作上限出現在熱路徑的各個部位。

Evidence and scopeQualitative scopeThis is a measurement paper, so its design is the experiment and the interpretation.
Power and thermal conditionsQualitative scopeThe central interpretation is a heat-path argument.
Capacity and rateQualitative scopeIn vacuum, heat leaves through conduction and radiation. Thus, the heat a chip generates must conduct through its package and mount into the satellite's structure and then radiate from the satellite's outer surface. The rate of that last step is fixed by the surface area and temperature.
Heat transfer14 · 14 mm · 82 · 15The Pi has a 14 × 14 mm die and a mount designed for airflow. Its chip reaches about 82 °C, while its mounting surface rarely exceeds 15 °C. Chip-to-board heat transfer is the bottleneck, with little body warming [85, §3.1, Fig. 3].
Power and thermal conditions52.6 · 38.5 mm · 30The Atlas module measures 52.6 × 38.5 mm and has better conduction. It keeps its chip cooler while heating the structure. Surface temperature rises over hours toward the 30 °C limit [85, §3.2, Fig. 6].
Reliability and accuracyQualitative scopeThe two boards therefore fail in opposite ways: the Pi throttles itself and the Atlas threatens the satellite.

證據與範圍定性範圍真空中的散熱依靠傳導與輻射，晶片產生的熱必須經封裝與固定座傳導進衛星結構，再從衛星外表面輻射出去，而最後這一步的速率由表面積與溫度決定。
證據與範圍14 · 14 mm · 82 · 15Raspberry Pi 的晶粒只有 14 × 14 mm，固定座是為氣流設計的，所以晶片升到約 82 °C 時，它所鎖定的表面很少超過 15 °C：瓶頸在晶片與板子之間，衛星本體的溫度變化較小[85, §3.1, Fig. 3]。
證據與範圍52.6 · 38.5 mm · 30Atlas 的模組較大（52.6 × 38.5 mm）、傳導路徑較好，自己的晶片較涼，卻把熱推進結構，所以表面溫度數小時內緩慢爬向 30 °C 的設計上限[85, §3.2, Fig. 6]。
證據與範圍定性範圍兩塊板子因此以相反的方式失敗：Pi 讓自己降頻，Atlas 威脅衛星。

A colder initial structure slows in-orbit heating and lowers device energy use. The recommendations combine task duration, mount conductivity, thermal sharing and battery-state scheduling.

較冷的初始結構使在軌升溫較慢，也降低裝置能耗。建議措施結合任務時長、固定座導熱、熱表面分工與電池狀態排程。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The spacecraft uses ground links for experiment control and data return. The ground comparison measures device power, while radiation evidence is limited to the campaign’s observed computational errors.

太空船透過地面鏈路控制實驗及回傳資料，地面對照量的是裝置功率，輻射證據則限於活動中觀察到的運算錯誤。

6. Limits and open questions6. 範圍與待解問題

  • The evidence covers Raspberry Pi and Atlas boards. Raspberry Pi 4B and Atlas 200 DK operate at 5–14 W. Peer-reviewed Jetson and GPU-class orbital thermal measurements remain pending. Chapter 8 extrapolates the 50-minute and 540-minute curves to 700 W only through Stefan–Boltzmann calculations, spanning two orders of magnitude.證據涵蓋 Raspberry Pi 與 Atlas 板卡。板子是 5–14 W 的 Raspberry Pi 4B 與 Atlas 200 DK。事實庫記載Jetson 與 GPU 級板卡的同儕審查在軌熱量測仍待完成，所以把 50 分鐘與 540 分鐘的曲線外推到 700 W 的加速器是跨越兩個數量級的跳躍，第 8 章只能靠 Stefan–Boltzmann 算術來做。
  • One satellite, twelve experiments. Two device types with two units each on a single 12U bus in a single sun-synchronous orbit over four months. The thermal numbers are properties of BUPT-1's structure and mounts as much as of the boards.一顆衛星、十二個實驗。兩種裝置各兩塊，在單一 12U 平台、單一太陽同步軌道上四個月。熱的數字既是板子的性質，也同樣是 BUPT-1 結構與固定座的性質。
  • Radiation evidence is a zero-error observation with event counts unspecified. Six-month zero-error operation agrees with Bhattacherjee et al.'s below-the-belt argument. Particle flux, upset and latch-up counts remain open. Rate comparison needs dose and event counts. Section 8.4 gives Suncatcher's proton-test reference of 1 silent-data-corruption event per 17 rad.輻射證據是零錯誤觀察，事件計數待補充。六個月的單事件錯誤計數為零令人鼓舞，也與 Bhattacherjee 等人「內帶以下」的論證一致，但粒子通量待記錄、翻轉或閂鎖計數，所以換算速率還需事件與劑量計數，才能與第 8.4 節 Suncatcher 質子測試每 17 rad 一次的靜默資料損毀數字比較。
  • Internal inconsistencies. Communication power is about 18 W in Fig. 11 and 38 W in §4.2. The claimed "2–4× slower" heating spans more than Table 3's 2.2× and 1.7×. The fact base retains both.內部差異。通訊功率在 Fig. 11 約 18 W、在 §4.2 為 38 W，而「慢 2–4 倍」的說法比 Table 3 兩列給出的 2.2 倍與 1.7 倍更寬。事實庫記錄了這兩點。
  • Link measurements remain open. Contact times and realized downlink throughput remain unspecified. Checking the 100 Mbps specification and the downlink time for 30 GB requires those measurements. The platform uses ground connectivity only.實際鏈路量測待補充。通聯時間與實際下行吞吐量仍待補充，核對 100 Mbps 規格及 30 GB 酬載資料下傳時間都需要這些量測。平台僅提供地面連線。
  • The ground baseline uses a laboratory power supply. A programmable monitor powers the replica. The 2–10% saving therefore compares devices. Satellite power-system efficiency and depth-of-discharge excursions require separate evidence.地面基準以電源供應器供電。複製品由可程式監測器供電，所以 2–10% 的節能是裝置層級的比較，衛星電力系統效率與放電深度偏移則需另外量測。
  • Guidelines await scheduler validation. Recommendations include short intermittent tasks, mount changes and a 30% DoD ceiling. A runtime enforcing them still needs flight tests. Its interaction with Kodan frame deadlines and Chapter 12 inference pipelines remains open.建議作為排程器仍待測試。短而間歇的任務、固定座改動與 30% DoD 上限都只是指引。強制執行這些規則的執行期系統仍待在軌測試，而這種工作週期如何與 Kodan 式的逐幀期限或第 12 章的多衛星推論管線互動仍是待解。
