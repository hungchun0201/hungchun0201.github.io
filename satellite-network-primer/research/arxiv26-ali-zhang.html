LEO Topology Design Under Real-World Deployment Constraints真實部署限制下的 LEO 拓樸設計

arXiv 2602.07756Muaz Ali & Beichuan Zhang (University of Arizona) · paper論文 preprint

Stable links remain feasible for a complete orbit. Using real Starlink ephemerides, the study builds shortcuts that reduce propagation delay and hop count. The comparison also shows how partial deployment can split a three-link grid into separate components.穩定鏈路在整個軌道週期內持續可行。研究以真實 Starlink 星曆建立捷徑，減少傳播延遲與跳數，並顯示部分部署如何使三鏈路網格分成多個連通分量。

If you read nothing else

Classic ISL topology papers assume a complete, symmetric constellation with static links. The existing Starlink shell is partial, uneven and changes daily. The authors filter candidate links from actual ephemerides. They construct deterministic cyclic LSL and simulated-annealing SA topologies from stable candidates.

The baseline is a +Grid adapted to existing neighbors. With three terminals per satellite, the number Starlink publishes, a 3-ISL grid splits into separate connected components on the real ephemerides. Delay includes only satellite-to-satellite propagation. Ground links, node delay and scheduling lie outside the model.

The work is a preprint.

所有經典的 ISL 拓樸論文都假設完整、對稱、鏈路靜態的星座，但真正存在的 Starlink 殼層是部分部署、分布疏密相異，而且每天持續變化。他們從這組候選鏈路建出兩種拓樸，一是稱為 LSL 的確定性循環樣式，二是稱為 SA 的模擬退火搜尋，再與一個依現有鄰居調整過的 +Grid 比較。若每顆衛星只有三個終端，也就是 Starlink 公布的數字，3-ISL 網格在真實星曆上甚至分成多個連通分量。限制在於這裡的延遲是衛星之間的純傳播時間，計算範圍排除地面段、節點延遲與排程器，而且這是一篇預印本。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

The measurement here is of geometry rather than of traffic. The authors propagate every active Shell 1 satellite through one orbital period from daily Space-Track TLEs. They then compute pairwise distances. This exposes two facts that the synthetic constellations hide.

The first is that the shell is far from the regular lattice the +Grid assumes. Thus, the "same-index satellite in the adjacent plane" that +Grid wires to may occupy an empty slot on a given day. The adapted +Grid must fall back to the nearest available neighbor, stretching paths.

這裡量的是幾何，流量則位於量測範圍之外。作者利用 Space-Track 為每顆衛星每天發布的一筆 TLE 快照，把 Shell 1 所有活躍衛星傳播一個軌道週期並計算兩兩距離，由此暴露出合成星座所掩蓋的兩件事。第一，這個殼層與 +Grid 假設的規則格點有顯著差異，所以 +Grid 要連的「相鄰軌道面上同序號的衛星」在某一天可能對應一個空席位，調整過的 +Grid 只能退而連到最近的可用鄰居，路徑因而被拉長。

3. The design3. 設計

The design has three parts, a feasibility filter, two topology constructors and an update rule. Figure A shows the filter and the LSL pattern.設計分為三部分：一個可行性過濾器、兩個拓樸建構器，以及一條更新規則。作者把這當成保守的選擇，並把暫時可行的鏈路留作後續工作[34, §3]。圖 A 畫出過濾器與 LSL 樣式。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

This trace-driven simulation evaluates geometric feasibility and propagation paths. Capacity is a max-min-fair allocation over 100 random satellite pairs. Laser acquisition time appears only as background, described as seconds to minutes depending on hardware and relative motion [34, §2], and is excluded from topology cost. This is defensible precisely because the stable-link filter means links are held for the whole orbit and only change at the daily update. The 15-second Starlink scheduler is outside the discussion. This is consistent with a satellite-to-satellite study that stays within satellite-to-satellite paths.這是一個由軌跡資料驅動的幾何模擬，評估範圍聚焦於幾何可行性與傳播路徑。容量是對 100 對隨機衛星的最大最小公平分配。雷射擷取時間只作為背景出現，描述為依硬體與相對運動而異的數秒到數分鐘[34, §2]，列於拓樸成本範圍之外。這一點站得住腳，正因為穩定鏈路過濾器意味著鏈路在整個軌道週期內持續保持，只在每日更新時才改變。Starlink 的 15 秒排程器位於論文範圍之外，對一個範圍限於衛星間路徑的衛星對衛星研究而言這是一致的。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • Preprint status. The paper is arXiv 2602.07756v1 and awaits peer review. Thus, the fact base tags every figure as preprint even though the headline numbers were checked against the text.預印本狀態。本文是 arXiv 2602.07756v1，同儕審查狀態仍待確認，所以即使主要數字已對照原文核對，事實庫仍把每個數字標記為預印本。
  • The 89.0 ms value measures one-way inter-satellite propagation. The metric averages one-way satellite-pair propagation, with zero node delay. Ground stations and bent-pipe legs lie outside its scope. Chapter 3 measures Starlink idle RTT around 40–50 ms for bent-pipe users and 100 ms for ISL-dependent islands. Each quantity requires its own path scope and delay definition.89.0 ms 量測的是衛星間單向傳播。它是對所有衛星對的單向平均，範圍排除地面站、彎管段及節點延遲。而第 3 章量到的 Starlink 閒置 RTT 下限，彎管用戶為 40 到 50 ms、依賴 ISL 的島嶼約 100 ms，比較時須保留各自的路徑範圍與延遲定義。
  • Rate is independent of range. The model holds 100 Gbps per direction constant to about 8,000 km. Chapter 2 instead shows a 10 cm aperture losing 8 dB between 2,000 and 5,000 km. The 8,000 km range also exceeds the 5,016 km line-of-sight limit in Equation 1. Thus, the range cap rather than the rate assumption is doing the work.速率固定於各種距離。到約 8,000 km 都固定為每方向 100 Gbps，這與第 2 章的鏈路預算矛盾，同一個 10 cm 孔徑在 2,000 到 5,000 km 之間會損失 8 dB。而 8,000 km 本身也超過論文自己 Eq. 1 所強制的 5,016 km 視線距離，所以真正起作用的是距離上限，速率則固定於各種距離。
  • Setup delay is sidestepped, outside the model. Links stay fixed within each full orbit. Daily incremental updates still change 1.0–1.3% of links. Each change costs the 2 to 30 s acquisition of Chapter 2 that the paper leaves outside its cost model.穩定鏈路避開週期內的建立成本。穩定鏈路在整個軌道週期內保持，去除了週期內的重組，但每日的增量更新仍會改變 1.0 到 1.3% 的鏈路，而每次改變都要付出第 2 章所說的 2 到 30 秒擷取時間，論文將其排除於成本之外。
  • The 15-second schedule and the user link are absent. This is appropriate for a satellite-to-satellite study but means the design requires additional models to establish end-to-end behavior. A topology with 8.2 hops still ends in a user link that reconfigures every 15 s.15 秒排程與用戶鏈路位於模型範圍之外，對衛星對衛星的研究來說這是合理的，但這也意味著設計須加入額外模型才能評估端到端行為。一個 8.2 跳的拓樸最後仍接到一條每 15 秒重組一次的用戶鏈路。
  • Baselines are uneven. Motif is evaluated only on synthetic 4-ISL constellations. Real-data 3-ISL grids disconnect and are excluded. With three ISLs, LSL performs worse than SA at 77–80 ms. Thus, the strongest real-data comparison is LSL and SA against an adapted +Grid only.各基準的評估範圍各異。Motif 只限於合成的 4-ISL 情境，3-ISL 網格因分成多個連通分量而在真實資料上被排除，而 LSL 自身在三條 ISL 下也比 SA 差（77 到 80 ms），所以最有力的真實資料比較只有 LSL 與 SA 對調整過的 +Grid。
  • SA's surrogates are validated by correlation. Capacity uses only 100 pairs. The paper leaves error bars for SA optimality and the 2.3× capacity ratio open.SA 的替代指標只以相關性驗證，容量數字也只用 100 對衛星，所以 SA 的最佳性與 2.3 倍的容量比在論文中的誤差範圍皆仍待補充。
