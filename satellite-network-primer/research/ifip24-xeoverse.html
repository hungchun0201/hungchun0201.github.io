Real Linux software needs simulated network time to match the wall clock. xeoverse precomputes predictable topology and updates only changed links. Its ground-link validation and ISL-path experiments cover separate parts of the model.原版 Linux 軟體需要模擬網路時間與牆鐘同步。xeoverse 預先計算可預測的拓樸，執行時只更新有變化的鏈路。地面鏈路驗證與 ISL 路徑實驗各自涵蓋模型的一部分。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

Chapter 7 arranges the tools along a fidelity–scale trade-off, and xeoverse is an attempt to sit in a corner that its predecessors left empty. Other work has since used xeoverse, including the SpaceCDN study by Bose et al. [89]. SpaceCDN simulated satellite caching on Shell 1 in xeoverse.第 7 章把工具沿著保真度與規模的取捨排列，而 xeoverse 試圖佔據前人留下的空角落。Celestial[79]則以每顆衛星一個 Firecracker microVM 追求邊緣運算的真實性，搭配簡化的鏈路圖。xeoverse 保留了 StarryNet 執行真實協定堆疊的承諾，但以 Mininet 網路命名空間取代容器，並把拓樸工作移到離線，依本教材的解讀，這讓它很適合用真實核心做壅塞控制實驗，而對其支援較有限：路由協定研究，因為它的路由是靜態的（tools_transport 筆記，推論）。此後它也成為其他研究的基礎，尤其是 Bose 等人的 SpaceCDN 研究[89]，在 xeoverse 中於 Shell 1 上模擬了衛星快取。

Model configurationHypatia [6] is an ns-3 packet simulator that runs its own protocol models. The tool scales to a full shell but runs slowly at high rates. Simulating one second of 9.2 Gbit/s TCP takes about 555 s. Thus, its transport results are all at 10 Mbit/s [80, §3–3.4] .
Constellation configurationStarryNet [40] runs real software in a Docker container per node. It reached 4,408 satellites using seven servers and 21.2 min of setup. Celestial [79] uses a Firecracker microVM per satellite and a simplified link graph. The xeoverse design instead combines real stacks, Mininet namespaces and offline topology computation. The primer therefore sees it as suitable for real-kernel congestion-control experiments. Its static routes provide limited support for routing-protocol studies (tools_transport notes, inference).
模型配置Hypatia[6]是一個 ns-3 封包模擬器，執行的是它自己的協定模型，因此能擴展到整個殼層，但在高速率下很慢：9.2 Gbit/s TCP 的一秒要花約 555 秒，這也是它的傳輸層結果全部停在 10 Mbit/s 的原因[6, §3–3.4]。
星座配置StarryNet[40]以每個節點一個 Docker 容器執行真實軟體，達到 4,408 顆衛星，但需要七台伺服器與 21.2 分鐘的建置。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

The paper's case for a new tool is a set of scaling measurements on its two baselines. Real software follows the machine's clock. Kernel retransmission timers, BBR probes and application frame clocks advance independently of network-simulation progress. Thus, an emulator that runs slower than real time hands that software a network whose delays and rates are distorted in time.論文提出新工具的理由，是對兩個基準工具所做的一組擴展性量測。真實軟體依牆鐘時間運作，因為核心的重傳計時器、BBR 的頻寬探測與應用程式的幀時鐘都隨機器時鐘前進，周圍網路模擬的進度則獨立於這些時鐘，所以一個比即時慢的模擬平台，交給這些軟體的是一個延遲與速率在時間上被扭曲的網路。

3. The design3. 設計

xeoverse rests on three ideas that all follow from the fact that orbits are predictable. The first is pre-computation. Before execution, xeoverse computes satellite positions, ground-link visibility and static Dijkstra routes for each time step. It also computes the four-ISL grid, with same-orbit-only ISLs available as an option.

The second mechanism is incremental update. Each step applies changed ISLs and ground links, then installs new routes as ordinary ip route entries. The default 1 s step can decrease to 1 ms. The third is focus. The simulator concentrates its work on links relevant to the simulated traffic [80, §III] . Each satellite, user terminal and gateway is a Mininet node.

These Linux network namespaces share the host kernel and cost less than containers or microVMs. Each link receives its propagation delay as latency. Ground-link capacity follows the Shannon formula, RF parameters, ITU Ku- and Ka-band channel models, and live OpenWeatherMap weather. These models add detail beyond the ISL model.

A terminal selects the nearest satellite or the satellite with the longest remaining visibility. In comparison, the ISL capacity is a single fixed value that the user configures [80, §III-B].

xeoverse 建立在三個想法上，而它們都源自軌道可預測這個事實。第一是預先計算：在執行開始前，就為每個時間步算好衛星位置、四條 ISL 的網格（或作為選項，只有同軌道面的 ISL）、地面鏈路可見性與靜態 Dijkstra 路由。第二是增量更新：即時迴圈在每一步只套用自上一步以來有變化的 ISL 與地面鏈路，並把新路由以一般的 ip route 項目安裝，預設步長 1 秒，可降到 1 ms。第三是聚焦：模擬器集中處理與模擬流量相關的鏈路，以減少維護其餘鏈路的成本[80, §III]。

每顆衛星、使用者終端與閘道都是一個 Mininet 節點，也就是共用主機核心的 Linux 網路命名空間，因此比容器或 microVM 便宜得多，而鏈路延遲設為各鏈路上的傳播延遲。地面鏈路的建模比 ISL 豐富：其容量由 Shannon 公式加上射頻參數、ITU Ku 與 Ka 頻段通道模型以及 OpenWeatherMap 的即時天氣得出，終端則連到最近的衛星或剩餘可見時間最長的衛星。相對地，ISL 容量是使用者設定的單一固定值[80, §III-B]。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The headline numbers are internally inconsistent. The abstract gives a 2.9× speedup over Hypatia, while the update-phase figure gives 2.3×. Pre-computation is 55 versus 162 min in Fig. 4. Section V-A instead calls it 'approximately 2 times higher' than Hypatia's. The introduction claims 5,442 satellites, while tests reach 1,584. Shell 1 has 5,346 printed ISLs, compared with 3,168 in a four-ISL grid (calculation table). The fact base records the first three as Part C item 44 and directs authors to quote both values with their locations.主要數字內部差異。摘要中對 Hypatia 的 2.9 倍加速，在更新階段的圖中變成 2.3 倍。預先計算在 Fig. 4 是 55 對 162 分鐘，在 §V-A 卻比 Hypatia「高約 2 倍」。引言宣稱的 5,442 顆衛星能力測試範圍僅達 1,584 顆。而 Shell 1 印出的 5,346 條 ISL 也與四 ISL 網格的 3,168 條鏈路存在差異（見計算表）。事實庫把前三項記為 Part C 第 44 項，並要求作者引用兩個數值及其出處。
  • Validation covers the part that uses ground links exclusively. The 2% and 16% agreement covers bent-pipe downlink throughput from one terminal. Ground-link capacity follows RF and weather models. ISL results show a 67.6% latency rise and a throughput collapse from 77.3 to 8 Mbps. Their validation requires ground truth. They depend on fixed ISL capacity, grid topology and static routing selected by the model or user. A disagreement with Hypatia on such a path is therefore a disagreement between two models. This is the shared blind spot that Cionca et al. summarise as "none of the existing satellite constellation emulators can match real-world measurements" preprint .驗證涵蓋的是僅採地面鏈路 的部分。2% 與 16% 的吻合比較的是單一終端的彎管下行吞吐量，其地面鏈路容量由射頻與天氣模型計算。而使 xeoverse 與 Hypatia 呈現差異的 ISL 路徑結果，也就是 67.6% 的延遲上升與 77.3 降到 8 Mbps 的吞吐量崩落，真實基準仍待補充，且取決於使用者或模型所選的固定 ISL 容量、網格拓樸與靜態路由。因此在這種路徑上與 Hypatia 的分歧，是兩個模型之間的分歧，也正是 Cionca 等人所總結的共同盲點：「現有的衛星星座模擬平台沒有一個能吻合真實世界的量測」預印本。
  • Outdated topology assumptions. The four-ISL grid follows the 2018 filing. Starlink satellites carry three laser terminals. The in-orbit topology remains proprietary (factbase Part C items 13 and 55). The ISLs are instantly reconfigurable, which ignores the 2–30 s laser setup time recorded for modern terminals (Part C items 18 and 56). Static Dijkstra routes fix routing behavior outside protocol convergence dynamics. This limits the tool for the routing questions of Chapter 5.過時的拓樸假設。四 ISL 網格沿用 2018 年的申請，而 Starlink 衛星搭載三個雷射終端，在軌拓樸仍屬專有資訊（事實庫 Part C 第 13 與 55 項）。ISL 被視為可瞬間重組，忽略了現代終端 2–30 秒的雷射建鏈時間（Part C 第 18 與 56 項）。靜態 Dijkstra 路由將路由協定收斂與錯誤行為排除於模型之外，限制了這個工具在第 5 章路由問題上的用途。
  • Scheduler behavior and measured ISL rate remain open. The model excludes the 15-second reconfiguration that dominates Starlink transport behavior. Thus, a congestion-control study on xeoverse must script it, as only LeoEM among the emulators does by default (Part C item 57). ISL capacity uses one value whose source remains open. SpaceX separately claims 100 Gbps sustained and 200 Gbps peak per laser, a company statement (Part C items 14 and 60). Throughput results over ISLs are therefore illustrations of dynamics, with prediction accuracy requiring separate validation.排程器行為與實測 ISL 速率仍待補充。在 Starlink 上主導傳輸協定所見現象的 15 秒重組位於模型範圍之外，所以在 xeoverse 上做壅塞控制研究必須自行加入腳本，而模擬平台中只有 LeoEM 預設具備這一點（Part C 第 57 項）。ISL 容量是一個來源仍待確認的單一數值，對照 SpaceX 宣稱的每具雷射持續 100 Gbps、峰值 200 Gbps 公司（Part C 第 14 與 60 項）。因此經 ISL 的吞吐量結果是動態的示意，預測準確度則須另行驗證。
  • Baselines measured by the authors. The xeoverse authors measured StarryNet and Hypatia on their own hardware. StarryNet's original paper used seven servers, while this benchmark uses one and excludes Celestial. The 40× comparison therefore reflects a single-machine constraint that favors the proposed design. The venue is taken from the IFIP digital-library listing rather than from the paper text. The numbers here come from the arXiv version.基準由作者自行量測。StarryNet 與 Hypatia 的執行時間由 xeoverse 作者在自己的硬體上量得，StarryNet 原論文使用七台伺服器，對照本文的單機設定，Celestial 則位於評測範圍之外，所以 40 倍這個數字是在有利於被提出設計的單機限制下比較工具。發表場合取自 IFIP 數位圖書館的列表，論文正文則須另行核對，此處數字則來自 arXiv 版本。
