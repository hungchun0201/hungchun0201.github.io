LeoCC turns the HotNets 2024 diagnosis of misleading reconfiguration signals into a Linux kernel module that works with existing Starlink infrastructure. ICMP probes to the PoP detect response gaps, after which the controller estimates bandwidth and base RTT from fresh samples [74, §4]. Live tests show throughput and delay gains over several controllers, with baseline-specific values collected in the result tables [74, §6.2, Fig. 10a]. An independent LeoEM evaluation places its goodput near SaTCP and BBRv3, while finding higher retransmissions and a gap in inter-RTT fairness reproduction preprint.

LeoCC 將 HotNets 對重組訊號誤導控制器的診斷，實作為可搭配既有 Starlink 基礎設施的 Linux 核心模組。送往 PoP 的 ICMP 探測辨識回應空隙，控制器再以事件後的新樣本估計頻寬與基礎 RTT[74, §4]。實際測試顯示相對多種控制器的吞吐量及延遲增益，結果表按基準列出數值[74, §6.2, Fig. 10a]。獨立 LeoEM 評估中，其 goodput 接近 SaTCP 與 BBRv3，同時呈現較高重傳，以及跨 RTT 公平性重現上的落差 preprint。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

LeoCC follows SaTCP [67] and the HotNets patches in Chapter 6's reconfiguration-aware designs, and appeared at SIGCOMM 2025. All treat scheduled changes as a major disturbance, but detect them at separate locations. SaTCP uses orbital elements with terminal and ground-station cooperation, the HotNets prototypes use gRPC maps and clocks, and LeoCC probes the PoP. The paper argues that the dish lacks a reconfiguration-reporting interface and second-level gRPC detection arrives late [74, §4.1.1]. For a remote sender, an eBPF program behind the dish inserts detection results into TCP ACK options [74, §5.1]. Thus both directions require user-side software, while the dish, satellites and Internet path retain their existing configuration.

第 6 章的感知重組設計依序涵蓋 SaTCP[67]、HotNets 修補及發表於 SIGCOMM 2025 的 LeoCC。三者皆將排程變動視為主要擾動，偵測位置則各有差異。SaTCP 由終端與地面站合作使用軌道根數，HotNets 原型採 gRPC 遮蔽圖與時鐘，LeoCC 則探測 PoP。論文指出天線缺少重組回報介面，秒級 gRPC 偵測也較晚到達[74, §4.1.1]。傳送端位於遠端伺服器時，終端後方主機透過 eBPF 將偵測結果寫入 TCP ACK 選項[74, §5.1]。因此雙向傳輸都需要使用者端軟體，天線、衛星與網際網路路徑則維持既有配置。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

Measurements from the three Starlink sites show rapidly changing capacity, shifting minimum RTT and random loss even below full utilization [74, §2.1, Fig. 2]. Off-peak comparisons find CUBIC, Vegas, BBRv3 and Copa underusing capacity, while BBRv1, Proteus and VIVACE build queues [74, §2.2, Fig. 3]. Within a reconfiguration interval, capacity and base RTT vary mildly, with observed RTTs forming discrete bands [74, §3, Fig. 5]. Scheduled boundaries and signal-triggered changes move the path between states, with a separate reported interval for OneWeb [74, §3, §4.1.1].

三個 Starlink 觀測點顯示容量快速變動、最小 RTT 漂移，以及利用率仍有餘裕時的隨機遺失[74, §2.1, Fig. 2]。離峰比較中，CUBIC、Vegas、BBRv3 與 Copa 的容量利用偏低，BBRv1、Proteus 與 VIVACE 則累積佇列[74, §2.2, Fig. 3]。重組區間內的容量與基礎 RTT 變化較小，實測 RTT 聚集為離散帶狀[74, §3, Fig. 5]。排程邊界與訊號觸發事件使路徑切換狀態，論文也另列 OneWeb 的重組週期[74, §3, §4.1.1]。

3. The design3. 設計

LeoCC combines detection, a network model and a rate controller [74, §3, Fig. 6]. The detector sends periodic ICMP probes to the closest reachable PoP and identifies reconfiguration when consecutive reply intervals exceed Δoutage [74, §4.1.1, Fig. 7]. Reply gaps remain visible even when every probe during an outage is lost, and one flow serves all connections on a device [74, §4.1.1, App. A]. The model assigns bandwidth and base RTT to each interval and discards samples from earlier states.

It maintains aggressive maximum-filter and moderate Kalman-filter bandwidth estimates, plus an RTT band [bRTTl, bRTTh] from a middle-pass filter [74, §4.1.2, §4.2.1]. After Startup, Dynamic Cruise uses the aggressive estimate while filtered RTT stays within the band. When RTT exceeds the upper edge by ΔRTT, it uses the moderate estimate, with BBR-style pacing gains. Reconfiguration Adaptation drains the queue with an inflight cap of 0.5·bBWm·bRTTl, refreshes the band and resumes the saved target rate [74, §4.2, Fig. 9].

A check subtracts terminal-to-PoP RTT from end-to-end RTT to identify terrestrial bottlenecks, which bypass this adaptation [74, §4.3]. The paper leaves its BBR base version unspecified. The code identifies BBRv3, and independent readers describe STARTUP re-entry at half the bandwidth-delay product preprint. That interpretation corresponds to the paper's 0.5·bBW·bRTT cap.

On wired paths, LeoCC disables reconfiguration adaptation and reaches BBR-level throughput using BBR-like control [74, §6.7, Figs. 24–25].

LeoCC 結合偵測器、網路模型與速率控制器[74, §3, Fig. 6]。偵測器定期探測最近可達的 PoP，當相鄰回應間隔超過 Δoutage 時判定重組[74, §4.1.1, Fig. 7]。即使中斷期間的探測全部遺失，回應空隙仍可辨識，而且單一探測流可供裝置上所有連線共用[74, §4.1.1, App. A]。模型為每個區間配置頻寬與基礎 RTT，丟棄較早狀態的樣本，同時保留最大值濾波的積極估計、Kalman 濾波的溫和估計，以及中通濾波得到的 RTT 帶 [bRTTl, bRTTh][74, §4.1.2, §4.2.1]。Startup 之後進入 Dynamic Cruise，濾波 RTT 位於帶內時採積極估計，超過上緣 ΔRTT 後則採溫和估計，並循環使用 BBR 式節奏增益。重組調適以 0.5·bBWm·bRTTl 限制在途量以排空佇列，再更新 RTT 帶並恢復保存的目標速率[74, §4.2, Fig. 9]。端到端 RTT 減去終端至 PoP RTT 的檢查可辨識地面瓶頸，此時略過調適[74, §4.3]。論文對 BBR 基礎版本的說明仍待補充，程式碼則標示源自 BBRv3，獨立研究將實作解讀為以約半個頻寬延遲乘積重新進入 STARTUP preprint，對應論文的 0.5·bBW·bRTT 上限。有線路徑會關閉重組調適，採類似 BBR 的控制並達到同級吞吐量[74, §6.7, Figs. 24–25]。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

The evaluation combines live Starlink tests and trace replay, reporting throughput and one-way delay distributions [74, §6.1–6.2, Fig. 10]. Eleven baseline controllers include SaTCP, which uses LeoCC's detector because its released code needs an external source of live handover times [74, §6.1]. LeoReplayer records capacity with saturating UDP and base RTT and loss with light ICMP, relying on separate protocol queues [74, §5.2, App. B]. The paper presents its recorder and player as a new tool, while the release describes a Mahimahi extension.

The recorded dataset covers terminal–server pairs in both directions [74, §6.3]. These are bent-pipe access paths, so their reconfiguration timing is observed in traces. Mazilu et al.'s independent study uses LeoEM over both bent-pipe and ISL paths preprint. Its parameters follow SaTCP's filed shell, +Grid links, link-rate cap and idealized setup, which define the scope of its topology results.

評估結合實際 Starlink 測試與軌跡重播，以分布呈現吞吐量及單向延遲[74, §6.1–6.2, Fig. 10]。十一種基準控制器包含 SaTCP，作者為它接上 LeoCC 偵測器，以提供釋出程式碼所需的實際換手時點[74, §6.1]。LeoReplayer 用飽和 UDP 記錄容量，以輕量 ICMP 記錄基礎 RTT 與遺失，前提是兩者使用獨立協定佇列[74, §5.2, App. B]。論文將錄製重播器介紹為新工具，程式碼則描述為 Mahimahi 延伸，資料集涵蓋雙向的終端與伺服器配對[74, §6.3]。這些皆為彎管存取路徑，重組時間取自軌跡觀察。Mazilu 等人的獨立研究以 LeoEM 評估彎管與 ISL 路徑 preprint，其範圍由承襲 SaTCP 的申報殼層、+Grid、速率上限及理想化建立時間界定。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待研究的問題

  • Live evidence covers residential terminals in one constellation. The authors identify mobile, aviation and maritime terminals, other constellations and more observation sites as future work [74, §8, App. E]. Detection thresholds and other parameters were tuned empirically at the measured sites [74, §5.1, Fig. 20].實際證據涵蓋單一星座的住宅終端。作者將移動、航空與海事終端、其他星座及更多觀測點列為後續研究[74, §8, App. E]。偵測門檻與其他參數均在這些觀測點上依經驗調整[74, §5.1, Fig. 20]。
  • Headline gains depend on the baseline. The result table separates each controller comparison on the same live uplink. Against a modern model-based controller, use the BBRv3 result, while delay comparisons use queue-building controllers [74, §6.2].主要增益取決於基準。結果表逐一列出同一實際上行路徑上的控制器比較。相對現代模型式控制器時，應採 BBRv3 的結果，延遲比較則以會累積佇列的控制器為基準[74, §6.2]。
  • Detection depends on measured Starlink behavior. It assumes stable ICMP access to a PoP and visible response gaps. LeoReplayer also assumes separate ICMP and TCP/UDP queues, based on an appendix awaiting peer review [74, §4.1.1, §6.5, App. B]. Operator changes to ICMP treatment could affect both.偵測依賴實測的 Starlink 行為。它假設 ICMP 可穩定探測 PoP，且回應間隔能呈現中斷。LeoReplayer 另假設 ICMP 與 TCP/UDP 各有佇列，依據來自同儕審查狀態待確認的附錄[74, §4.1.1, §6.5, App. B]。營運商調整 ICMP 處理方式時，兩者都可能受影響。
  • Repeated adaptation raises retransmission costs. Independent studies identify high retransmissions and repeated startup phases, while OrbCC also questions probing overhead preprint. The original paper reports probe and CPU costs. Retransmission comparisons rely on the independent evaluations.反覆調適會增加重傳成本。獨立研究指出較高重傳與反覆啟動，OrbCC 也質疑探測開銷 preprint。原論文報告探測與 CPU 成本，重傳比較則需參照獨立評估。
  • Fairness and deployment need broader evidence. LeoEM and the original live-flow study report different inter-RTT fairness outcomes. Published deployment evidence remains limited to the authors' testbed. The related Path Phase Boundary proposal is an expired individual IETF draft (fact base Chapter 6).公平性與部署需要更廣證據。LeoEM 與原始實際流研究的跨 RTT 公平性結果互有差異，公開部署證據仍限於作者測試床。相關「路徑相位邊界」提案是一份已過期的 IETF 個人草案（事實庫第 6 章）。
  • The evaluations have separate path scopes. LeoCC traces cover bent-pipe user access. Independent ISL results use LeoEM's legacy +Grid assumptions and idealized setup.
