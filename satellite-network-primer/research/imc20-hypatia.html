Exploring the "Internet from Space" with Hypatia用 Hypatia 探索「來自太空的網際網路」

ACM IMC 2020Kassing, Bhattacherjee, Águas, Saethre, Singla (ETH Zürich) · paper論文 peer-reviewed

Hypatia adds packets, queues and transport behavior to a geometric constellation model. Its experiments explain reactions to path changes under assumed link settings. Applying the results to Starlink requires comparison with the deployed network and its scheduler.Hypatia 在幾何星座模型中加入封包、佇列及傳輸行為，以指定鏈路設定解釋傳輸協定對路徑變化的反應。套用至 Starlink 時，須另行核對實際部署網路及排程器。

Hypatia predates Starlink service and awaits validation against it. Izhikevich et al. compared it with measured RTTs and found sustained user-observed spikes missing. Their measurement-driven model was 1.8 times more accurate on average. [35, §6.1.2, Fig. 6]這個工具早於 Starlink 開始服務，對照它的驗證仍待完成，而當 Izhikevich et al.[35, §6.1.2, Fig. 6]拿它和實測的 Starlink RTT 比較時，發現它產生的 RTT 曲線缺少真實使用者看到的持續性尖峰，而他們以量測驅動的模型平均準確 1.8 倍。

Measure or comparison參數或比較項目Values, conditions and evidence數值、條件與證據
Queue behaviorHypatia combines an ns-3 packet simulator with a Cesium visualizer. FCC filing parameters define the constellation and four static +Grid laser neighbors per satellite. Shortest paths refresh every 100 ms, and packets traverse 100-packet queues. Researchers can therefore observe TCP responses to moving topology alongside propagation delay.
Round-trip timeThe results expose network dynamics. On Kuiper's first shell, Manila–Dalian RTT ranges from 25 to 48 ms within 200 s. Starlink and Kuiper city pairs have a median four path changes per 200 s. A 16 ms shortening reorders packets enough to halve NewReno's congestion window with zero packet loss. [6, Figs. 3, 4, 8]
ThroughputTransport experiments use 10 Mbit/s. Simulating one second of a 9.2 Gbit/s TCP flow requires about 555 s of wall-clock time. Throughput results therefore demonstrate path-change effects, with laser capacity outside their evidence scope. [6, Fig. 2]
封包層模擬模型Hypatia 是一個以 ns-3 為核心的封包層模擬器，附帶 Cesium 視覺化工具，它把向 FCC 申請的星座參數讀進來，替每顆衛星接上四條靜態的 +Grid 雷射鄰居，每 100 ms 重算最短路徑，並讓真實封包穿過 100 個封包長的佇列，使研究者第一次能看見 TCP 如何對移動中的拓樸做出反應，將分析從傳播延遲曲線擴展到封包行為。
往返時間它的主要發現都關於動態：在 Kuiper 第一層殼上，Manila–Dalian 的 RTT 在 200 s 內於 25 到 48 ms 之間擺盪，Starlink 與 Kuiper 的城市對每 200 s 中位數會換四次路徑，而一次 16 ms 的路徑縮短所造成的封包重排，足以在完整保留封包的情況下把 NewReno 的壅塞視窗砍半[6, Figs. 3, 4, 8]。
吞吐量所有傳輸層實驗都跑在 10 Mbit/s，因為模擬一條 9.2 Gbit/s TCP 流的一秒鐘要花約 555 s 的實際時間[6, Fig. 2]，所以這些吞吐量數字說的是路徑變動，而適用於該模型的路徑動態，雷射容量屬於另一個問題。

The question the paper sets out to answer is what an end-to-end connection experiences while the satellites beneath it keep moving. The answer it gives is that both the path and its length change constantly. A Rio de Janeiro–St. These are the numbers that Chapter 5 uses to motivate geography-based routing. They are entirely a product of the +Grid geometry under a static elevation mask, because failures, saturation and scheduling lie outside the model.這篇論文要回答的問題是，當腳下的衛星持續移動時，一條端到端連線會經歷什麼，而它給的答案是，路徑本身與路徑長度都持續變動。這些就是第 5 章用來引出地理式路由的數字，而它們完全是 +Grid 幾何在靜態仰角遮罩下的產物，因為模型只涵蓋幾何與固定鏈路，故障、飽和及排程屬於額外機制。

3. The design3. 設計

Hypatia is a pipeline rather than a monolith, and Figure A follows it from left to right. The tool converts filed Keplerian elements into two-line element sets in the WGS72 frame and cross-checks them against pyephem. The same input file reproduces orbital positions at any instant [6, §3]. The inter-satellite graph is a +Grid with four lasers per satellite.

Two reach same-orbit neighbors, and two reach one neighbor in each adjacent orbit. It stays fixed throughout a run. This occurs because the paper explicitly leaves dynamic ISL reconfiguration out [6, §3.1]. The Cesium visualiser renders the same state as an animated globe, which is how the paper's path-change figures were produced.

Hypatia 由多個依序執行的階段組成，圖 A 由左到右跟著它走。星座以申請文件中的克卜勒根數進入，工具把它們轉成 WGS72 座標系下的兩行根數 (TLE)，並與 pyephem 交叉比對，使任何拿到同一份檔案的人都能重現任一時刻的軌道位置[6, §3]。星間圖是 +Grid，每顆衛星四條雷射，依序連到同軌道的前後衛星以及相鄰兩個軌道面各一顆鄰居，且在整個模擬過程中保持固定，因為論文明確把動態 ISL 重組排除在外[6, §3.1]。Cesium 視覺化工具把同一份狀態畫成動態地球，論文中的路徑變更圖就是這樣做出來的。

Measure or comparison參數或比較項目Values, conditions and evidence數值、條件與證據
Ground infrastructureGround stations occupy the 100 most populous cities, each with a static parabolic antenna. Minimum elevation is 25° for Starlink, 30° for Kuiper and 10° for Telesat. Handoffs preserve packets by construction. [6, §2.2, §3.2, §5.1]
Ground infrastructureEvery 100 ms a Floyd–Warshall computation in networkx produces fresh forwarding state for the whole network. The state enters the ns-3 module. Each satellite and ground station becomes a node with 100-packet link queues and a uniform user-selected link rate. [6, §3.3, §4.2]
Latency comparisonPropagation delay follows orbital geometry continuously. Forwarding state updates every 100 ms, which lets Hypatia expose packet behavior beyond a static latency calculation. Packets can follow temporarily longer paths between forwarding updates. At a 1,000 ms step, missed changes affect 6% of Kuiper pairs. At 100 ms, the fraction is 0.4%. [6, §5.3, Fig. 9]
地面設施地面站是全球人口最多的 100 個城市，每個城市有一面靜態的拋物面天線，連到最低仰角以上的衛星，Starlink 為 25°、Kuiper 為 30°、Telesat 為 10°，而換手在設計上完整保留封包[6, §2.2, §3.2, §5.1]。
星座配置每 100 ms，networkx 中的 Floyd–Warshall 計算為整個網路產生新的轉送狀態，載入 ns-3 模組，其中每顆衛星與地面站都是一個節點，鏈路上有 100 個封包的佇列，鏈路速率則由使用者選定一個均一值[6, §3.3, §4.2]。
傳播延遲Hypatia 進一步刻畫封包行為的關鍵在於，傳播延遲隨幾何連續變化，而轉送狀態只在 100 ms 的步長上更新，所以一個封包可能走在一條片刻之前還是最短、如今已繞路的路徑上，而步長的選擇確實有影響，1,000 ms 的步長會漏掉 6% Kuiper 城市對的路徑變更，100 ms 則只漏 0.4%[6, §5.3, Fig. 9]。

5. What was measured5. 量到了什麼

The claims below are grouped by what they say about the network. The right-hand column records the setup each one depends on. The transport results deserve particular care. This occurs because they are the ones most often quoted out of context. Those conclusions are mechanism statements and remain valid. In comparison, the absolute throughputs are properties of a 10 Mbit/s link that nobody believes describes a laser terminal.下列宣稱依它們對網路的陳述分組，右欄記錄每一項所依賴的設定。傳輸層結果需要尤其小心，因為它們最常被脫離脈絡引用。這些結論是關於機制的陳述，至今仍然成立，而絕對吞吐量則是一條 10 Mbit/s 鏈路的性質，其解讀範圍限於設定的鏈路容量。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待解的問題

  • The tool still requires validation against the deployed constellation. Hypatia appeared before Starlink sold a terminal. The single independent comparison found that it misses the sustained RTT spikes that at least 70% of customers see at least once a day. Thus, the measurement-driven HitchHiking model came out 1.8 times more accurate on average [35, §6.1.2, §7.2, Fig. 6]. Any statement that Hypatia was "validated against Starlink" is wrong.這個工具對所建模星座的驗證仍待完成。Hypatia 問世時 Starlink 正值終端銷售開始之前，而唯一一次獨立比較發現其預測缺少至少 70% 使用者每天至少遇到一次的持續性 RTT 尖峰，因此以量測驅動的 HitchHiking 模型平均準確 1.8 倍[35, §6.1.2, §7.2, Fig. 6]。任何說 Hypatia「已對照 Starlink 驗證」的陳述都是錯的。
  • The fixed four-laser +Grid follows a 2019 filing. Current Starlink satellites carry three terminals, and SpaceX keeps the topology proprietary. Thus, TinyLEO's baseline is a three-ISL grid [39, §6.1] . Ali and Zhang find a three-ISL grid disconnected on real ephemerides. Hypatia path-change statistics therefore describe a design-space baseline requiring separate deployment validation. This is a preprint result. [34, Tab. 2]每顆衛星四條雷射、固定的 +Grid 是 2019 年申請文件的假設，而目前的 Starlink 衛星只有三個終端，SpaceX 也拓樸仍屬專有資訊，這正是 TinyLEO 以三條 ISL 的網格作為基準的原因[39, §6.1]。Ali 與 Zhang 指出在真實星曆上三條 ISL 的網格含有互相隔離的連通分量[34, Tab. 2]preprint，所以 Hypatia 的路徑變更統計描述的是設計空間的基準，實際在軌網路需要量測驗證。
  • Laser acquisition is absent. Real terminals require 2–30 s to acquire a new partner. Bhattacharjee et al. derive tolerable setup delays of only 2.3–80.63 ms. Above these thresholds, acquisition costs exceed path-change gains. Hypatia requires dynamic-link modeling to address the topology questions in Chapter 2. The latter source is a preprint. [17, Tab. 1] [32]雷射擷取位於模型範圍之外。真實終端需要 2–30 s 才能和新夥伴建鏈[17, Tab. 1]，Bhattacharjee 等人推導出的可容忍建鏈延遲只有 2.3–80.63 ms，超過就會讓換路徑額外延遲超過換路徑的收益[32]preprint，所以一個所有雷射永遠亮著的模擬器需要加入擷取機制才能回答第 2 章提出的拓樸問題。
  • The 15-second reconfiguration at seconds :12, :27, :42 and :57 [13] lies outside the model, and ground-station handoffs are lossless by construction. Hypatia therefore produces RTT variability entirely from geometry. User measurements largely reflect the scheduler and the drop-front queue described in Chapter 6.在第 :12、:27、:42、:57 秒發生的 15 秒重組[13]位於模型範圍之外，地面站換手在設計上也完整保留封包，所以 Hypatia 顯示的 RTT 變動完全來自幾何，而使用者量到的變動主要來自排程器與第 6 章的前端丟棄佇列。
  • Transport experiments use 10 Mbit/s, four orders of magnitude below the company claim of 100 Gbps sustained per laser. Per-hop switching delay lies outside Hypatia. Other papers select 1 ms or 10 ms. Cite Hypatia throughput as an illustration of network dynamics. [32] [33]所有傳輸層結果都在 10 Mbit/s，而公司宣稱每條雷射可持續 100 Gbps company，兩者差了四個數量級，而且將每跳交換延遲列於範圍之外，其他論文則選用 1 ms 或 10 ms[32][33]。來自 Hypatia 的吞吐量數字是動態的示意，引用時應該這樣說明。
  • The radio ground-link model excludes antenna gain and interference. The ISL model excludes Doppler. Weather, mobile terminals, multipath and congestion-control hooks require additional implementation. Filings may be optimistic, and high-goodput packet simulation runs slowly. The xeoverse comparison records 266.76 s for 60 simulated seconds at 660 satellites. At 1,584 satellites, the cost is 698 s. [6, §7] Its London–San Francisco path grows from 18 to 21 hops, raising mean latency by 67.6%. Hypatia fails to reproduce that change in this comparison. [80, §V-A]作者列出的限制界定了模型範圍。射頻地面鏈路模型將天線增益與干擾列於範圍之外，ISL 模型將多普勒效應列於範圍之外。天氣、移動終端、多路徑與壅塞控制掛鉤都需要額外實作。申請文件可能過於樂觀，而逐封包模擬在高吞吐量下執行較慢[6, §7]。後來的工具量化了這種慢，xeoverse 記錄 Hypatia 在 660 顆衛星時模擬 60 s 要 266.76 s，1,584 顆時要 698 s[80, §V-A]，同一比較也發現 Hypatia 重現狀態仍待確認 xeoverse 產生的 London–San Francisco 跳數 18 變 21 所造成的 67.6% 平均延遲上升。
  • Exact reproduction requires the released code to establish ISL range cutoff, atmospheric limits and Walker phasing. RTT uncertainty intervals also remain open.精確重現需要查閱釋出的程式碼，以補齊 ISL 距離上限、大氣限制與 Walker 相位參數。RTT 數字的誤差範圍也仍待補充。
