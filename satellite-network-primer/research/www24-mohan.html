Mohan and colleagues combine M-Lab tests, RIPE Atlas probes, application sessions and controlled dishes to map Starlink performance across regions. They measure geographic latency differences, load-induced bufferbloat, asymmetric goodput and higher delay on islands that rely on distant gateways [49, §3–6, Figs. 5–16]. Aligned changes at Edinburgh and Munich persist even with a single satellite visible, supporting a global scheduling explanation. The paper appeared at the Web Conference 2024. ISL use is inferred from latency geography, while direct path identification requires further visibility.

Mohan 與同事結合 M-Lab 測試、RIPE Atlas 探針、應用測試及受控終端，建立跨區域的 Starlink 效能圖像。量測呈現地理延遲差異、負載造成的緩衝膨脹、上下行 goodput 差距，以及依賴遠方閘道的島嶼較高延遲[49, §3–6, Figs. 5–16]。愛丁堡與慕尼黑的同步變動，在僅一顆衛星可見時仍持續，支持全球排程解釋。論文發表於 Web Conference 2024，ISL 使用則由延遲地理推論，直接辨識路徑仍需更多可見資訊。

1. Where this paper sits1. 這篇論文的位置

This study tests whether earlier Belgian and British latency and bufferbloat findings extend across regions. It combines crowd-sourced tests, distributed probes and controlled dishes, linking earlier single-site work to later measurement methods. HitchHiking expands reachable endpoint coverage, while Tanveer et al. explains the global timing observed here. The primer uses this Web Conference 2024 paper for measured performance and the bent-pipe latency budget. PoP placement is central to its explanation of latency outside North America and Europe.

本研究檢驗較早比利時與英國的延遲及緩衝膨脹發現，如何延伸至其他區域。群眾測試、分散探針與受控終端，將單點研究銜接至後續量測方法。HitchHiking 擴大可達端點涵蓋，Tanveer 等人則解釋此處觀察到的全球節律。本教材以這篇 Web Conference 2024 論文建立實測效能及彎管延遲預算，PoP 位置是北美與歐洲以外延遲解釋的核心。

2. The problem, measured2. 問題，用量測說話

M-Lab identifies Starlink through AS14593 and uses major national mobile operators as terrestrial baselines. Atlas probes provide controlled cloud targets [49, §3]. Regional RTT distributions show higher delays and longer tails in some European and South American locations [49, §4, Figs. 5–7]. Goodput and TCP-inferred loss vary inversely [49, Figs. 9, 21]. The Manila PoP opening provides a before-and-after comparison: local traffic avoids the former Japan detour and latency falls sharply [49, Fig. 19]. The tables preserve the regional percentiles and measurement conditions.

M-Lab 透過 AS14593 辨識 Starlink，並以各國主要行動業者作地面基準，Atlas 探針則提供受控雲端目標[49, §3]。區域 RTT 分布顯示，部分歐洲與南美地點具有較高延遲及較長尾端[49, §4, Figs. 5–7]，goodput 與 TCP 推得的遺失率呈反向變化[49, Figs. 9, 21]。馬尼拉 PoP 啟用提供了前後比較：本地流量略過原先日本繞行後，延遲大幅下降[49, Fig. 19]。表格保留各區百分位與量測條件。

Download load raises RTT more than upload load, locating the deep queue on the downlink side [49, §4, Fig. 8]. Zoom measurements connect packet loss with additional forward-error-correction traffic and directional delay differences [49, §5, Fig. 10]. Luna gaming results place Starlink closer to cellular than wired service under the tested conditions [49, §5, Tab. 1]. These application examples illustrate mechanisms, while broader population claims require more sessions.

下載負載比上傳更明顯地拉高 RTT，將深佇列定位於下行側[49, §4, Fig. 8]。Zoom 量測將封包遺失連結至額外的前向錯誤更正流量，以及各方向的延遲差異[49, §5, Fig. 10]。Luna 遊戲在測試條件下的 Starlink 結果，較接近行動服務[49, §5, Tab. 1]。這些應用案例用於說明機制，延伸至整體使用者則需要更多測試場次。

3. The design3. 設計

The measurement layers provide complementary evidence. M-Lab gives broad coverage with coarse public-IP location, Atlas supplies controlled targets and long time series, and local dishes add detailed instrumentation. Crowd-sourced gateway and PoP locations divide RTT into terminal-to-gateway, gateway-to-PoP and PoP-to-target segments [49, §6.1, Figs. 12–13, App. D]. The resulting bent-pipe budget supplies the baseline for later idle-RTT comparisons. Each method's location, access-technology and time-resolution limits remain part of the interpretation.

各量測層提供互補證據。M-Lab 涵蓋廣，位置依公開 IP 粗略推定，Atlas 提供受控目標與長期序列，本地終端則加入詳細儀器。群眾蒐集的閘道與 PoP 位置，將 RTT 分為終端至閘道、閘道至 PoP，以及 PoP 至目標三段[49, §6.1, Figs. 12–13, App. D]。得到的彎管預算提供後續閒置 RTT 比較的基準，各方法的位置、存取技術及時間解析度限制仍需一併解讀。

4. Evaluation setup4. 實驗設定

All results come from production-network measurements. The archive, Atlas series and single application sessions cover separate periods, while the two local dishes use controlled sky views [49, §3, §6.2]. The Réunion–Frankfurt latency advantage over fiber supports an ISL inference, with satellite identities hidden [49, §6.1, Fig. 14]. Islands lacking nearby gateways require distant ground exits. Good weather, fixed terminals and one-second polling define the controlled experiment's operating and timing scope.

結果全部來自營運網路量測，檔案庫、Atlas 序列與單次應用測試涵蓋各自期間，兩支本地終端則採受控視野[49, §3, §6.2]。留尼旺至法蘭克福相對光纖的延遲優勢，支持 ISL 推論，衛星身分仍隱藏於路徑中[49, §6.1, Fig. 14]。附近閘道缺席的島嶼需要遠方地面出口，良好天氣、固定終端及一秒輪詢，則界定受控實驗的操作與時間範圍。

6. Limits and open questions6. 限制與待研究的問題

  • Broad tests and controlled sites answer separate questions. Public-IP locations and unspecified access technology limit interpretation of the crowd sample. Controlled mechanisms come from fixed dishes in good weather, so their operating scope remains local.廣泛測試與受控站點回答各自範圍的問題。公開 IP 定位及待確認的存取技術，限制群眾樣本的解讀。受控機制則來自好天氣下的固定終端，因此操作範圍維持於當地條件。
  • Geography supports indirect ISL evidence. The island comparisons reveal delay consistent with laser routing, while identities and global traffic shares require additional observations. HitchHiking later adds engineer-confirmed Nigerian routing evidence.地理提供間接 ISL 證據，島嶼比較呈現與雷射路由相符的延遲，身分及全球流量比例仍需更多觀察。HitchHiking 後續加入工程師確認的奈及利亞路由證據。
  • One-second polling and clock drift bound the scheduling evidence. Cross-site alignment locates global boundaries, while later high-frequency probes resolve the short spike shape. The authors describe their timing explanation as a theory supported by observations [49, §6.2].一秒輪詢與時鐘漂移界定了排程證據的解析度。跨站點對齊可定位全球邊界，後續高頻探測才解析短期尖峰形狀。作者將此時間解釋稱為觀察支持的理論[49, §6.2]。
  • BBR defines the congestion-control scope. The bufferbloat results describe ndt7 under that controller. Kassem et al. provides the IMC 2022 BBR–CUBIC comparison.BBR 界定了壅塞控制範圍，緩衝膨脹結果描述 ndt7 在此控制器下的行為。IMC 2022 的 BBR 與 CUBIC 比較應參閱 Kassem 等人。
  • Gateway-to-PoP source statements give a regional range and a separate lower bound. Cite their locations with the range, preserving geographic scope.閘道至 PoP 的來源陳述包含區域範圍及另一項下界。引用時應附位置並提供範圍，同時保留地理條件。
  • Later SpaceX reports use a PoP endpoint after router queue-management changes. Their dates, values and telemetry interval appear in the evidence table. Comparing them with academic cloud-target RTT requires matched endpoints and conditions. company較晚的 SpaceX 報告採用路由器佇列管理調整後的 PoP 端點，日期、數值與遙測間隔集中列於證據表。與學術雲端目標 RTT 比較時，需對齊端點及條件。company
