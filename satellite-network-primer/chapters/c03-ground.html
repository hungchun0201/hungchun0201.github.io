  • User link使用者鏈路 The radio link between a subscriber terminal and the satellite, as opposed to the gateway (feeder) link. 使用者終端與衛星之間的無線電鏈路，相對於閘道（饋線）鏈路。
  • Phased array相位陣列 A flat antenna of many elements whose relative phases steer the beam electronically, using electronic beam steering. 由許多元件組成的平板天線，以元件間的相對相位電子化地轉向波束，以電子方式控制波束。
  • OFDM frameOFDM 幀 The repeating 1.333 ms unit of the Starlink downlink: sync symbols, header, then data symbols over 1,024 subcarriers. Starlink 下行每 1.333 ms 重複一次的單位：同步符號、標頭，然後是分佈在 1,024 個子載波上的資料符號。
  • Rain fade雨衰 Extra attenuation of a Ku- or Ka-band link while rain lies on the path, seen in Starlink as a 17–27% throughput drop. 當路徑上有雨時 Ku 或 Ka 頻段鏈路的額外衰減，在 Starlink 上表現為 17–27% 的吞吐量下降。
  • Beam波束 One steerable spot of radio illumination formed by the satellite's array. A satellite forms many at once. 衛星陣列形成的一個可轉向的照射光斑。一顆衛星同時形成許多個。
  • Cell服務格 The ground area whose users are served together by a beam, roughly 24 km across in Starlink. 由同一波束一起服務的地面區域，在 Starlink 中直徑約 24 km。
  • Gateway閘道站 A ground station of several parabolic dishes that carries the satellite's traffic to and from the terrestrial network. 由數個拋物面天線組成的地面站，把衛星的流量送往地面網路並接回。
  • Point of presence (PoP)網路接入點（PoP） The site where the operator's network hands traffic to the public Internet. Every Starlink flow is tunnelled to one. 業者網路把流量交給公共網際網路的地點。每一條 Starlink 流量都被隧道送到某一個 PoP。
  • Bent pipe彎管轉送 The single-satellite path terminal → satellite → gateway in the same footprint, with ISLs excluded. 終端 → 衛星 → 同一覆蓋區內閘道的單衛星路徑，路徑排除 ISL。
  • Bufferbloat緩衝區膨脹 Latency inflation caused by a large standing queue, 2–4× the idle RTT on a loaded Starlink link. 由大型常駐佇列造成的延遲膨脹，在負載下的 Starlink 鏈路上達到閒置 RTT 的 2–4 倍。
  • Drop-front queue前端丟棄佇列 A queue that discards the oldest packet when full, rather than the arriving one. 滿載時丟棄最舊封包，保留新到封包的佇列。
  • Reconfiguration interval重新配置週期 The 15-second period on which Starlink's global scheduler reassigns satellites and resources, at seconds :12, :27, :42 and :57. Starlink 全域排程器重新指派衛星與資源的 15 秒週期，落在每分鐘的第 12、27、42、57 秒。
  • Handover換手 The moment a terminal switches from one serving satellite to another. 終端從一顆服務衛星切換到另一顆的時刻。
  • Direct-to-cell (D2C)直連手機（D2C） Service from a satellite to an unmodified mobile phone in a terrestrial cellular band. 衛星以地面行動通訊頻段直接服務原有規格的手機。

3.2Beams, cells and per-satellite capacity波束、服務格與每顆衛星的容量

A satellite divides its coverage into simultaneously steered spots. The satellite’s phased array forms simultaneous beams. Each beam illuminates a steerable ground patch as the satellite passes, then hands that patch to another satellite. The ground area whose subscribers are served together by a beam is a cell .

The smaller hexagonal cell size often quoted elsewhere requires peer-reviewed or regulatory evidence. This primer uses the documented cell size.

一顆衛星會把覆蓋區分成多個可同時控制的照射區。它自己的相位陣列同時形成許多光斑，每個光斑是一道波束，也就是衛星在飛越時固定照在一塊地面、然後交給下一顆衛星的可轉向無線電照射區。由同一道波束一起服務的地面區域是一個服務格。

經常被轉述的更小六角形服務格尺寸，仍需同儕審查或監管來源佐證。本教材採用已有記錄的服務格尺寸。

On the ground side of the satellite sit two kinds of site. The global gateway count still requires source evidence. A point of presence, or PoP, is the site where the operator's private network hands traffic to the public Internet. Every traceroute in every study shows user packets tunnelled to the PoP assigned to the terminal before they emerge. Thus, the PoP rather than the gateway is the user's logical exit.在衛星的地面一側有兩種站點。全球閘道總數仍待來源證據。網路接入點（PoP）是業者的私有網路把流量交給公共網際網路的地點，每一份研究中的每一條 traceroute 都顯示使用者封包先被隧道送到指派給該終端的 PoP 再浮出，所以使用者在邏輯上的出口位於 PoP，閘道則負責衛星與地面的接續。

The usual bent-pipe path sends terminal traffic to a satellite, then directly to a gateway within its footprint. Terrestrial fiber carries it onward to the PoP. The space segment uses groundward links. Every independent measurement finds this to be the dominant mode.

Michel et al.’s 2022 Belgian traceroutes to San Francisco, Singapore and European anchors shared European exits. This indicated groundward routing at that terminal and time [48, §3.1]. The fraction of global traffic that crosses an ISL still requires quantitative study. The Nigerian one-third is the only estimate, and it describes a region with two ground stations.

在一般情形下把這些元件串起來的路徑是彎管轉送：終端向上發射到一顆衛星，同一顆衛星直接向下發射到其覆蓋區內的閘道，閘道再經地面光纖轉送到 PoP，整條路徑經由對地鏈路完成。每一項獨立量測都發現這是主要模式。Michel 等人 2022 年從比利時到舊金山與新加坡的 traceroute 和到歐洲錨點的流量經由同樣的歐洲出口離開，顯示當時該終端採用對地轉送路徑[48, §3.1]。全球流量中跨越 ISL 的比例仍待論文量化。奈及利亞的三分之一是唯一的估計，而它描述的是一個只有兩座地面站的地區。

Latency comparisonBufferbloat is latency growth caused by a large standing bottleneck queue. Mohan et al. measure download RTT rising two- to fourfold, reaching about 400–500 ms. The 60th percentile stays near 100 ms under upload and reaches about 200 ms under download [49, §4, Fig. 8].

Capacity comparisonTen CUBIC and ten BBRv1 flows retain unequal shares. Queue capacity is about 1,400–1,600 packets and is packet-limited. Drops follow queue length rather than delay. This supports a length-based policy outside CoDel-like active queue management. The discipline is drop-front , which discards the oldest packet when the queue is full rather than the arriving one [45, §4, §6–7] .

Measurements show that Starlink's latency changes in steps on a fixed clock, alongside the effects of orbital geometry. This primer calls the 15 s period the reconfiguration interval , the cadence on which a global controller reassigns satellites, beams and resources to terminals. Mohan et al. observed simultaneous boundaries in Edinburgh and Munich, establishing a global clock. They also saw shifts with the dish restricted to one usable satellite. Thus, the boundary effect occurs even while the serving satellite stays fixed [49, §6.2, Fig. 16].量測顯示，Starlink 延遲除了受到軌道幾何影響，也會依固定時鐘呈現階躍變化。本教材把這 15 s 的週期稱為重新配置週期，即全域控制器把衛星、波束與資源重新指派給終端的節拍。Mohan 等人在愛丁堡與慕尼黑同時觀察到同樣的邊界，確立了這個時鐘是全球性的，並用一台視野被遮蔽到只剩一顆可用衛星的天線看到同樣的轉移，這排除了衛星更換是邊界效應本身的原因[49, §6.2, Fig. 16]。

The systems problem in D2C lies less in the radio than in the cellular core behind it. Liu et al. describe cellular session state at each hop between phone, radio access network and mobile core. A satellite base station leaves within minutes. Repeated registration and handover then couple the satellite operator, mobile operator and user.

MOSAIC runs radio and user planes onboard each satellite. It replaces session state with policy-embedded one-time tokens issued by the mobile operator and enforced by the SIM. In-band tokens support operation with either available or absent ISLs. This suits LEO satellites with unreliable ISLs [59, §3–6].

D2C 的系統設計重點，在於無線電背後的行動核心網路。Liu 等人指出，一個行動通訊會話以狀態的形式保存在手機、無線接取網路與行動業者核心網路之間的每一跳上，因此當基地台是一顆幾分鐘內就離開的衛星時，衛星業者、行動業者與使用者會因為一連串的重新註冊與換手而緊密耦合。他們的設計 MOSAIC 讓每顆衛星自成一體：在星上執行無線電與使用者平面，並以行動業者簽發、由 SIM 卡強制執行、內嵌政策的一次性權杖取代逐會話的狀態，權杖以頻內方式傳遞，所以這個設計在具備或缺少 ISL 的情境下皆能運作，這點很重要，因為許多 LEO 衛星的 ISL 可靠度有限[59, §3–6]。

The two downlinks close the chapter on the same lesson as its first sections. For a dish, phone or orbital camera, spectrum and SINR on one radio hop set the available rate. A phone shares 5 MHz near 0 dB and obtains a few Mbps. A dish uses a 240 MHz channel and obtains hundreds.

Ground infrastructure and routing shape latency alongside propagation. Subscribers depend on their assigned PoP. Imaging satellites depend on the next visible ground station.

這兩種下行以本章開頭幾節同樣的教訓作結。端點涵蓋 Starlink 天線、手機及軌道上的相機時，速率都由單一無線電跳上的頻譜與 SINR 決定，這就是為什麼在 0 dB 下共用 5 MHz 的手機只得到每秒幾 Mb，而在 240 MHz 頻道上的天線能得到數百 Mb。而延遲主要受最近地面設施的位置及導流方式影響，對用戶而言那是 PoP，對成像衛星而言則是下一個可見的地面站。
