Recall
回顧
Section 1.1 derives a 97.73 min period at 650 km from Kepler’s relation. Section 1.3 introduces J₂-driven nodal precession.
第 1.1 節由克卜勒關係給出 650 km 的軌道週期為 97.73 分鐘，第 1.3 節則介紹 J₂ 是節點進動的成因。

Free orbital motion permits a cluster with separations of roughly a hundred meters. Formation flying controls satellites so their relative positions follow a prescribed, repeating geometry. Each path in a reference satellite’s frame is its relative orbit. Suncatcher starts with Hill–Clohessy–Wiltshire (HCW) equations about a circular reference orbit [36, p. 7]. These linearized relative-motion equations originated in rendezvous guidance (Clohessy & Wiltshire, J. Aerospace Sci. 27(9), 1960).

The coordinates are \(x\) radially, \(y\) along track and \(z\) across track. Mean reference-orbit motion is \(n = 2\pi/T\). Burnett and Schaub give the unforced equations as follows [103] preprint.

一群相距一百公尺的衛星之所以可能，是因為軌道力學的一項性質，可由自由軌道運動形成。編隊飛行是控制多顆衛星使其相對位置遵循預先設定、週期重複之幾何的做法，而每顆衛星在參考衛星座標系中遵循的幾何稱為它的相對軌道。Suncatcher 論文自稱以之為解析起點的[36, p. 7]，是 Hill–Clohessy–Wiltshire（HCW）方程，即繞圓形參考軌道之相對運動的線性化方程，最初為會合導引而發表（Clohessy & Wiltshire，J. Aerospace Sci. 27(9)，1960）。以 \(x\) 表徑向偏移、\(y\) 表沿軌偏移、\(z\) 表橫軌偏移、\(n = 2\pi/T\) 表參考軌道的平均運動，依 Burnett 與 Schaub[103]preprint 的寫法，自由運動的的方程為

\[ \ddot x - 2n\dot y - 3n^2 x = 0,\qquad \ddot y + 2n\dot x = 0,\qquad \ddot z + n^2 z = 0 . \]

The first two equations couple radial and along-track motion through the Coriolis terms \(2n\dot y\) and \(2n\dot x\). In comparison, the cross-track motion is an independent oscillation at the orbital frequency. The along-track equation integrates once to \(\dot y + 2nx = c\). The constant \(c\) is the drift rate. A radial offset with zero compensating along-track velocity changes the satellite’s orbital period relative to the reference.

Separation therefore grows linearly with time. Bounded motion requires \(\dot y_0 = -2n x_0\). The in-plane solution is then \(x(t) = A_0\cos(nt+\alpha)\) and \(y(t) = -2A_0\sin(nt+\alpha) + y_{\rm off}\) [103] preprint. This closed ellipse has along-track semi-axis \(2A_0\), twice radial semi-axis \(A_0\). Suncatcher describes motion “±R prograde, ±R/2 in altitude” relative to S0. Apoapsis occurs at three twelfths and periapsis at nine twelfths of an orbit [36, pp. 4–5].

The equations supply the factor two.

Pénot and Balakrishnan therefore describe the same orbits as ellipses of eccentricity \(\sqrt{3}/2\), since \(b/a = \sqrt{1 - 3/4} = 1/2\) [102] preprint.

前兩式透過柯氏項 \(2n\dot y\) 與 \(2n\dot x\) 耦合徑向與沿軌運動，橫軌運動則是以軌道頻率振盪的獨立項。沿軌方程積分一次得 \(\dot y + 2nx = c\)，常數 \(c\) 就是漂移率：具有徑向偏移、補償性沿軌速度為零的衛星，其軌道週期與參考軌道略有差異，會隨時間線性分離。因此有界運動需要初始條件 \(\dot y_0 = -2n x_0\)，在此條件下面內解為 \(x(t) = A_0\cos(nt+\alpha)\) 與 \(y(t) = -2A_0\sin(nt+\alpha) + y_{\rm off}\)[103]preprint，是一個封閉橢圓，其沿軌半軸 \(2A_0\) 恰為徑向半軸 \(A_0\) 的兩倍。這正是 Suncatcher 論文所述的 2:1 橢圓：每顆外圍衛星相對中央衛星 S0 做「順行方向 ±R、高度方向 ±R/2」的運動，在十二分之三圈時到達遠地點、十二分之九圈時到達近地點[36, pp. 4–5]。這個因子 2 來自方程本身，所以 Pénot 與 Balakrishnan 能把同樣的軌道描述為離心率 \(\sqrt{3}/2\) 的橢圓，因為 \(b/a = \sqrt{1 - 3/4} = 1/2\)[102]preprint。

The HCW solution rotates and stretches the square phase pattern. This gives the formation a repeated shape cycle and makes direct and diagonal link distances vary predictably.

HCW 解使方形相位圖樣旋轉並拉伸，形成重複的形狀週期，直接與對角鏈路的距離也隨之規律改變。

Circular relative orbits remove this stretch. Each cross-track oscillation is in phase with radial motion and \(\sqrt3\) times larger. Their relative-element condition is \(i_d = \sqrt3\, e_d\). Adding this cross-track term to Section 11.2’s HCW solution gives the following result.

他們的替代方案以圓形相對軌道直接消除拉伸。若給每顆衛星一個與徑向振盪同相、幅度為其 \(\sqrt3\) 倍的橫軌振盪，也就是他們相對軌道根數記法中的條件 \(i_d = \sqrt3\, e_d\)，則打開橫軌項後第 11.2 節的 HCW 解變成

\[ x = A_0\cos(nt+\alpha),\quad y = -2A_0\sin(nt+\alpha),\quad z = \sqrt3\,A_0\cos(nt+\alpha)\ \ \Rightarrow\ \ x^2+y^2+z^2 = 4A_0^2 , \]
