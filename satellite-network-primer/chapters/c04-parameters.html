4.1Why a parameter sheet, and how to read the tags為什麼需要參數表，以及如何閱讀標籤

Section 1.4 ties paper geometry to its frozen FCC filing. Section 2.3 traces per-laser rates to company statements relayed by journalists. Section 3.4 presents independent deployment evidence from measurement campaigns begun in 2022.
第 1.4 節說明了論文中的「Starlink」幾何其實是該論文所凍結的某一份 FCC 申報，第 2.3 節說明了每一個單雷射速率都是記者轉述的公司說法，而第 3.4 節說明了關於真實網路的唯一獨立事實，來自 2022 年起的量測活動。

The first three chapters introduce each quantity alongside its physical or measurement basis. Simulation, emulation and analysis require these values together. A parameter sheet is a dated table of the constants supporting an evaluation. It includes shell geometry, laser rates, node delays, scheduler period and queue size.

Each value carries its source document and date. Terrestrial networking papers rarely need such a table because the hardware they model has data sheets. Satellite papers use three source classes written for separate purposes. Their definitions of Starlink can differ.

Thus, the sheet has to carry provenance as a column rather than as a footnote.

前三章一次產生一個數字，每個數字都出現在解釋其物理或量測的那個句子裡，而現在想要模擬、仿真或分析 LEO 網路的讀者需要同時拿到全部。參數表就是這樣的集合：一份附日期的表，列出評估所依賴的每個常數，從殼層的高度與軌道面數、到單雷射速率、節點延遲、排程器週期與佇列大小，每個數值都附上它所出自的文件以及該文件的日期。地面網路論文很少需要這種表，因為它們所模擬的硬體有規格書，而衛星網路論文的常數繼承自三類為各異目的而寫、甚至對「Starlink 是什麼」看法各異的來源，這正是出處必須成為表中一欄並隨每項數值清楚列示的原因。

The first source is the regulatory record. A filing snapshot records the constellation geometry in one dated FCC document. Later modifications replace it, while contemporary papers preserve it in their simulators. 第一類來源是監管紀錄。每一份 FCC 文件固定了一個申報快照，也就是那份附日期文件中所陳述的星座幾何，之後的修正會取代它，而當年的論文則把它凍結進模擬器裡。

An evidence tag identifies a value’s source class. It tells readers where to check the claim. Peer-reviewed values point to a refereed paper’s section, table or figure. Regulatory values point to filings or standards, including SDA’s optical specification.

Preprint values point to a specific arXiv version with review status requiring confirmation. Company values point to operator statements. Textbook values point to established references such as Vallado. The tag identifies provenance.

Accuracy requires examination of the underlying claim and evidence. The tags let the two cases be told apart at a glance.

證據標籤是本教材附在數字上的標記，用來說明它屬於上述哪一類，因此讀者能用什麼方式查證。同儕審查的數字可以對照審查過的論文的某一節、表或圖來檢查，監管文件的數字可以對照申報或像 SDA 光學終端規格這類標準，預印本的數字對照的是仍待確認審查的 arXiv 版本，公司的數字只能對照營運商自己的說法，而教科書的數字則對照 Vallado 這類標準參考書。標籤標示出處，數值的準確度則須另行檢查原始主張及證據。SpaceX 的單雷射 100 Gbps 很可能是準確的，且仍待獨立驗證。標籤讓這兩種情況一眼就能分辨。

4.2The sheet參數表

The parameter sheet gives 2026 study inputs with their values, evidence tags and exact source locations. Rows follow evaluation structure: constellation geometry, ISLs, ground segment, scheduler and satellite bus. They end with Part III’s terrestrial references and Chapter 11’s Suncatcher cluster. Values from separate sources remain separate entries. A contested or unknown value needs an explicit assumption, such as "we assume 1 ms per hop, pending measurement." Readers can then assess that choice.

傳播延遲最後一列用參數表自己的數字重述了 Handley 的開創性論證：走完整 9,845 km，光纖需要 48 ms 而 ISL 鏈需要 39 ms，所以只有當目的地遠到傳播佔主導時軌道才會贏，而且只有在節點延遲很小時才成立。

4.5How to cite a satellite number in a paper如何在論文中引用一個衛星數字

The habits of Section 4.2 become a procedure when a number leaves the sheet and enters a manuscript. Reviewers ask five questions about each LEO constant. Answering them with the value makes its meaning and scope easier to assess. The fourth is the evidence tag, stated in the sentence rather than left to a reference list that readers rarely open. The fifth is the location in the source, down to the section, table, figure or paragraph number.
