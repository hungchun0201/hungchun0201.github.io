The instrument determines what a quantitative claim measures. Measurement observes the deployed scheduler, queues and PoP placement from attached hosts, while operator configuration and satellite internals remain outside its control. A packet simulator advances coded links, queues and protocols through simulated events, giving control and repeatability within the mechanisms its authors implement. An emulator runs existing operating systems, routing daemons and applications in real time over modeled virtual links. Its software is real, while constellation geometry, delay and capacity still come from a model.

工具決定量化論斷衡量的範圍。量測由連上已部署網路的主機觀察排程器、佇列與 PoP，組態仍由營運商控制，衛星內部也在觀測範圍之外。封包模擬器在模擬時間中推進程式化的鏈路、佇列與協定事件，提供可控制、可重現的實驗，範圍由作者實作的機制決定。模擬平台則在模型化的虛擬鏈路上即時執行原版作業系統、路由常駐程式與應用程式。受測軟體是真實的，星座幾何、延遲與容量仍來自模型。

Figure 7.1 maps the trade between fidelity and scale. Measurement reaches the locations of its vantage points and observes the operator’s chosen topology. Packet simulation supports whole constellations and hypothetical designs, with cost growing with processed packets and realism defined by its models. Emulation retains a real network stack but uses memory and CPU for each node. The largest published examples therefore need several servers for a shell or constellation.

圖 7.1 呈現逼真度與規模的取捨。量測的地理範圍由觀測點位置決定，觀察的是營運商選定的拓樸。封包模擬可研究整個星座與假想設計，成本隨封包事件數成長，逼真度則由模型界定。模擬平台保留真實網路堆疊，但每個節點都占用記憶體與 CPU，因此最大規模的已發表案例需要多台伺服器支撐殼層或星座。

Each method has an observation boundary. External probes measure RTT and can infer ISL use from delay, while satellite identities, laser hops and separate space–gateway queues require additional evidence. Simulation exposes only implemented mechanisms, which is why measurement first discovered the fifteen-second schedule. Emulation also abstracts the physical layer. StarryNet’s authors describe virtualization’s “limited ability to emulate physical layer (PHY) characteristics” [40, §8]. Figure 7.2 marks where observation gives way to modeling assumptions.

各種方法都有觀測邊界。外部探測可量測 RTT，並依延遲推論 ISL 使用情形，衛星身分、雷射跳數與太空、閘道的各佇列則需要額外證據。模擬只呈現已實作的機制，因此十五秒排程最先由量測發現。模擬平台也會抽象化實體層，StarryNet 作者稱虛擬化「模擬實體層（PHY）特性的能力有限」[40, §8]。圖 7.2 標出觀測轉為建模假設的環節。

TLE 與 SGP4 提供模擬背後的幾何，位置誤差與更新頻率也界定事件預測的時間精度。

Chapter summary
本章摘要

LEO evidence comes from measurement, packet simulation and emulation, each with a fidelity–scale trade. Dishes, LENS, RIPE Atlas, HitchHiking and IPv6 scanning reveal deployed scheduling, queues and PoP behavior within their observation boundaries. Hypatia explains RTT variation, route changes and reordering through an idealized +Grid, with high-rate runtime limiting experiments. StarryNet provides containerized digital twins, Celestial supports low-cost edge services, xeoverse offers real-time static routes, and LeoEM models one scheduled path.

Their laser rate, topology and delay assumptions define each result’s scope. Model agreement supports calibration, while direct deployed-network evidence supports validation. Panel 7.1 shows why omissions can preserve medians yet shift tails by tens of milliseconds.

LEO 證據來自量測、封包模擬與模擬平台，各有逼真度與規模的取捨。天線、LENS、RIPE Atlas、HitchHiking 與 IPv6 掃描在各自範圍內揭露排程、佇列與 PoP 行為。Hypatia 透過理想化 +Grid 解釋 RTT、路徑與重排，高速率計算成本則限制實驗規模。StarryNet 提供容器數位孿生，Celestial 支援低成本邊緣服務，xeoverse 提供即時靜態路由，LeoEM 模擬單一排程路徑。雷射速率、拓樸與延遲假設界定各項結果，模型一致支持校準，部署證據則支持驗證。面板 7.1 顯示省略項可能保留中位數，卻讓尾端改變數十毫秒。
