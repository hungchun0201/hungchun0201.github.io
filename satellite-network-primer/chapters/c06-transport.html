A transport protocol observes acknowledgement timing and gaps, then uses them to infer network conditions. Rules designed for wired networks can misread a satellite network that reconfigures on a timer. Congestion control adjusts the sender’s congestion window, or cwnd, which limits outstanding bytes and bounds throughput by cwnd divided by RTT. Linux’s default CUBIC treats loss as queue overflow, reduces its window, and regrows it along a cubic curve.

That curve changes from concave to convex around the previous loss point. BBR estimates bottleneck bandwidth and minimum RTT, then paces to that model, treating moderate loss as noise. Both associate loss and delay with queues partly filled by the sender, an assumption that satellite reconfiguration can violate.

傳輸層從確認封包的時序與缺口推估網路狀態。有線網路的解讀規則遇到按時重配的衛星網路時，可能產生誤判。壅塞控制調整傳送端的壅塞視窗 cwnd，限制待確認位元組，使吞吐量上限等於 cwnd 除以 RTT。Linux 預設的 CUBIC 把遺失視為佇列溢位，先縮窗，再沿三次曲線回升，曲線以先前遺失的視窗大小為中心由凹轉凸。BBR 則估計瓶頸頻寬與最小 RTT，按模型調整送出節奏，把中等程度的遺失當作雜訊。兩者都把遺失、延遲連結到傳送端參與填滿的佇列，衛星重新配置卻可能破壞這個假設。

Chapter 3 describes Starlink’s fifteen-second global scheduling period, with boundaries at :12, :27, :42 and :57. At mid-latitudes, at least 89% of handovers fall within one second of a boundary ( Section 3.5 ).
第 3 章的重新配置週期是 Starlink 全域排程器的 15 秒週期，邊界落在每分鐘的第 12、27、42、57 秒，在中緯度地區，至少 89% 的天線換手落在邊界前後一秒之內（第 3.5 節）。

Reconfiguration-aware congestion control discounts loss and delay samples associated with scheduled satellite changes, so window reductions respond to congestion. The three designs differ in their source of event information. SaTCP receives predicted handovers from components at the terminal and ground station. The HotNets 2024 patches read dish diagnostics. LeoCC detects gaps in responses to a 10 ms ICMP probe from the user’s device to the PoP, making detection entirely end-host based.

感知重新配置的控制器會降低排程變化所產生之遺失與延遲樣本的權重，讓縮窗主要回應壅塞。三種設計各有事件來源：SaTCP 由終端與地面站元件預測換手並通知傳送端，HotNets 2024 修補讀取天線診斷，而 LeoCC 在使用者裝置上以每 10 ms 送往 PoP 的 ICMP 回應空隙偵測事件，完全由端點運作。

Cao and Zhang built SaTCP on CUBIC with handover predictions from public orbital elements. About 90% of prediction errors across 1,605 satellites fell within ±0.6 s [67, Fig. 4]. Around each prediction, the sender suspends reductions for \(2T_{err} + RTO_{min} + 0.1\,\text{s}\). Here \(T_{err}\) bounds prediction error, and \(RTO_{min}\) is the minimum retransmission timeout.

A timeout within that period also skips the usual reset to one packet [67, §V-C–V-D]. Outside it, SaTCP uses ordinary CUBIC behavior. Its gain therefore depends on the share of CUBIC losses caused by handovers. Figure 6.2 shows why the error band requires a freeze window wider than the outage.

Cao 與 Zhang 把 SaTCP 建在 CUBIC 之上，並以由公開軌道根數算出的換手預測驅動它，在 1,605 顆衛星上約有 90% 的預測誤差落在 ±0.6 s 以內[67, Fig. 4]。因此傳送端在預測時刻前後的一段抑制期 \(2T_{err} + RTO_{min} + 0.1\,\text{s}\) 內暫停一切視窗縮小，其中 \(T_{err}\) 是預測誤差的界限、\(RTO_{min}\) 是最小重傳逾時，而抑制期內發生的逾時會略過平常把視窗重設為一個封包的動作[67, §V-C–V-D]。在抑制期之外，SaTCP 的行為與 CUBIC 完全相同，這讓它原則上可以部署，也意味著它在任一路徑上的增益等於該路徑上 CUBIC 遺失中屬於換手遺失的比例。圖 6.2 畫出單一邊界附近的機制，以及迫使視窗必須比它要覆蓋的中斷更寬的誤差帶。

SaTCP suppresses window reductions around predicted handovers. Its gain is largest where handover loss strongly limits CUBIC, and smaller where the baseline already fills the modeled path.

SaTCP 在預測換手附近抑制縮窗。當換手遺失強烈限制 CUBIC 時增益最大，基線原已充分利用模型路徑時則較小。

Chapter summary
本章摘要

Starlink congestion control can misread path reordering, scheduled changes and drop-front queue behavior. In Hypatia, reordering halved NewReno’s window while every packet arrived. Reconfiguration bursts and capacity steps can leave CUBIC and BBR near half the UDP-measured capacity. The shared 1,400–1,600-packet drop-front queue gives 85 ms mean delay, against 122 ms for equal-size drop-tail.

Event-aware designs learn timing from the network, dish or endpoint. Their reported gains depend on the comparator and experiment.

Conditions and reported evidence
#Evidence record
1Event-aware designs learn timing from the network, dish [56], or end-host probes.
2SaTCP rises from 34.3 to 138.1 Mbps in LeoEM, while live-uplink LeoCC gains 85% over BBRv3 and 253% over CUBIC.
3An independent emulator comparison places SaTCP, BBRv3 and LeoCC near 90–94 against CUBIC’s 49.
4SaTCP can reduce a CUBIC neighbor to 8.4 Mbps, and LeoCC has the highest measured retransmission rate in that comparison.
5Their deployment evidence remains at research scale.

Zoom’s delay and error-correction costs rise, while Teams cuts roughly 0.8 Mbps at boundaries. Real-ISL transport needs direct measurement, especially for seconds-long acquisition events that challenge lossless training fabrics.
