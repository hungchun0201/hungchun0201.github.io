An inter-satellite link (ISL) directly connects two satellites by radio or laser. propagation delay equals distance divided by light speed in the medium. Satellite paths include ascent to the shell and descent to Earth. These extra slant ranges explain the roughly 3,000 km crossover. The per-kilometer advantage becomes useful once the great-circle distance offsets this extra travel.星間鏈路（ISL）是兩顆衛星之間直接的無線電或雷射連線，其基本時間成本是傳播延遲，也就是距離除以介質中的光速。3,000 km 這個門檻之所以存在，是因為衛星路徑得先爬到殼層再下來，比光纖路徑多出兩段斜距。因此大圓距離須足夠長，才能以每公里的速度優勢抵銷額外路程。

How long is one hop? The +Grid gives each satellite two links to fore-and-aft neighbors within its plane. Two further links reach same-index satellites in adjacent planes [3, §3] [6, §3.1]. 2.2The optical link budget decides the rate光學鏈路預算決定速率

Geometry fixes how long a bit takes to arrive. The link budget, the decibel accounting of transmit power, antenna gains and losses, fixes how many bits can arrive per second. The price is that a spot that small must be aimed to a few microradians. This is the subject of Section 2.4.幾何決定一個位元要多久才到達。鏈路預算，也就是以分貝記帳發射功率、天線增益與各項損耗，則決定每秒能到達多少位元。發散角只有數十微弧度，所以飛行 2,000 km 後到達的光斑直徑是數十公尺，對照無線電的數十公里。代價是這麼小的光斑必須瞄準到幾微弧度，這是第 2.4 節的主題。

Evaluation conditionsA free-space optical link follows Friis form: \(P_R = P_T\,G_T\,G_R\,(\lambda/4\pi d)^2\,L_{\text{other}}\). For diffraction-limited aperture \(D\), gain is \(G \approx (\pi D/\lambda)^2\). Full divergence angle is \(\theta \approx 1.22\lambda/D\) [36, p. 8, Eq. 1].
Aperture gainGain grows with the square of \(D/\lambda\). A 10 cm aperture at 1,550 nm gives 106 dB gain. A 0.3 m dish at 23 GHz gives 37 dB [computed below]. The divergence is tens of microradians, so the spot that arrives after 2,000 km is tens of meters across rather than tens of kilometers.
評估條件對自由空間光鏈路而言，預算的形式與任何無線電鏈路相同的 Friis 式 \(P_R = P_T\,G_T\,G_R\,(\lambda/4\pi d)^2\,L_{\text{other}}\)，其中口徑為 \(D\) 的繞射極限望遠鏡增益是 \(G \approx (\pi D/\lambda)^2\)，而光束離開它時的全發散角是 \(\theta \approx 1.22\lambda/D\)[36, p. 8, Eq. 1]。
評估條件讓雷射勝出的兩個因素就寫在這些公式裡：增益隨 \(D/\lambda\) 的平方成長，所以 1,550 nm 的 10 cm 口徑有 106 dB 增益，而 23 GHz 的 0.3 m 碟形天線只有 37 dB［下方計算］。

The +Grid is attractive because all four of its links are quasi-static. Two intra-plane links connect satellites with fixed relative positions. Two inter-plane links connect same-index neighbors whose separation varies between equatorial and high-latitude values. Once acquired, these links can last an orbit or longer.

This is exactly the regime the acquisition times of Section 2.4 allow. Whether the breathing is survivable depends on the inclination. In polar Walker Star patterns such as Iridium, same-index separation scales with cosine of latitude and reaches zero at the pole. Across the seam, neighbors move in opposite directions.

Node delayVacuum propagation costs 3.336 µs per kilometer, against 4.895 µs in fiber. The 47% advantage offsets extra path length beyond about 3,000 km. Papers assume 0, 1 or 10 ms node delay, pending public measurements.
Path length and hopsChord lengths depend on the filing. For 24 × 66, intra-plane and inter-plane hops are 659 km and 1,807 km. For 72 × 22, they are 1,969 km and 604 km. At 53°, inter-plane distance falls to 0.60 of its equatorial value. Maximum line-of-sight range is 5,016 km.
Evaluation conditionsAt 2,000 km, the far-field budget gives 106 dB per 10 cm aperture and 264 dB free-space loss. Transmitting 1 W delivers 1.58 µW. This supports 63 Gbps at 196 photons per bit and exceeds TBIRD’s measured threshold by 13 dB. Long-range lasers consequently operate around 1–200 Gbps. A 100–200 m near-field link can reach roughly 10 Tbps.
