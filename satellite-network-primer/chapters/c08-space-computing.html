  • Downlink bottleneck下行瓶頸 The limit set by pass minutes times station rate on how much a satellite can send to the ground per orbit. 由過頂分鐘數乘以地面站速率決定的每圈可下傳資料量上限。
  • Specific power比功率 Watts of array output per kilogram of array mass. 每公斤陣列質量所產生的瓦數。
  • Depth of discharge放電深度（DoD） The fraction of a battery's capacity drawn each cycle, traded against cycle life. 每個循環取用的電池容量比例，與循環壽命互為取捨。
  • Radiator輻射散熱板 The surface that sheds a satellite's waste heat as infrared, at a rate set by its temperature to the fourth power. 把衛星廢熱以紅外線輻射出去的表面，速率由溫度的四次方決定。

  • Single-event effect (SEE)單粒子效應（SEE） A fault caused by one particle, from a flipped bit to a destructive latch-up. 單一粒子造成的故障，從位元翻轉到破壞性閂鎖都算。
  • Single-event functional interrupt (SEFI)單粒子功能中斷（SEFI） A single-event fault that halts a device until it is reset or power-cycled. 使裝置停擺、直到重置或斷電重啟才恢復的單粒子故障。
  • Silent data corruption (SDC)靜默資料損毀（SDC）An incorrect computational result accompanied by a normal error status.產生錯誤計算結果，同時回報正常錯誤狀態。

Chapters 5–7 treat satellites as routers. This chapter examines them as computers through the progression from downlink limits to processor limits and measured thermal constraints. Orbital-edge work introduced filtering, Kodan addressed on-board processing, and flight measurements exposed heat limits in commodity hardware. Power, thermal, radiation and launch requirements then frame Suncatcher’s design and the record of flown systems.

第 5–7 章將衛星當作路由器，本章則從下行限制、處理器限制與實測熱控限制，檢視它作為電腦的條件。軌道邊緣研究引入資料過濾，Kodan 處理機上運算瓶頸，飛行量測則揭露商用硬體的散熱限制。電力、熱控、輻射與發射需求共同界定 Suncatcher 設計與已飛行系統的證據範圍。

8.4Radiation: TID, SEE, SEFI and SDC輻射：TID、SEE、SEFI 與 SDC

Recall
回顧
Section 8.2 places Suncatcher in a sunlit 650 km dawn–dusk orbit. Section 8.1 establishes that OEC’s Jetson TX2 results came from simulation. Both facts matter here, because altitude sets the dose and the Jetson is one of the few accelerators with a published proton test.
第 8.2 節為了日照把 Suncatcher 的叢集放在 650 km 的晨昏軌道，第 8.1 節則指出 OEC 對 Jetson TX2 的所有結果都來自模擬。這兩點在此都很重要，因為高度決定劑量，而 Jetson 是少數有公開質子測試結果的加速器之一。

Radiation produces cumulative degradation and individual particle faults. Total ionising dose, TID, measures deposited energy in rad(Si), progressively shifting transistor thresholds and increasing leakage. A single-event effect, SEE, comes from one particle striking a sensitive node, ranging from repairable memory flips to destructive latch-up. A single-event functional interrupt, SEFI, halts the device until reset or a power cycle.

Silent data corruption, SDC, returns an incorrect result with a normal error status. In training, that result can enter all-reduce and spread through every model replica. Device tests express event occurrence against dose, and an assumed orbital dose rate converts those results into event-rate estimates.

輻射造成累積退化與單一粒子故障。總游離劑量 TID 以 rad(Si) 衡量沉積能量，逐漸改變電晶體臨界電壓與漏電。單粒子效應 SEE 則來自一顆粒子擊中敏感節點，範圍從可修復記憶體翻轉到破壞性閂鎖。單粒子功能中斷 SEFI 使裝置停擺，直到重置或斷電重啟。靜默資料損毀 SDC 則回傳錯誤結果，錯誤狀態仍顯示正常，訓練時可能經 all-reduce 傳播至各模型副本。元件測試把事件發生與劑量相連，再依假設的軌道劑量率換算事件率。

Radiation margins depend on altitude, shielding and the tested device. The published Trillium versions report separate maximum test doses, so the selected source version matters.

輻射餘裕取決於高度、屏蔽與受測元件，Trillium 各版本報告的最高測試劑量各異，因此須標明採用版本。

Chapter summary
本章摘要

Orbital edge computing reduces the data sent through limited ground contacts, while Kodan addresses the resulting processor bottleneck through model specialization. Solar arrays and eclipse batteries determine power-system mass, with dawn–dusk orbits reducing storage demand. Radiator emission follows εσT⁴, and flight measurements show that the conduction path can constrain cooling first. Trillium’s shielded cumulative-dose margin shifts attention toward frequent silent corruption and memory errors. Suncatcher’s launched-power comparison depends on a projected launch-price decline, while independent studies estimate higher total orbital costs. Flown systems currently establish independent on-board computing, with shared-job crosslinks still awaiting demonstration.

軌道邊緣運算縮小有限地面通聯需承載的資料，Kodan 則以模型專門化處理後續的處理器瓶頸。太陽能陣列與地影電池決定電力系統質量，晨昏軌道降低儲能需求。散熱板遵循 εσT⁴，飛行量測也顯示傳導路徑可能先成為限制。Trillium 的屏蔽累積劑量餘裕，使設計焦點轉向頻繁的靜默損毀與記憶體錯誤。Suncatcher 的已發射電力比較依賴發射降價推估，獨立研究則估出更高總成本。飛行系統目前建立各自的機上運算，承載共同工作的交叉鏈路仍待示範。
