  • Low Earth orbit (LEO)低地球軌道（LEO） Orbits below about 2,000 km altitude, where broadband constellations fly. 高度約 2,000 km 以下的軌道，寬頻星座都在這裡飛行。
  • Orbital period軌道週期 Time for one revolution, fixed by altitude alone for a circular orbit. 繞行一圈所需的時間，圓軌道時只由高度決定。
  • Elevation angle仰角 Angle of the satellite above the user's local horizon. The mask is the minimum the operator allows. 衛星高於使用者地平線的角度。遮罩是營運商允許的最低值。
  • Slant range斜距 Straight-line distance from user to satellite, which sets the propagation delay. 使用者到衛星的直線距離，決定傳播延遲。
  • Pass過頂 The interval during which one satellite stays above the elevation mask. 一顆衛星停留在仰角遮罩之上的時間區間。
  • Orbital plane軌道面 The plane containing one orbit, located by its inclination and ascending node. 包含一條軌道的平面，由傾角與升交點定位。
  • Inclination傾角 Tilt of the orbital plane to the equator. It bounds the latitudes a satellite reaches. 軌道面相對赤道的傾斜角，決定衛星能到達的最高緯度。
  • Shell殼層 A group of planes sharing one altitude and inclination, filed and operated as a unit. 共享同一高度與傾角的一組軌道面，在申請與營運上視為一個單位。
  • Walker notationWalker 記法 i: t/p/f describes a symmetric constellation by inclination, total, planes and phasing. 以 i: t/p/f 描述對稱星座的傾角、總數、面數與相位。
  • Phase offset相位偏移 The along-track shift between satellites in neighboring planes, 360·f/t degrees. 相鄰軌道面衛星之間沿軌方向的錯位，等於 360·f/t 度。
  • Seam接縫 Where the first and last planes of a Star pattern meet moving in opposite directions. Star 型星座中第一與最後一個軌道面相遇、彼此反向運動的地方。
  • Nodal precession升交點進動 Slow drift of the ascending node caused by Earth's oblateness (J₂). 地球扁率（J₂）造成的升交點緩慢漂移。
  • Sun-synchronous orbit (SSO)太陽同步軌道（SSO） A retrograde orbit whose nodal precession matches the Sun's apparent motion. 升交點進動速率與太陽視運動相同的逆行軌道。
  • Eclipse地影 The part of each orbit spent in Earth's shadow. 每一圈軌道中位於地球陰影內的部分。

Ground-track motionThe sub-satellite point sweeps across the ground at \(v_g = v\,R_E/a\), which at 550 km is 6.983 km/s. 1.2Seeing a satellite: elevation mask, slant range, coverage and the pass看見一顆衛星：仰角遮罩、斜距、覆蓋與過頂

Recall
回顧
Section 1.1 gave the period and the mean motion \(n = 2\pi/T\). This section adds the minimum angle for terminal communication. Together, they determine visibility.
第 1.1 節給出了週期與平均運動 \(n = 2\pi/T\)。本節加上終端建立通訊所需的最低角度，再由這兩項條件推導可見範圍。

A user terminal measures a satellite by its elevation angle ε above the local horizon. Each operator sets a minimum elevation, called the mask, for link attempts. Near the horizon, signals cross more atmosphere, antenna gain falls and buildings or trees can block the path. The mask and the triangle between Earth's center, the user and the satellite determine three quantities [4, Ch. 2] [5, Ch. 11] .

The coverage circle’s Earth-central half-angle is \(\lambda(\varepsilon) = \arccos[(R_E/a)\cos\varepsilon] - \varepsilon\). slant range means the straight-line user–satellite distance: \(d(\varepsilon) = R_E[\sqrt{(a/R_E)^2 - \cos^2\varepsilon} - \sin\varepsilon]\). The satellite’s nadir angle is \(\eta(\varepsilon) = \arcsin[(R_E/a)\cos\varepsilon]\). These three angles sum to 90°. Delay depends on the serving satellite’s sky position.

A scheduler switching between zenith and 25° elevation can double propagation delay within one minute.

使用者終端用衛星高於當地地平線的仰角 ε 描述衛星的位置。營運商設定的最低通訊仰角稱為遮罩。接近地平線時，訊號穿越的大氣較多、天線增益下降，建築物與樹木也可能遮擋路徑。給定遮罩後，由地心、使用者與衛星構成的球面三角形可求得三個量[4, Ch. 2][5, Ch. 11]。覆蓋圓的地心半角是 \(\lambda(\varepsilon) = \arccos[(R_E/a)\cos\varepsilon] - \varepsilon\)，斜距也就是使用者到衛星的直線距離是 \(d(\varepsilon) = R_E[\sqrt{(a/R_E)^2 - \cos^2\varepsilon} - \sin\varepsilon]\)，衛星端的天底角則是 \(\eta(\varepsilon) = \arcsin[(R_E/a)\cos\varepsilon]\)，三個角相加為 90°。因此延遲取決於服務衛星恰好在天空中的哪個位置，當排程器把終端在天頂衛星與 25° 衛星之間切換時，傳播延遲會在一分鐘之內變動達兩倍。

1.3Shells, planes and Walker patterns殼層、軌道面與 Walker 星座

One satellite gives a few minutes of service per pass, so coverage comes from arrangement. An orbital plane contains a circular orbit. Inclination i measures its tilt from the equator. RAAN locates the ascending node, where the orbit crosses the equator northward. Inclination i bounds the latitudes a satellite reaches. Two families exist.一顆衛星每次過頂只能提供幾分鐘的服務，所以覆蓋來自於排列。軌道面是包含一條圓軌道的平面，由它的傾角 i（相對赤道的傾斜）以及升交點經度（RAAN，軌道向北穿越赤道的那一點）來定位。傾角 i 決定衛星可到達的緯度範圍。它分成兩個家族。

Plane configurationA shell groups planes with a shared altitude and inclination. It is the FCC authorization unit and the unit used in simulations. Walker notation i: t/p/f spreads t satellites evenly across p equally spaced planes. The integer f ranges from 0 to p−1 and sets a phase offset of 360·f/t degrees. This is the along-track shift between corresponding satellites in neighboring planes (Walker, J. Brit. Interplanetary Soc. 37, 1984. Ballard, IEEE Trans. AES 16(5), 1980 textbook).
Plane configurationWalker Delta spreads p ascending nodes across 360° of RAAN, keeping neighboring planes co-rotating. Starlink’s 53° and 43° shells, all Kuiper shells and their Hypatia models use this pattern. Hypatia describes satellites as "uniformly spaced out" within orbits, with orbits "uniformly spread across the Equator" [6, §3.1].
Plane configurationWalker Star spreads near-polar planes across about 180° of RAAN. Iridium, OneWeb Gen1, Telesat’s polar shell and Starlink’s 97.6° shell use this pattern. Satellites move northward on one half of Earth and southward on the other. The first and last planes meet at a seam where neighbors counter-rotate at twice orbital speed. Links across that seam last seconds, while other links can last an orbit.

Orbital altitudeVisibility is where altitude really bites. This occurs because the elevation mask fixes the coverage half-angle λ, the slant range and the longest pass together. At 550 km, a 25° mask gives λ = 8.45° and slant range 1,123 km. One-way delay is 3.75 ms, and maximum zenith pass is 4.49 min. For 4,408 satellites, a uniform-sphere model gives about 24 visible satellites. Tanveer et al. measured 35–44 at mid-latitudes.
