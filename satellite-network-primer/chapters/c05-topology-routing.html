Two quantities define this comparison. A path’s stretch is its latency or length divided by the great-circle value between the same endpoints. A stretch of 1.25 therefore means a path one quarter longer than the geodesic. Hop count records the inter-satellite links a packet crosses and commonly estimates the link capacity a flow consumes. LEO wins when the 47% speed advantage exceeds the grid detour and the ground legs to the first and last satellites. The crossover consequently depends on altitude, elevation mask and grid geometry.

伸長比與跳數是比較路徑的兩個量。伸長比等於路徑延遲或長度除以同一對端點的大圓值，1.25 代表比測地線多走四分之一。跳數記錄封包穿越的星間鏈路數，常用來估計一條流消耗的鏈路容量。當每公里 47% 的速度優勢足以補償網格繞路及地面到首尾衛星的上下行距離，LEO 路徑便能取得延遲優勢。交叉距離因此取決於高度、仰角遮罩與網格形狀。

Hop count estimates the link resources each flow consumes. For equal-rate links, bisection capacity is the sum of link rates across a cut that divides the network into two halves. Each crossing flow consumes capacity on the links it traverses. Under a fixed traffic matrix, aggregate throughput therefore scales approximately inversely with average hop count.

Halving the hops at equal link rate can roughly double delivered traffic. CoNEXT’s hop-focused multi-motif reduced hops 53% while adding 9% stretch [1, §2.4, §5.5]. Chapter 10 uses hop count and cut width to compare torus and Clos fabrics for orbital clusters.

跳數可估計每條流使用的鏈路資源。在等速率鏈路組成的圖中，對分容量等於跨越網路兩半之間割集的鏈路速率總和。每條流都會消耗所經鏈路的容量，因此在固定流量矩陣下，總吞吐量大致與平均跳數成反比。鏈路速率相同時，把跳數減半可讓交付流量約增為兩倍。CoNEXT 偏重跳數的多 motif 以增加 9% 伸長比換得 53% 跳數減少 [1, §2.4, §5.5]。第 10 章會以跳數與割集寬度比較軌道叢集的 torus 與 Clos 架構。

5.5Traffic engineering in 70 ms: SaTE70 ms 內的流量工程：SaTE

Shortest-path routing optimizes distance while leaving load outside its objective. Continental demand can therefore concentrate on a few inter-plane links. Traffic engineering allocates each source–destination demand across candidate paths, subject to link capacities, to satisfy as much demand as possible. Stable terrestrial WANs solve this linear program every few minutes. A constellation’s topology holding time ends whenever a ground link or ISL appears or disappears.

SaTE estimates an average holding time near 70 ms at Starlink scale, while Gurobi needs about 46 s for the corresponding problem [70, §2.3–2.4]. The graph changes roughly 650 times during that solve. SaTE therefore targets solutions fast enough for the current topology.

最短路徑路由以距離為目標，負載則在其目標範圍之外，因此大陸的長途需求可能集中於少數跨面鏈路。流量工程把各來源–目的地需求分配至候選路徑，在各鏈路容量限制內滿足盡可能多的需求。拓樸穩定的地面廣域網路可每隔幾分鐘求解一次線性規劃。星座的拓樸維持時間則在任何地面鏈路或 ISL 出現、消失時結束。SaTE 估計 Starlink 規模的平均維持時間約 70 ms，Gurobi 求解對應問題卻需約 46 s [70, §2.3–2.4]。求解期間圖已改變約 650 次，因此 SaTE 的目標是在當前拓樸仍適用時完成求解。

SaTE learns a traffic allocator that can finish within a short topology holding interval. Graph pruning and variable-size attention make the problem tractable, while experimental rate scaling defines its throughput scope.

SaTE 學習能在短拓樸維持區間內完成的流量配置器。圖修剪與可變規模注意力降低計算負擔，速率縮放則界定吞吐量範圍。

Chapter summary
本章摘要

Propagation simulations explain when orbital routes gain a distance advantage. Measured endpoints and newer topology results supply separate evidence.

Conditions and reported evidence
#Evidence record
1The “LEO beats fiber” claim comes from three propagation simulations published in 2018–2020.
2Their vacuum paths use +Grid stretch near 1.25 and compare against great-circle or stretched fiber.
3Altitude, elevation mask and fiber model move the crossover from about 1,000 to 10,000 km.
4Measured end-to-end delay has a roughly 40 ms floor shaped by PoPs and scheduling.
5Topology research progressed from motifs with a 54% gain on a four-terminal 24×66 filing to ground relays and stable links on real ephemerides.

The stable-link results cut hops 65%, more than double capacity, and reveal disconnected three-ISL grids. Earth-fixed routing cells reduce updates by two to three orders of magnitude. SaTE solves in 17 ms against a 70 ms holding time, while 264 daily maneuvers make repair time an operational metric. Section 5.7 records the filing, terminal and setup assumptions that accompany these results.
