A · The gap and the document that exposes itA · 缺口與揭露它的文獻

The reviewed literature still needs a collective simulation for a Tbps formation. Van Berkel assumes fixed-rate lasers and a static graph [101, Tab. 3, §IV-E]. Suncatcher's HCW geometry changes neighbor distances periodically [36, p. 4, Fig. 3], while its spatial-multiplexing rule makes aggregate bandwidth depend inversely on distance [36, p. 3, Fig. 1]. Together, these assumptions imply predictable rate variation over half-orbit cycles. Direction A asks whether a collective schedule can use that variation.

已檢閱文獻仍需補上 Tbps 編隊的集體通訊模擬。van Berkel 採固定雷射速率與靜態圖[101, Tab. 3, §IV-E]，Suncatcher 的 HCW 幾何則使鄰居距離週期變動[36, p. 4, Fig. 3]，空間多工法則又讓總頻寬與距離成反比[36, p. 3, Fig. 1]。這些假設共同產生可預測的半軌道週期速率變化，方向 A 因而檢驗集體通訊排程如何利用它。

A · The falsifiable questionA · 可檢驗的問題

Can advance knowledge of \(d_{ij}(t)\) keep exposed communication within ten percent of its best orbital phase? Compare with static rings and trees provisioned at the worst-case distance. Close the direction if a static schedule stays within ten percent of the orbit-aware result at every phase. If improvement requires repointing, charge the measured setup range [33, Tab. 1] and compare setup time with step time before simulation.

預知 \(d_{ij}(t)\) 的排程，可將暴露通訊維持在最佳軌道相位的百分之十範圍內嗎？比較對象是按最差距離配置的靜態環與樹。若靜態排程在每個相位都位於軌道感知結果的百分之十內，即達到停止條件。若改善依賴重新指向，則需計入量得的建立時間範圍[33, Tab. 1]，先比較建立與步驟時間，再進行模擬。

A · Metrics and baselinesA · 指標與基準

Report step time across a full orbit as its mean, peak-to-trough ratio and exposed fraction \(T_{\text{exposed}}/T_{\text{step}}\). Count repointings per orbit as a schedule cost. Compare a conservative ring provisioned at 200 m with an optimistic ring provisioned at 100 m. The conservative ring leaves closest-approach capacity idle, while the optimistic ring stalls when rates fall below its allocation. Require gains over both static baselines at every orbital phase.

以整圈軌道的步驟時間平均、峰谷比及暴露比例 \(T_{\text{exposed}}/T_{\text{step}}\) 作為指標，並以每圈重新指向次數計入成本。比較基準為按 200 m 配置的保守環，以及按 100 m 配置的積極環。保守環在最近距離留下閒置容量，積極環則在速率低於分配份額時停滯，因此需在每一個軌道相位都勝過這兩個靜態基準，控制基準選擇的影響。

A · What would falsify itA · 停止研究的條件

The rate-versus-range measurement is the first decision gate, using the proposed 100 m–2 km test interval. If the tested link stays effectively fixed across distance, use a static-fabric model. That trade needs steps well below a minute to matter. Confocal multiplexing estimates also exclude beam quality, truncation and jitter [36, p. 8].速率對距離的量測是第一個決策關卡。若鏈路在各距離維持近似固定速率，則改採靜態織網模型。

Chapter summary
本章摘要

The six directions connect specific source gaps to measurable tests. A examines collectives under predictable rate variation, B compares visibility-constrained fabrics, and C measures verification and recovery costs. D sizes ground transfer, E compares orbital and ground serving, and F supplies the flight-calibrated link model. Each direction has a baseline, a sensitivity and a stopping criterion, with detailed numerical assumptions collected in the tables.

六個方向將來源中的具體缺口連到可量測檢驗。A 研究可預測速率變化下的集體通訊，B 比較視線約束織網，C 評估驗證與恢復成本，D 設計地面傳輸規模，E 比較軌道與地面服務，F 則提供飛行校準鏈路模型。各方向均有基準、敏感度與停止條件，詳細數值假設集中列於表格。
