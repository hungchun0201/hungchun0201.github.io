Who this is for寫給誰

This primer assumes basic knowledge of packets and GPUs and introduces orbital mechanics and link budgets. It helps readers assess satellite network research and AI systems that span satellites. Its companion, the AI Training Network Primer, explains networks inside terrestrial training clusters. Part III uses that material and identifies the relevant sections.

本教材假設讀者了解封包與 GPU，並從基礎介紹軌道與鏈路預算，協助讀者評估衛星網路及跨衛星 AI 研究。姊妹篇AI 訓練網路入門介紹地面訓練叢集內部的網路，第三部會標明引用其中哪些內容。

The primer uses one reference numbering system. A superscript such as [1, §4, Tabs. 1–2] identifies a work and its exact source location. Hover over a marker, focus it with the keyboard, or tap it to see the full citation. Select its reference entry to open the global bibliography. Entries include publication links and local digests where available. Source claims and calculated values carry separate labels.

全書採用統一的文獻編號。上標引文，例如 [1, §4, Tabs. 1–2]，標明文獻編號及原文位置。滑鼠移入、鍵盤聚焦或觸控點選標記，即可查看完整書目。點選文獻項目可前往全書參考文獻，其中列出可取得的論文連結與導讀。公司宣稱、監管申報與自行計算的數值各有來源標記。

Where the numbers come from數字從哪裡來

Each number in Parts I and II cites its source paper, standard or filing in the sentence that uses it. Section, table or figure references let readers locate the original value. Peer-reviewed papers use the default format. Other sources carry the tags below. These tags distinguish company claims, measured results and preprints awaiting peer review. Part III examines proposed systems using Part I's physics and Part II's measurements, with its arithmetic labeled as calculated values.

第一、二部的每個數字都隨句標明來源論文、標準或申報文件，並列出節次、表格或圖號，方便查證。經同儕審查的論文採預設格式，其餘來源使用下列標記。這些標記區分公司宣稱、實測結果及等待同儕審查的預印本。資料僅來自公司聲明時，內文會明確歸因。第三部以第一部的物理原理與第二部的量測資料討論擬議系統，自行推導的數值標為「計算值」。

How the chapters connect各章的關係

Read Part I in order. Chapter 1 explains geometry, Chapter 2 calculates inter-satellite delay and link budgets, and Chapter 3 adds ground links and network measurements. Chapter 4 collects these results in a parameter sheet for later chapters. Part II allows any reading order after Chapter 4, though Chapter 7 explains the simulator capabilities needed to assess most results. Part III builds on Parts I and II and Chapters 3 to 8 of the AI Training Network Primer. Chapter 10 restates the required facts about parallelism, collectives, step budgets, topology, transport and routing.

第一部適合依序閱讀。第 1 章介紹幾何，第 2 章計算星間延遲與鏈路預算，第 3 章加入地面段及真實網路量測，第 4 章整理成供後續章節引用的參數表。讀完第 4 章後，第二部可依興趣選讀。細讀論文導讀前，建議先讀第 7 章，了解各模擬器的能力及適用範圍，因為本領域多數結果來自模擬。第三部以第一、二部及姊妹篇第 3 至 8 章為基礎，第 10 章會重述所需的平行化、集體通訊、步驟時間預算、拓樸、傳輸層與路由概念。

Companion primer and conventions姊妹篇與慣例

Distances use km, link rates use Gb/s (bits), storage uses GB (bytes), and latency uses ms. The text distinguishes one-way propagation delay from round-trip time. Starlink shell names follow FCC filings. Each digest identifies the constellation model behind its numbers and explains differences from deployment. Language choices and quiz answers stay on this device.

距離以 km、鏈路速率以 Gb/s（位元）、儲存量以 GB（位元組）、延遲以 ms 表示，全文區分單向傳播延遲與往返時間。Starlink 殼層名稱依 FCC 申報文件。導讀會指出數值所對應的星座模型，並說明它與實際部署的差異。語言選擇及測驗作答皆儲存在這台裝置上。
