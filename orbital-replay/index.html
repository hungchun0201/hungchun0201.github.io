Read the evidence in context.

Local formation positions come from declared simulations. Earth, nebula, starfield and luminous orbits are illustrative artwork, with enlarged glyphs and an exaggerated formation footprint. They are never actual satellite ephemeris, a live feed or a ground-contact prediction.

Optical link identities and rates are assumptions or exported transport values. Measured ground-access traces stay separate: they cannot locate a satellite or establish inter-satellite capacity.

Import orbital-replay/v1 JSON. Positions interpolate only between available samples; telemetry holds the latest exported sample. Missing values display “Not exported”. Repeated imports replace the current replay atomically; rejected files preserve it. Files remain in browser memory.
